# Numbers

## Meta
- Grade: 1
- Subject: Maths
- Theme tags: numbers
- Source basis: NCERT Class 1 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 1 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 1.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Practice Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- **stem**: Which number comes just after 5?
- A) 6
- B) 4
- C) 7
- D) 3
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: One more than 5 is 6.

### Q2
- **stem**: How many fingers do you have on one hand?
- A) 4
- B) 5
- C) 6
- D) 10
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: One hand has 5 fingers.

### Q3
- **stem**: Which number is bigger: 8 or 3?
- A) 3
- B) 1
- C) 8
- D) 0
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: 8 is more than 3.

### Q4
- **stem**: Count the stars: ★ ★ ★. How many?
- A) 2
- B) 4
- C) 5
- D) 3
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: There are 3 stars.

### Q5
- **stem**: Which number comes just before 10?
- A) 9
- B) 11
- C) 8
- D) 12
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Just before 10 is 9.

### Q6
- **stem**: What is the number name for 7?
- A) six
- B) seven
- C) eight
- D) five
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 7 is written as seven.

### Q7
- **stem**: Which set shows 4 apples?
- A) 🍎🍎🍎
- B) 🍎🍎
- C) 🍎🍎🍎🍎
- D) 🍎
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Four apples means 🍎🍎🍎🍎.

### Q8
- **stem**: Fill in: 2, 4, 6, __.
- A) 5
- B) 7
- C) 10
- D) 8
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: The pattern adds 2 each time. Next is 8.

### Q9
- **stem**: Which number is the smallest?
- A) 2
- B) 9
- C) 7
- D) 5
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 2 is the smallest of these.

### Q10
- **stem**: Riya has 1 ball. Amaira has 1 ball. How many balls in all?
- A) 1
- B) 2
- C) 3
- D) 0
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 1 + 1 = 2.

### Q11
- **stem**: Which digit is in 14?
- A) 2 and 4
- B) 1 and 5
- C) 1 and 4
- D) 4 and 0
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: 14 has digits 1 and 4.

### Q12
- **stem**: What comes next: 1, 2, 3, __?
- A) 5
- B) 0
- C) 6
- D) 4
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: After 3 comes 4.

### Q13
- **stem**: Kabir counts to 10. Which number did he say last?
- A) 10
- B) 9
- C) 8
- D) 11
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Counting to 10 ends on 10.

### Q14
- **stem**: Which shows more: 5 or 9?
- A) 5
- B) 9
- C) same
- D) 0
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 9 is more than 5.

### Q15
- **stem**: Zero means…
- A) ten
- B) one
- C) nothing
- D) many
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Zero means no things — nothing.

### Q16
- **stem**: Meera sees 6 birds. 1 flies away. How many are left?
- A) 6
- B) 7
- C) 4
- D) 5
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: 6 − 1 = 5.

### Q10

- B) 1

### Q11

- A) 🐱
- B) 🐱🐱🐱

- D) 🐱🐱🐱🐱

### Q12

### Q13

### Q15

- B) 50
- C) 5
- D) 1
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Adding zero keeps the number: 5.

### Q16
- **stem**: Order from small to big: 2, 9, 4. What is middle?
- A) 2
- B) 9
- C) 1
- D) 4
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Small to big: 2, 4, 9. Middle is 4.
