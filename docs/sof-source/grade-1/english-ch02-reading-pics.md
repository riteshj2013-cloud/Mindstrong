# Reading Pictures

## Meta
- Grade: 1
- Subject: English
- Theme tags: reading-pics
- Source basis: NCERT Class 1 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 1 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 1.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- stem: Picture: a smiling sun. What is it?
- A: sun
- B: moon
- C: star only
- D: cloud
- answer: A
- explanation: A smiling sun picture shows the sun.

### Q2
- stem: Read: "The cat is on the mat." Where is the cat?
- A: in a box
- B: on the mat
- C: under a car
- D: in water
- answer: B
- explanation: The sentence says on the mat.

### Q3
- stem: Who can fly?
- A: fish
- B: dog
- C: bird
- D: cow
- answer: C
- explanation: Birds can fly.

### Q4
- stem: Read: "I like mango." What does the child like?
- A: apple
- B: milk
- C: rice
- D: mango
- answer: D
- explanation: The word mango is in the sentence.

### Q5
- stem: At night we often see the…
- A: moon
- B: sun
- C: school bus
- D: rainbow
- answer: A
- explanation: At night we see the moon.

### Q6
- stem: Read: "Rani has a red bag." What colour is the bag?
- A: blue
- B: red
- C: green
- D: black
- answer: B
- explanation: The sentence says red bag.

### Q7
- stem: Which animal lives in water?
- A: cat
- B: hen
- C: fish
- D: goat
- answer: C
- explanation: Fish live in water.

### Q8
- stem: Picture clue: umbrella. When do we use it?
- A: to eat
- B: to sleep
- C: to swim
- D: in the rain
- answer: D
- explanation: We use an umbrella in the rain.

### Q9
- stem: Read: "Tom runs fast." What does Tom do?
- A: runs
- B: sleeps
- C: eats
- D: sits
- answer: A
- explanation: Tom runs — that is the action.

### Q10
- stem: We drink…
- A: stones
- B: water
- C: sand
- D: smoke
- answer: B
- explanation: We drink water.

### Q11
- stem: Read: "The hen lays an egg." Who lays the egg?
- A: cow
- B: dog
- C: hen
- D: cat
- answer: C
- explanation: The hen lays the egg.

### Q12
- stem: Which is a place to sleep?
- A: spoon
- B: ball
- C: pen
- D: bed
- answer: D
- explanation: We sleep on a bed.

### Q13
- stem: Picture: school bag. Where do you take it?
- A: to school
- B: to the moon
- C: into the sea
- D: under soil
- answer: A
- explanation: A school bag goes to school.

### Q14
- stem: Read: "Birds live in nests." Where do birds live?
- A: cars
- B: nests
- C: cups
- D: shoes
- answer: B
- explanation: The sentence says nests.

### Q15
- stem: We write with a…
- A: shoe
- B: plate
- C: pencil
- D: chair
- answer: C
- explanation: We write with a pencil.

### Q16
- stem: Read: "It is hot in summer." How is summer?
- A: cold
- B: dark always
- C: wet only
- D: hot
- answer: D
- explanation: The sentence says hot.
