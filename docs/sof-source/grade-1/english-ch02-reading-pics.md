# Reading Pictures

## Meta
- Grade: 1
- Subject: English
- Theme tags: reading-pics
- Source basis: NCERT Class 1 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 1 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 1.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- stem: Picture: a smiling sun. What is it?
- A: moon
- B: sun
- C: star only
- D: cloud
- answer: B
- explanation: A smiling sun picture shows the sun.

### Q2
- stem: Read: "The cat is on the mat." Where is the cat?
- A: in a box
- B: on the mat
- C: under a car
- D: in water
- answer: B
- explanation: The sentence says on the mat.

### Q3
- stem: Who can fly?
- A: fish
- B: bird
- C: dog
- D: cow
- answer: B
- explanation: Birds can fly.

### Q4
- stem: Read: "I like mango." What does the child like?
- A: apple
- B: mango
- C: milk
- D: rice
- answer: B
- explanation: The word mango is in the sentence.

### Q5
- stem: At night we often see the…
- A: sun
- B: moon
- C: school bus
- D: rainbow
- answer: B
- explanation: At night we see the moon.

### Q6
- stem: Read: "Rani has a red bag." What colour is the bag?
- A: blue

- D: black
- answer: B
- explanation: The sentence says red bag.

### Q7
- stem: Which animal lives in water?
- A: cat
- B: fish
- C: hen

- answer: B
- explanation: Fish live in water.

### Q8
- stem: Picture clue: umbrella. When do we use it?

### Q9
- stem: Read: "Tom runs fast." What does Tom do?

- B: runs
- C: eats

- explanation: Tom runs — that is the action.

### Q10
- stem: We drink…

### Q11

### Q12

### Q13

### Q15

### Q16
- stem: Read: "It is hot in summer." How is summer?
