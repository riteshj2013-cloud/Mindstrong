# Plants

## Meta
- Grade: 1
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: plants
- Source basis: NCERT Class 1 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 1 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 1.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Quiz Set A — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** Which part of a plant grows under the soil?

**Options:**
- A) leaf
- B) root
- C) flower
- D) fruit

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Roots grow under the soil.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** Leaves are mostly which colour?

**Options:**
- A) blue
- B) green
- C) pink
- D) black

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Most leaves are green.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** A seed can grow into a…

**Options:**
- A) stone
- B) new plant
- C) cloud
- D) car

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A seed grows into a new plant.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Which part makes food for the plant?

**Options:**
- A) root
- B) leaf
- C) stone
- D) pot

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Leaves make food with sunlight.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** We water plants because they need…

**Options:**
- A) music
- B) water
- C) toys
- D) shoes

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Plants need water to live.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** A flower is often…

**Options:**
- A) under soil only
- B) bright and pretty
- C) made of metal
- D) a rock

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Flowers are often bright and pretty.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Which do plants need to grow?

**Options:**
- A) sunlight
- B) TV
- C) phones
- D) cars

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Plants need sunlight.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** A mango grows on a…

**Options:**
- A) fish
- B) tree
- C) cloud
- D) bike

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Mangoes grow on trees.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Roots help the plant to…

**Options:**
- A) fly
- B) hold in the soil
- C) sing
- D) read

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Roots hold the plant in the soil.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Which is a plant?

**Options:**
- A) dog
- B) rose
- C) cup
- D) ball

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A rose is a plant.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** Fruits often have ___ inside.

**Options:**
- A) seeds
- B) wheels
- C) books
- D) socks

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Fruits keep seeds inside.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** The stem…

**Options:**
- A) holds the plant up
- B) is always under soil
- C) is an animal
- D) makes thunder

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The stem holds the plant up.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** A cactus lives where it is…

**Options:**
- A) very wet always
- B) dry
- C) under the sea
- D) in snow only

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Cactus plants like dry places.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** We get wood from…

**Options:**
- A) trees
- B) fish
- C) clouds
- D) stones only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Wood comes from trees.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** Which is NOT a plant part?

**Options:**
- A) root
- B) leaf
- C) wheel
- D) stem

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** A wheel is not a plant part.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Plants are…

**Options:**
- A) living
- B) not living
- C) made of plastic only
- D) always toys

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Plants are living things.


## Quiz Set B — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** Which part drinks water from the soil?

**Options:**
- A) flower
- B) root
- C) fruit skin
- D) thorn only

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Roots drink water from the soil.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** Sunlight helps leaves to…

**Options:**
- A) make food
- B) dance
- C) sleep only
- D) make noise

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Leaves use sunlight to make food.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** A baby plant is called a…

**Options:**
- A) seedling
- B) rocket
- C) pillow
- D) truck

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A baby plant is a seedling.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Which grows from a seed?

**Options:**
- A) chair
- B) plant
- C) glass
- D) phone

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** A plant grows from a seed.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** We should ___ plants.

**Options:**
- A) care for
- B) kick
- C) burn always
- D) ignore forever

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** We should care for plants.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** A leaf is usually…

**Options:**
- A) flat and green
- B) round like a ball only
- C) made of iron
- D) blue metal

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Leaves are often flat and green.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Bees visit flowers for…

**Options:**
- A) nectar
- B) stones
- C) books
- D) shoes

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Bees take nectar from flowers.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** A coconut tree is…

**Options:**
- A) tall
- B) a fish
- C) a bird
- D) a cup

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Coconut trees are tall.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Soil helps plants by giving…

**Options:**
- A) TV shows
- B) a place to grow
- C) music
- D) cars

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Soil is where plants grow.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Which is a flower?

**Options:**
- A) lotus
- B) spoon
- C) eraser
- D) sock

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A lotus is a flower.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** Dry leaves fall in…

**Options:**
- A) autumn / dry season
- B) only at night always
- C) from the moon
- D) from cars

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Leaves fall in dry or autumn times.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** The green colour in leaves helps them…

**Options:**
- A) make food
- B) bark like dogs
- C) fly planes
- D) cook rice

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Green helps leaves make food.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** A pot plant still needs…

**Options:**
- A) water
- B) no care
- C) only darkness forever
- D) salt only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Potted plants need water too.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** Which comes first?

**Options:**
- A) seed
- B) big tree
- C) fruit only
- D) wood chair

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Life often starts with a seed.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** Thorns on a rose…

**Options:**
- A) can prick
- B) are sweets
- C) are roots
- D) are fruits

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Thorns can prick — be careful.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Plants give us…

**Options:**
- A) oxygen / fresh air
- B) only plastic
- C) only noise
- D) only smoke

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Plants help give fresh air.
