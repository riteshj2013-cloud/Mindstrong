# My Body

## Meta
- Grade: 1
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: my-body
- Source basis: NCERT Class 1 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 1 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 1.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Quiz Set A — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** We see with our…

**Options:**
- A) eyes
- B) ears
- C) nose
- D) tongue

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** We see with our eyes.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** We hear with our…

**Options:**
- A) eyes
- B) ears
- C) hands
- D) feet

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** We hear with our ears.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** We smell with our…

**Options:**
- A) eyes
- B) ears
- C) nose
- D) hair

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** We smell with our nose.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** We taste with our…

**Options:**
- A) elbow
- B) knee
- C) ear
- D) tongue

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** We taste with our tongue.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** We have how many hands?

**Options:**
- A) 2
- B) 1
- C) 3
- D) 4

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** We have 2 hands.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** We walk with our…

**Options:**
- A) ears
- B) feet
- C) nose
- D) hair

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** We walk with our feet.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Brush your ___ every day.

**Options:**
- A) shoes only
- B) books
- C) teeth
- D) walls

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Brush your teeth every day.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** We breathe air in through our…

**Options:**
- A) toes
- B) elbows
- C) hair
- D) nose / mouth

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** We breathe through nose or mouth.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Wash your hands ___ eating.

**Options:**
- A) before
- B) never
- C) only once a year
- D) with paint

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Wash hands before eating.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Our heart is inside our…

**Options:**
- A) shoe
- B) chest
- C) hat
- D) bag

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** The heart is in the chest.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** We have how many eyes?

**Options:**
- A) 1
- B) 3
- C) 2
- D) 4

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Most people have 2 eyes.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** Skin helps us to…

**Options:**
- A) fly
- B) bark
- C) lay eggs
- D) feel touch

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Skin helps us feel touch.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** Exercise makes our body…

**Options:**
- A) stronger
- B) weaker always
- C) made of stone
- D) invisible

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Exercise helps us stay strong.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** We should sleep at…

**Options:**
- A) never
- B) night / rest time
- C) only in class always
- D) in the rain without care

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Sleep helps the body rest.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** Fingers are on our…

**Options:**
- A) ears
- B) nose tip only
- C) hands
- D) knees only

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Fingers are on our hands.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Which helps us chew food?

**Options:**
- A) hair
- B) nails only
- C) eyelashes
- D) teeth

**Answer:** D

**Explanation:** Teeth help us chew food.
