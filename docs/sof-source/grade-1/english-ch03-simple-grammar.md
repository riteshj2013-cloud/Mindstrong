# Simple Grammar

## Meta
- Grade: 1
- Subject: English
- Theme tags: simple-grammar
- Source basis: NCERT Class 1 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 1 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 1.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- stem: Pick the correct sentence.
- A: i like tea.
- B: I like tea.
- C: i Like tea.
- D: I like Tea always wrong.
- answer: B
- explanation: Sentences start with a capital letter.

### Q2
- stem: We say ___ apple.
- A: a
- B: an
- C: the the
- D: two
- answer: B
- explanation: Apple starts with a vowel sound — use an.

### Q3
- stem: The boys ___ playing.
- A: is
- B: are
- C: am
- D: be
- answer: B
- explanation: Boys is more than one — use are.

### Q4
- stem: She ___ my sister.
- A: am
- B: is
- C: are
- D: be
- answer: B
- explanation: She takes is.

### Q5
- stem: I ___ a student.
- A: is
- B: are
- C: am
- D: be
- answer: C
- explanation: With I we use am.

### Q6
- stem: Pick the naming word (noun).
- A: run
- B: happy
- C: school
- D: quickly
- answer: C
- explanation: School names a place — a noun.

### Q7
- stem: Pick the doing word (verb).
- A: red
- B: jump
- C: soft
- D: tall
- answer: B
- explanation: Jump is an action — a verb.

### Q8
- stem: Which needs a capital letter?
- A: monday
- B: tree
- C: cup
- D: sand
- answer: A
- explanation: Day names start with a capital: Monday.

### Q9
- stem: A cat ___ soft.
- A: am
- B: is
- C: are
- D: be
- answer: B
- explanation: One cat — use is.

### Q10
- stem: We say ___ umbrella.
- A: a
- B: an
- C: two two
- D: an an
- answer: B
- explanation: Umbrella starts with a vowel sound — an.

### Q11
- stem: They ___ happy.
- A: is
- B: am
- C: are
- D: be
- answer: C
- explanation: They is plural — use are.

### Q12
- stem: Pick the full stop sentence.
- A: Where is Bo
- B: Bo is brave.
- C: Wow
- D: Oh
- answer: B
- explanation: A telling sentence ends with a full stop.

### Q13
- stem: Which is a question?
- A: I sit.
- B: Are you ready?
- C: The sun is hot.
- D: Red bag.
- answer: B
- explanation: Questions often start with Are/Is/What and end with ?

### Q14
- stem: My name ___ Kabir.
- A: am
- B: is
- C: are
- D: be
- answer: B
- explanation: Name is singular — is.

### Q15
- stem: We use ___ before ball.
- A: a
- B: an
- C: an an
- D: the the the
- answer: A
- explanation: Ball starts with a consonant sound — a.

### Q16
- stem: Pick the opposite of big.
- A: large
- B: huge
- C: small
- D: tall
- answer: C
- explanation: Small is the opposite of big.
