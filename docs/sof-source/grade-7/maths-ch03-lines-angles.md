## Practice Set B

### Q01
- **stem**: If ∠A and ∠B are vertically opposite and ∠A = 2x + 10, ∠B = 3x − 20, then x = ?
- **options**:
  - A) 30
  - B) 10
  - C) 20
  - D) 40
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 2x + 10 = 3x − 20 → 30 = x.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q02
- **stem**: An angle is 20° more than its complement. The angle is?
- **options**:
  - A) 55°
  - B) 35°
  - C) 70°
  - D) 110°
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: x + (x − 20) = 90 → 2x = 110 → x = 55.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q03
- **stem**: An angle is equal to its supplement. The angle is?
- **options**:
  - A) 90°
  - B) 45°
  - C) 180°
  - D) 60°
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: x + x = 180 → x = 90.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q04
- **stem**: In △ABC, ∠A = 50°, ∠B = 60°. Exterior at C is?
- **options**:
  - A) 110°
  - B) 70°
  - C) 130°
  - D) 80°
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Interior at C is 70°; exterior = 180 − 70 = 110, also = A + B.
- **difficulty**: mixed
