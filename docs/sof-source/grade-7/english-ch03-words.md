# Grade 7 English — Word Power

- grade: 7

## Interactive lesson outline
- L1: Synonyms share meaning; antonyms oppose.
- L2: Idioms are figurative.
- L3: Prefixes and suffixes reshape words.
- L4: Context chooses the best word.
