# Plants

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: plants
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Quiz Set A — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** Seeds need ___ to sprout.

**Options:**
- A) water and warmth
- B) only darkness forever
- C) plastic
- D) metal

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Seeds need water and warmth to sprout.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** Leaves make food using…

**Options:**
- A) sunlight
- B) moonlight only
- C) noise
- D) plastic

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Leaves use sunlight to make food.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** Roots grow mostly…

**Options:**
- A) above the soil
- B) under the soil
- C) in the sky
- D) in shoes

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Roots grow under the soil.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Which plant part becomes a fruit?

**Options:**
- A) flower
- B) root hair only
- C) thorn only
- D) dead leaf

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A flower can become a fruit.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** We eat the root of a…

**Options:**
- A) carrot
- B) mango
- C) rose petal only
- D) banana skin only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Carrot is a root we eat.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** The stem carries ___ up to the leaves.

**Options:**
- A) water
- B) stones
- C) toys
- D) books

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The stem carries water up.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Plants take in carbon dioxide and give out…

**Options:**
- A) oxygen
- B) plastic
- C) sand
- D) smoke only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Plants give out oxygen.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** A climber plant needs…

**Options:**
- A) support
- B) wheels
- C) batteries
- D) screens

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Climbers need support to grow up.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Which is a cereal plant?

**Options:**
- A) wheat
- B) rose
- C) neem only
- D) cactus fruit only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Wheat is a cereal plant.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Dry seeds stored in a jar…

**Options:**
- A) may stay dormant
- B) always sprout at once
- C) turn into fish
- D) become metal

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Dry seeds can stay dormant until watered.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** Chlorophyll makes leaves look…

**Options:**
- A) green
- B) blue metal
- C) pink always
- D) glass

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Chlorophyll is green.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** Which helps scatter seeds?

**Options:**
- A) wind and animals
- B) only silence
- C) only plastic bags
- D) only darkness

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Wind and animals can scatter seeds.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** A potato grows underground but is a…

**Options:**
- A) stem
- B) flower
- C) leaf only
- D) fruit only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Potato is an underground stem.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** Plants in water (like lotus) are…

**Options:**
- A) aquatic plants
- B) desert only
- C) space plants
- D) metal plants

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Lotus is an aquatic plant.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** We should not ___ plants without care.

**Options:**
- A) pluck or harm
- B) water
- C) give sunlight
- D) protect

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Do not harm plants carelessly.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Photosynthesis mainly happens in the…

**Options:**
- A) leaf
- B) rock
- C) plastic pot only
- D) wire

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Photosynthesis happens in the leaf.


## Quiz Set B — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** A seedling is a…

**Options:**
- A) young plant
- B) old tree only
- C) dead leaf
- D) stone

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A seedling is a young plant.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** Flowers attract insects with…

**Options:**
- A) colour and smell
- B) noise machines
- C) plastic toys
- D) metal

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Colour and smell attract insects.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** Which part holds the plant upright?

**Options:**
- A) stem
- B) petal only
- C) seed coat only
- D) nectar

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The stem holds the plant up.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Mango seed is found…

**Options:**
- A) inside the fruit
- B) in the leaf tip only
- C) in the air only
- D) in a stone always

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** The seed is inside the mango fruit.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** We eat the leaf of…

**Options:**
- A) spinach
- B) carrot root only
- C) potato only
- D) coconut shell only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Spinach leaves are eaten.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** Plants need air, water and…

**Options:**
- A) sunlight
- B) TV
- C) phones
- D) petrol

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Sunlight is needed with air and water.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** A cactus stores water in its…

**Options:**
- A) stem
- B) flower only
- C) roots only always
- D) seeds only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Cactus stems store water.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** Which is NOT a plant need?

**Options:**
- A) video games
- B) water
- C) light
- D) air

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Video games are not a plant need.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Trees help us by giving…

**Options:**
- A) shade and air
- B) only noise
- C) only smoke
- D) only plastic

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Trees give shade and help the air.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Germination means a seed…

**Options:**
- A) starts to grow
- B) turns to metal
- C) flies to space
- D) becomes a fish

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Germination is when a seed starts to grow.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** The green food made by leaves travels through the…

**Options:**
- A) stem
- B) only the soil forever
- C) only the sky
- D) only wires

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Food moves through the stem.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** Which plant grows in water?

**Options:**
- A) lotus
- B) cactus
- C) desert thorn only
- D) pine on dry rock only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Lotus grows in water.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** Farmers grow plants for…

**Options:**
- A) food
- B) only noise
- C) only dust
- D) only plastic

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Farmers grow plants for food.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** Fallen leaves can become…

**Options:**
- A) compost over time
- B) glass
- C) metal
- D) plastic

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Leaves can break down into compost.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** A tendril helps a plant to…

**Options:**
- A) climb
- B) swim
- C) bark
- D) fly planes

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Tendrils help plants climb.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Without sunlight for long, a green plant may…

**Options:**
- A) become weak
- B) turn into a car
- C) start singing
- D) become a fish

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** No light → plant becomes weak.
