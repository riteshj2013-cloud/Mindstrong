# Time & Money

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: Maths
- Theme tags: time-money
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Practice Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- **stem**: How many minutes are in 1 hour?
- A) 30
- B) 60
- C) 100
- D) 24
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 1 hour = 60 minutes.

### Q2
- **stem**: A clock shows 3:00. The hour hand points to…
- A) 12
- B) 3
- C) 6
- D) 9
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: At 3:00 the hour hand is on 3.

### Q3
- **stem**: ₹10 + ₹5 = ?
- A) ₹10
- B) ₹15
- C) ₹20
- D) ₹5
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 10 + 5 = ₹15.

### Q4
- **stem**: There are ___ days in a week.
- A) 5
- B) 6
- C) 7
- D) 8
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A week has 7 days.

### Q5
- **stem**: Morning comes ___ night.
- A) after
- B) before the sun sets
- C) never
- D) only in winter
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Morning comes after night.

### Q6
- **stem**: A ₹1 coin and a ₹2 coin make…
- A) ₹2
- B) ₹3
- C) ₹4
- D) ₹1
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 1 + 2 = ₹3.

### Q7
- **stem**: Half past 2 is written as…
- A) 2:00
- B) 2:30
- C) 2:15
- D) 3:00
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Half past 2 = 2:30.

### Q8
- **stem**: Which is more money: ₹20 or ₹12?
- A) ₹12
- B) ₹20
- C) same
- D) ₹0
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: ₹20 is more than ₹12.

### Q9
- **stem**: How many hours in a day?
- A) 12
- B) 24
- C) 60
- D) 7
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A day has 24 hours.

### Q10
- **stem**: A notebook costs ₹10. You give ₹20. Change?
- A) ₹5
- B) ₹10
- C) ₹20
- D) ₹0
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 20 − 10 = ₹10 change.

### Q11
- **stem**: The short hand on a clock is the…
- A) minute hand
- B) hour hand
- C) second only always
- D) date
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The short hand shows the hour.

### Q12
- **stem**: ₹5 × 2 = ?
- A) ₹5
- B) ₹10
- C) ₹15
- D) ₹7
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Two ₹5 make ₹10.

### Q13
- **stem**: School often starts in the…
- A) morning
- B) midnight only
- C) only at 3 a.m.
- D) never
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: School often starts in the morning.

### Q14
- **stem**: Which coin is worth more: ₹1 or ₹5?
- A) ₹1
- B) ₹5
- C) same
- D) neither
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: ₹5 is worth more than ₹1.

### Q15
- **stem**: Quarter past 4 is…
- A) 4:15
- B) 4:30
- C) 4:45
- D) 4:00
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Quarter past = 15 minutes past → 4:15.

### Q16
- **stem**: You buy a pencil for ₹7 with a ₹10 note. Change?
- A) ₹2
- B) ₹3
- C) ₹7
- D) ₹10
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 10 − 7 = ₹3.

### Q13
