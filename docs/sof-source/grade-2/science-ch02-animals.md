# Animals

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: animals
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Quiz Set A — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** Animals that eat only plants are…

**Options:**
- A) herbivores
- B) carnivores
- C) machines
- D) rocks

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Herbivores eat plants.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** Animals that eat other animals are…

**Options:**
- A) herbivores
- B) carnivores
- C) plants
- D) stones

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Carnivores eat other animals.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** Birds have ___ to fly.

**Options:**
- A) wings
- B) fins only
- C) roots
- D) wheels

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Birds use wings to fly.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Fish breathe with…

**Options:**
- A) lungs like us always
- B) gills
- C) leaves
- D) noses only

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Fish breathe with gills.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** A cow is a…

**Options:**
- A) herbivore
- B) carnivore
- C) bird
- D) insect

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Cows eat grass — herbivores.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** Which animal may hibernate in cold?

**Options:**
- A) bear
- B) fish in fire
- C) eagle always
- D) ant always

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Some bears hibernate in cold weather.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Insects usually have ___ legs.

**Options:**
- A) 4
- B) 6
- C) 8
- D) 2

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Insects have 6 legs.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** A frog’s young one is a…

**Options:**
- A) tadpole
- B) puppy
- C) kitten
- D) cub

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A young frog is a tadpole.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Animals need food, water and…

**Options:**
- A) air and shelter
- B) only screens
- C) only plastic
- D) only noise

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Animals need air and shelter too.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Which is an omnivore?

**Options:**
- A) human
- B) cow only
- C) tiger only
- D) deer only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Humans can eat plants and animals.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** Camels store fat in their…

**Options:**
- A) humps
- B) fins
- C) wings
- D) beaks

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Camels have humps with fat.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** Which animal gives wool?

**Options:**
- A) sheep
- B) fish
- C) frog
- D) eagle

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Sheep give wool.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** Nocturnal animals are active at…

**Options:**
- A) night
- B) only noon
- C) only in class
- D) never

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Nocturnal means active at night.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** A nest is a home for…

**Options:**
- A) birds
- B) fish only always
- C) worms in space
- D) cars

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Birds live in nests.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** Endangered animals need…

**Options:**
- A) protection
- B) more hunting
- C) less forests forever
- D) noise only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Endangered animals need protection.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Which has a backbone?

**Options:**
- A) dog
- B) jellyfish
- C) worm
- D) ant

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A dog has a backbone.


## Quiz Set B — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** Deer eat plants, so they are…

**Options:**
- A) herbivores
- B) carnivores
- C) cars
- D) rocks

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Deer are herbivores.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** A tiger is a…

**Options:**
- A) herbivore
- B) carnivore
- C) plant
- D) insect only

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Tigers eat meat — carnivores.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** Ducks have ___ feet for swimming.

**Options:**
- A) webbed
- B) wheels
- C) roots
- D) claws only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Webbed feet help ducks swim.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Whales are…

**Options:**
- A) mammals that live in water
- B) fish with scales only
- C) insects
- D) birds

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Whales are water mammals.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** Honey is made by…

**Options:**
- A) bees
- B) cows
- C) sheep
- D) hens

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Bees make honey.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** Migration means animals…

**Options:**
- A) travel with seasons
- B) never move
- C) turn to stone
- D) become plants

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Migration is seasonal travel.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Spiders have ___ legs.

**Options:**
- A) 6
- B) 8
- C) 4
- D) 2

**Answer:** B

**Explanation:** Spiders have 8 legs.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** A caterpillar can become a…

**Options:**
- A) butterfly
- B) frog
- C) fish
- D) bird nest

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Caterpillar becomes a butterfly.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Pets need regular…

**Options:**
- A) food and care
- B) neglect
- C) only screens
- D) only noise

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Pets need food and care.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Which animal lives in a burrow?

**Options:**
- A) rabbit
- B) eagle only
- C) whale
- D) shark

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Rabbits may live in burrows.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** Elephants use their trunks to…

**Options:**
- A) pick food and drink
- B) fly
- C) lay eggs
- D) make honey

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Trunks help pick food and drink.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** Silk comes from…

**Options:**
- A) silkworm
- B) cow
- C) goat
- D) hen

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Silkworms give silk.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** Animals that are active by day are…

**Options:**
- A) diurnal
- B) nocturnal only
- C) made of metal
- D) plants

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Diurnal means active by day.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** A hive is a home for…

**Options:**
- A) bees
- B) lions
- C) whales
- D) cows

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Bees live in a hive.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** We should not ___ wild animals.

**Options:**
- A) hurt or tease
- B) respect
- C) learn about carefully
- D) protect

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Do not hurt wild animals.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Which animal lays eggs?

**Options:**
- A) hen
- B) dog
- C) cat
- D) cow

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Hens lay eggs.
