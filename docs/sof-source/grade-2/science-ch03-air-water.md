# Air & Water

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: air-water
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Quiz Set A — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** We need ___ to breathe.

**Options:**
- A) air
- B) stones
- C) plastic
- D) noise

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** We breathe air.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** Moving air is called…

**Options:**
- A) wind
- B) soil
- C) fire
- D) metal

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Moving air is wind.

**Q3.**

**Stem:** Water can be liquid, solid (ice) or…

**Options:**
- A) gas or vapour
- B) metal
- C) plastic
- D) wood

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Water can become vapour (gas).

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Ice is water in the ___ form.

**Options:**
- A) solid
- B) liquid only
- C) gas only
- D) plastic

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Ice is solid water.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** We should drink ___ water.

**Options:**
- A) clean
- B) dirty
- C) salty sea only
- D) muddy

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Drink clean water.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** Rain comes from…

**Options:**
- A) clouds
- B) stones
- C) plastic bags
- D) shoes

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Rain falls from clouds.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Boiling water can turn into…

**Options:**
- A) steam or vapour
- B) sand
- C) glass
- D) iron

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Boiling makes steam.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** Air is all around us but we usually…

**Options:**
- A) cannot see it
- B) can eat it like bread
- C) paint it only
- D) hold it easily always

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Air is invisible.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Saving water means…

**Options:**
- A) not wasting it
- B) leaving taps open
- C) polluting rivers
- D) throwing bottles in drains

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Saving water means not wasting it.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** A breeze is…

**Options:**
- A) gentle wind
- B) a rock
- C) a fire
- D) a fruit

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A breeze is a gentle wind.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** Fish need ___ in water to live.

**Options:**
- A) oxygen in the water
- B) plastic
- C) sand only
- D) fire

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Fish need oxygen in water.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** Puddles dry up because water…

**Options:**
- A) evaporates
- B) turns to stone
- C) freezes always
- D) becomes metal

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Water evaporates into air.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** Which is a use of water?

**Options:**
- A) drinking and cleaning
- B) only flying planes
- C) only making noise
- D) only making plastic

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** We drink and clean with water.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** Smoke and dust can make air…

**Options:**
- A) dirty or polluted
- B) sweeter always
- C) made of gold
- D) silent forever

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Smoke and dust pollute air.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** Clouds are made of tiny…

**Options:**
- A) water droplets
- B) stones
- C) plastic bits only
- D) metal sheets

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Clouds hold tiny water droplets.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Cover food and water to keep them…

**Options:**
- A) clean and safe
- B) dirty
- C) hot always
- D) salty always

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Covering keeps food and water safe.


## Quiz Set B — 16 MCQs

**Q1.**

**Stem:** Wind can help…

**Options:**
- A) dry clothes and fly kites
- B) grow rocks overnight
- C) turn air into gold
- D) stop the sun

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Wind dries clothes and flies kites.

**Q2.**

**Stem:** Steam is water as a…

**Options:**
- A) gas
- B) solid only
- C) plastic
- D) metal

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Steam is water vapour (gas).

**Q3.**

**Stem:** We get much of our drinking water from…

**Options:**
- A) rivers and taps after cleaning
- B) only from fire
- C) only from plastic smoke
- D) only from sand

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Water often comes from rivers, cleaned for taps.

**Q4.**

**Stem:** Ice melts into…

**Options:**
- A) liquid water
- B) smoke plastic
- C) sand
- D) wood

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Ice melts to liquid water.

**Q5.**

**Stem:** A strong wind storm can be…

**Options:**
- A) dangerous
- B) always safe under trees
- C) made of candy
- D) silent always

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Storms can be dangerous — stay safe.

**Q6.**

**Stem:** Plants also need ___ from air.

**Options:**
- A) gases from air
- B) plastic bags
- C) metal sheets
- D) screens

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Plants use air gases too.

**Q7.**

**Stem:** Closing the tap while brushing…

**Options:**
- A) saves water
- B) wastes water
- C) makes more rain instantly
- D) stops air

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Closing the tap saves water.

**Q8.**

**Stem:** Fog is tiny water drops in the…

**Options:**
- A) air near the ground
- B) only underground always
- C) only in books
- D) only in shoes

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Fog is tiny drops in air near the ground.

**Q9.**

**Stem:** Dirty water can make us…

**Options:**
- A) ill
- B) fly
- C) grow taller instantly
- D) turn invisible

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Dirty water can make us ill.

**Q10.**

**Stem:** Air takes up…

**Options:**
- A) space
- B) no space ever
- C) only colour
- D) only taste

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Air takes up space — fill a balloon.

**Q11.**

**Stem:** The water cycle includes rain, clouds and…

**Options:**
- A) evaporation
- B) plastic making
- C) metal melting only
- D) noise

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Evaporation is part of the water cycle.

**Q12.**

**Stem:** We should not throw rubbish into…

**Options:**
- A) rivers and lakes
- B) dustbins
- C) recycling bins
- D) compost carefully

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Do not throw rubbish into rivers.

**Q13.**

**Stem:** A fan moves…

**Options:**
- A) air
- B) stones
- C) water from wells always
- D) soil only

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** A fan moves air.

**Q14.**

**Stem:** Snow is water in a ___ form.

**Options:**
- A) solid
- B) liquid only
- C) gas only
- D) plastic

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Snow is solid water.

**Q15.**

**Stem:** Clean air is important for…

**Options:**
- A) healthy breathing
- B) making more dust
- C) stopping rain forever
- D) hiding the sun

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Clean air helps healthy breathing.

**Q16.**

**Stem:** Boil water to help make it…

**Options:**
- A) safer to drink when advised
- B) dirtier
- C) into plastic
- D) into sand

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Boiling can make water safer.
