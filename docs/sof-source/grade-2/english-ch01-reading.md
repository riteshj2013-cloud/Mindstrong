# Reading

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: English
- Theme tags: reading
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- stem: Read: "Tina waters the plants every morning." When does Tina water them?
- A: every morning
- B: at night
- C: only on Sunday
- D: never
- answer: A
- explanation: The sentence says every morning.

### Q2
- stem: Read: "The little puppy hid under the table." Where did the puppy hide?
- A: on the roof
- B: under the table
- C: in the sky
- D: in a cup
- answer: B
- explanation: Under the table — from the sentence.

### Q3
- stem: Read: "Rohan shared his lunch with Meena." What did Rohan do?
- A: hid his lunch
- B: threw his lunch
- C: shared his lunch
- D: sold his lunch
- answer: C
- explanation: He shared his lunch with Meena.

### Q4
- stem: Why do we wear a raincoat?
- A: to fly
- B: to sleep underwater
- C: to cook
- D: to stay dry in rain
- answer: D
- explanation: A raincoat keeps us dry in the rain.

### Q5
- stem: Read: "Grandma tells stories at bedtime." When does Grandma tell stories?
- A: at bedtime
- B: at noon only
- C: in maths class only
- D: never
- answer: A
- explanation: At bedtime — from the sentence.

### Q6
- stem: Read: "The bus was late, so Kabir ran." Why did Kabir run?
- A: he was sleepy
- B: the bus was late
- C: it was a holiday
- D: he lost a shoe for fun
- answer: B
- explanation: Because the bus was late.

### Q7
- stem: Which sentence shows kindness?
- A: She broke the toy.
- B: She shouted angrily.
- C: She helped her friend.
- D: She hid the book forever.
- answer: C
- explanation: Helping a friend is kind.

### Q8
- stem: Read: "Ants work together to carry food." What do ants do?
- A: sleep all day only
- B: drive cars
- C: read novels
- D: work together
- answer: D
- explanation: They work together to carry food.

### Q9
- stem: What is the main idea: 'Bo planted seeds. He watered them. Green shoots came up.'?
- A: Bo grew plants
- B: Bo flew a plane
- C: Bo baked a cake
- D: Bo swam
- answer: A
- explanation: The lines are about growing plants.

### Q10
- stem: Read: "Do not touch the hot pan." What should you do?
- A: touch the pan
- B: not touch the hot pan
- C: lick the pan
- D: throw the pan
- answer: B
- explanation: Do not touch — stay safe.

### Q11
- stem: Read: "Leela felt proud after her race." How did Leela feel?
- A: sad
- B: sleepy only
- C: proud
- D: angry
- answer: C
- explanation: Proud — from the sentence.

### Q12
- stem: A library is a place to…
- A: swim with sharks
- B: park aeroplanes
- C: bake only
- D: borrow books
- answer: D
- explanation: Libraries are for books.

### Q13
- stem: Read: "First wash hands. Next eat. Then play." What comes first?
- A: wash hands
- B: play
- C: eat
- D: sleep
- answer: A
- explanation: First means wash hands comes first.

### Q14
- stem: Read: "The moon looks bright tonight." What looks bright?
- A: the sun
- B: the moon
- C: a shoe
- D: a spoon
- answer: B
- explanation: The moon looks bright.

### Q15
- stem: Which title fits a story about a lost kitten found at home?
- A: Space Rockets
- B: Deep Sea Sharks
- C: The Kitten Comes Home
- D: Maths Only
- answer: C
- explanation: The title matches the kitten story.

### Q16
- stem: Read: "Aarav closed the tap to save water." Why did Aarav close the tap?
- A: to waste water
- B: to make noise
- C: to cook rice
- D: to save water
- answer: D
