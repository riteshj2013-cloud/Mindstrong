# Reading

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: English
- Theme tags: reading
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- stem: Read: "Tina waters the plants every morning." When does Tina water them?
- A: at night
- B: every morning
- C: only on Sunday
- D: never
- answer: B
- explanation: The sentence says every morning.

### Q2
- stem: Read: "The little puppy hid under the table." Where did the puppy hide?
- A: on the roof
- B: under the table
- C: in the sky
- D: in a cup
- answer: B
- explanation: Under the table — from the sentence.

### Q3
- stem: Read: "Rohan shared his lunch with Meena." What did Rohan do?
- A: hid his lunch
- B: shared his lunch
- C: threw his lunch
- D: sold his lunch
- answer: B
- explanation: He shared his lunch with Meena.

### Q4
- stem: Why do we wear a raincoat?
- A: to stay dry in rain
- B: to fly
- C: to sleep underwater
- D: to cook
- answer: A
- explanation: A raincoat keeps us dry in the rain.

### Q5

- C: in maths class only
- D: never

### Q6

- A: the bus was late
- B: he was sleepy
- C: it was a holiday
- D: he lost a shoe for fun
- answer: A
- explanation: Because the bus was late.

### Q7
- stem: Which sentence shows kindness?

- D: She hid the book forever.

### Q8

- C: drive cars
- D: read novels

### Q9

- C: Bo baked a cake

### Q10

- D: throw the pan

### Q11
- stem: Read: "Leela felt proud after her race." How did Leela feel?

### Q12
- stem: A library is a place to…
- A: borrow books
- B: swim with sharks

- answer: A
- explanation: Libraries are for books.

### Q13

### Q14
