# Place Value

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: Maths
- Theme tags: place-value
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Practice Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- **stem**: In 47, the digit 4 stands for…
- A) 4 ones
- B) 4 tens
- C) 40 tens
- D) 7 tens
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 4 is in the tens place → 4 tens.

### Q2
- **stem**: In 47, the digit 7 stands for…
- A) 7 tens
- B) 7 ones
- C) 70
- D) 4 ones
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 7 is in the ones place → 7 ones.

### Q3
- **stem**: How many tens in 30?
- A) 3
- B) 0
- C) 30
- D) 10
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 30 = 3 tens and 0 ones.

### Q4
- **stem**: 10 ones make…
- A) 1 ten
- B) 10 tens
- C) 1 one
- D) 0
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 10 ones bundle into 1 ten.

### Q5
- **stem**: Which number is 2 tens and 5 ones?
- A) 25
- B) 52
- C) 205
- D) 7
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 2 tens + 5 ones = 25.

### Q6
- **stem**: Expand 38.
- A) 30 + 8
- B) 3 + 8
- C) 38 + 0 only wrong
- D) 80 + 3
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 38 = 30 + 8.

### Q7
- **stem**: Which is greater: 29 or 92?
- A) 29
- B) 92
- C) same
- D) 0
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 92 has more tens, so it is greater.

### Q8
- **stem**: What is the place of 6 in 61?
- A) ones
- B) tens
- C) hundreds
- D) none
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: In 61, 6 is in the tens place.

### Q9
- **stem**: 45 = ___ tens + ___ ones
- A) 4 and 5
- B) 5 and 4
- C) 40 and 5 wrong words
- D) 9 and 0
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 45 = 4 tens and 5 ones.

### Q10
- **stem**: Smallest two-digit number?
- A) 10
- B) 11
- C) 9
- D) 01
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 10 is the smallest two-digit number.

### Q11
- **stem**: Biggest two-digit number?
- A) 99
- B) 90
- C) 100
- D) 89
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 99 is the biggest two-digit number.

### Q12
- **stem**: 20 + 7 = ?
- A) 27
- B) 207
- C) 72
- D) 9
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 20 + 7 = 27.

### Q13
- **stem**: Riya has 3 packs of 10 stickers and 4 loose. How many?
- A) 34
- B) 43
- C) 7
- D) 304
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 3 tens + 4 ones = 34.

### Q14
- **stem**: Which shows 5 tens?
- A) 50
- B) 5
- C) 15
- D) 500
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 5 tens = 50.

### Q15
- **stem**: In 80, how many ones?
- A) 0
- B) 8
- C) 80
- D) 10
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 80 has 0 ones.

### Q16
- **stem**: Order small to big: 15, 51, 25. Middle number?
- A) 15
- B) 25
- C) 51
- D) 10
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Order: 15, 25, 51. Middle is 25.
