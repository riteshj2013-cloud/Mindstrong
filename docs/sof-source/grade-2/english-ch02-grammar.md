# Grammar

## Meta
- Grade: 2
- Subject: English
- Theme tags: grammar
- Source basis: NCERT Class 2 themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 2 (very short sentences)

## Learning objectives
- Age-appropriate olympiad-lite practice for Grade 2.
- Set A and Set B have 16 MCQs each (32 total).

## Set A — 16 MCQs

### Q1
- stem: Pick the correct sentence.
- A: she go to school.
- B: She goes to school.
- C: She going to school.
- D: She gone to school.
- answer: B
- explanation: She goes — singular + -s on the verb.

### Q2
- stem: They ___ playing in the park.
- A: is
- B: are
- C: am
- D: be
- answer: B
- explanation: They takes are.

### Q3
- stem: Choose the past tense of walk.
- A: walk
- B: walks
- C: walked
- D: walking
- answer: C
- explanation: Walked is past tense.

### Q4
- stem: A ___ names a person, place or thing.
- A: verb
- B: noun
- C: adjective
- D: question
- answer: B
- explanation: A noun names a person, place or thing.

### Q5
- stem: Pick the describing word.
- A: run
- B: happy
- C: school
- D: under
- answer: B
- explanation: Happy describes a feeling.

### Q6
- stem: I ___ finished my homework.
- A: has
- B: have
- C: is
- D: are
- answer: B
- explanation: I have finished…

### Q7
- stem: Which needs a capital letter?
- A: mumbai
- B: river
- C: pencil
- D: cloud
- answer: A
- explanation: City names: Mumbai.

### Q8
- stem: We ___ to the market yesterday.
- A: go
- B: goes
- C: went
- D: going
- answer: C
- explanation: Yesterday → past → went.

### Q9
- stem: An adjective describes a…
- A: noun
- B: full stop
- C: number only
- D: silence
- answer: A
- explanation: Adjectives describe nouns.

### Q10
- stem: She ___ a song now.
- A: sing
- B: sings
- C: is singing
- D: sang
- answer: C
- explanation: Now → is singing.

### Q11
- stem: Pick the pronoun for Rani.
- A: he
- B: she
- C: it
- D: they
- answer: B
- explanation: Rani is a girl → she.

### Q12
- stem: This is ___ apple.
- A: a
- B: an
- C: the the
- D: two
- answer: B
- explanation: An before vowel sound.

### Q13
- stem: Which is a question word?
- A: quietly
- B: where
- C: green
- D: slowly
- answer: B
- explanation: Where asks about place.

### Q14
- stem: The opposite of always is…
- A: never
- B: often
- C: sometimes
- D: daily
- answer: A
- explanation: Never is the opposite of always.

### Q15
- stem: Join: soft + ly =
- A: softly
- B: softing
- C: softs
- D: softness
- answer: A
- explanation: soft + ly = softly (how).

### Q16
- stem: Pick the correct punctuation.
- A: What is your name.
- B: What is your name?
- C: What is your name!
- D: what is your name
- answer: B
- explanation: Questions end with ?
