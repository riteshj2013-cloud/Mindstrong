## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Equal Groups
- **tts**:
  - Three plates with four samosas on each. Four plus four plus four is twelve.
  - So three times four is twelve!
- **on_screen**: Three plates appear one by one, each with 4 samosas; a counter adds 4 + 4 + 4 and then flips to show 3 × 4 = 12. Child adds a fourth plate and watches the total change.
- **check**: 5 + 5 + 5 + 5 is the same as ___ × 5. — Answer: 4

### step_2: Arrays and Turning Around
- **tts**:
  - Rows and columns make an array. Turn it around, and the total stays the same.
  - Three times five equals five times three.
- **on_screen**: A tray of 3 rows × 5 idlis; child taps "Rotate" and it becomes 5 rows × 3, with both totals showing 15.
- **check**: 6 × 4 = 4 × ___ — Answer: 6

### step_3: Tables Are Skip Counting
- **tts**:
  - Tables are just skip counting. In the nine table, the digits always add up to nine!
  - Nine times seven is sixty-three.
- **on_screen**: A frog hops along a number line in jumps of 9; each landing spot (9, 18, 27 …) lights up, and the child taps any answer to see its digits add to 9. A tables grid from 2 to 12 is available to explore.
- **check**: What is 9 × 6? — Answer: 54

### step_4: The Magic of Zero and One
- **tts**:
  - Any number times zero is zero.
  - Any number times one stays exactly the same.
- **on_screen**: Zero empty baskets of mangoes show 0 mangoes; then one basket with 75 mangoes shows 75. Child sorts cards like 0 × 58 and 1 × 58 into the right answer bins.
- **check**: What is 0 × 75? — Answer: 0

### step_5: Multiplying Bigger Numbers
- **tts**:
  - Multiply the ones first, then the tens. Carry over when you need to!
  - Forty-six times three is one hundred thirty-eight.
- **on_screen**: Column method for 46 × 3 with place value blocks; the carried 1 hops up above the tens column. Then 214 × 4 and 12 × 11 are split into easy parts (12 × 10 + 12 × 1).
- **check**: What is 23 × 4? — Answer: 92

### step_6: Sharing and Grouping
- **tts**:
  - Division means sharing equally, or making equal groups.
  - Twenty-four laddoos shared by four friends gives six each.
- **on_screen**: 24 laddoos are dealt one by one onto 4 plates until they run out; then 24 laddoos are packed into boxes of 6, showing 4 boxes.
- **check**: What is 35 ÷ 5? — Answer: 7

### step_7: Fact Families and Missing Numbers
- **tts**:
  - Multiplication and division are partners.
  - If seven times eight is fifty-six, then fifty-six divided by eight is seven.
- **on_screen**: A triangle with 7, 8 and 56 at its corners; child builds the four facts (7 × 8, 8 × 7, 56 ÷ 8, 56 ÷ 7), then fills a missing-number box in 8 × ___ = 72.
- **check**: 8 × ___ = 72 — Answer: 9

### step_8: Leftovers and Word Problems
- **tts**:
  - Sometimes things don't share equally. What is left over is the remainder.
  - The remainder is always smaller than the number you divide by.
- **on_screen**: 26 pencils are shared into 4 pencil boxes; 2 pencils are left on the desk and glow as the remainder. Then a short story about buying notebooks for ₹25 each asks the child to pick "multiply" or "divide" first.
- **check**: What is 26 ÷ 4? — Answer: 6 remainder 2

## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 + 6 is the same as which of these?
- **options**:
  - A) 6 × 6
  - B) 5 + 6
  - C) 5 × 6
  - D) 6 × 4
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: There are 5 groups of 6, so it is 5 × 6.
- **skill**: repeated_addition
- **difficulty**: easy
