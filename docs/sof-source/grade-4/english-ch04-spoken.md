## Interactive lesson outline
- L1: Hello! Today we practise speaking kindly in real situations.
- L2: Greetings change with the time of day: good morning, good afternoon, good evening.
- L3: Polite requests use May I or Could I, and they often add please.
- L4: Magic manners words: please, thank you, sorry and excuse me.
- L5: On the phone, say who you are, offer to take a message, and end with thank you or bye.
- L6: Let's try one. Rough: "Give me your pencil." Kinder: "Could I borrow your pencil, please?"
- L7: When someone says thank you, a warm reply is "You're welcome."
- L8: If you bump someone, say sorry and check they are all right.
- L9: (optional) In India, children often say uncle or auntie to respectful older people who are not family.
- L10: Now choose the kindest, clearest reply in each situation.
- L11: Wonderful — your kind voice is ready for practice!

### D1 — At the Library
**Librarian:** Good afternoon, Aarav. How may I help you?
**Aarav:** Good afternoon, ma'am. May I borrow this book on rivers, please?
**Librarian:** Yes. Please return it by next Friday.
**Aarav:** Thank you, ma'am. I will.
**Librarian:** You're welcome. Enjoy reading!

### D2 — At the Fruit Shop
**Shopkeeper:** Hello! What would you like?
**Meera:** Hello, uncle. Could I have half a kilo of apples, please?
**Shopkeeper:** Of course. Here you are.
**Meera:** Thank you. How much is that?
**Shopkeeper:** Forty rupees.
**Meera:** Here is fifty. Please keep the change — oh, wait, here is exact change.
**Shopkeeper:** Thank you. Come again!

### D3 — Phone Call
**Riya:** Hello, this is Riya speaking.
**Caller:** Hello, Riya. May I speak to your mother, please?
**Riya:** She is in a meeting. May I take a message?
**Caller:** Yes. Please tell her that Mrs. Khan called about the PTA meeting.
**Riya:** I will tell her. Thank you for calling.
**Caller:** Thank you, Riya. Bye!

### D4 — In the Classroom
**Teacher:** Class, please take out your notebooks.
**Kabir:** Excuse me, ma'am. May I sharpen my pencil?
**Teacher:** Yes, Kabir. Please be quick.
**Kabir:** Thank you, ma'am.
**Ananya:** Ma'am, I forgot my eraser at home.
**Teacher:** You may borrow one from a friend. Next time, pack carefully.
**Ananya:** Sorry, ma'am. I will remember.
