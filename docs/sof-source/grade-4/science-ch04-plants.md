# Chapter 4: Plants

## Meta
- Grade: 4
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: plants, roots, stem, leaf, photosynthesis, chlorophyll, germination, seeds, plant uses, fibre, timber
- Source basis: NCERT Class 4 EVS / typical olympiad themes (original items only)

## Pictorial notes
- Visual items: optional later; this pack ships text-first MCQs (24 + 24). Inline `**Diagram (SVG):**` pictorials may be added in a follow-up.

## Learning objectives
- I can name plant parts and say what each mainly does.
- I can explain photosynthesis in simple words: sunlight, air, water → food + oxygen.
- I can list what most seeds need to germinate and the first stages of growth.
- I can give Indian examples of plant uses: food, fibre, timber, medicine and cleaner air.
- I can tell tap root from fibrous root and give everyday examples.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Plant parts at work
- **Visual:** A labelled garden plant: roots in soil, stem, leaves, flower and a small fruit. Tara taps each label.
- **TTS:**
  - "Every plant part has a job."
  - "Roots drink water and hold the plant."
  - "The stem stands tall and carries water."
  - "Leaves make food. Flowers can become fruits with seeds."
- **Interaction:** Tap-to-label. Drag part names onto the plant. A chime plays for each correct match.

### Scene 2: The leaf kitchen
- **Visual:** A big green leaf with arrows: sun, air (carbon dioxide) and water in; food and oxygen out.
- **TTS:**
  - "Leaves are the plant's kitchen."
  - "Chlorophyll makes leaves green and traps sunlight."
  - "Plants take in carbon dioxide and give out oxygen."
  - "This food-making is called photosynthesis."
- **Interaction:** Mix-the-ingredients. Drag sun, water and air cards onto the leaf. Watch a food sparkle appear.

### Scene 3: Wake up, seed!
- **Visual:** Four jars: dry seeds, wet cold seeds, wet warm seeds, wet warm seeds with no air. Only the warm wet jar with air sprouts moong.
- **TTS:**
  - "Germination is a seed starting to grow."
  - "Most seeds need water, air and warmth."
  - "The baby root comes out first, then the shoot."
  - "Stored food feeds the seedling until leaves can make food."
- **Interaction:** Choose-the-jar. Pick which jar will sprout and arrange root-then-shoot cards in order.

### Scene 4: Plants we use
- **Visual:** A market scene: rice and dal, cotton cloth, wooden chair, tulsi plant, shade tree along a road.
- **TTS:**
  - "Plants give us food, fibre, wood and medicines."
  - "Cotton and jute become cloth and bags."
  - "Trees give shade and fresher air."
  - "Using less paper helps save trees."
- **Interaction:** Sort cards into Food, Fibre, Timber, Medicine and Air-and-shade baskets.

## Quiz Set A — 24 MCQs

### A1
**Stem:** Which part of a plant usually grows under the soil and takes in water?
- A) Root
- B) Flower
- C) Fruit
- D) Leaf

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Roots grow down into the soil. They take in water and minerals and hold the plant firmly.

### A2
**Stem:** Which plant part is often called the plant's kitchen because it makes food?
- A) Stem
- B) Leaf
- C) Root
- D) Seed

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Green leaves make food for the plant using sunlight, air and water. That process is called photosynthesis.

### A3
**Stem:** What do we call the process by which green leaves make food?
- A) Germination
- B) Evaporation
- C) Photosynthesis
- D) Digestion

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Photosynthesis means green leaves make food using sunlight, air (carbon dioxide) and water.

### A4
**Stem:** Which gas do green plants take in from air during photosynthesis?
- A) Oxygen
- B) Nitrogen
- C) Steam
- D) Carbon dioxide

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** During photosynthesis, plants take in carbon dioxide from the air and give out oxygen.

### A5
**Stem:** Which gas do green plants give out during photosynthesis that animals need to breathe?
- A) Oxygen
- B) Carbon dioxide
- C) Smoke
- D) Helium

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Photosynthesis releases oxygen into the air. Animals and people need oxygen to breathe.

### A6
**Stem:** The green colour in leaves comes mainly from a pigment called ______.
- A) Haemoglobin
- B) Chlorophyll
- C) Melanin
- D) Iodine

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Chlorophyll is the green pigment that helps leaves trap sunlight for photosynthesis.

### A7
**Stem:** What does the stem mainly do for a plant?
- A) Make seeds only
- B) Dig deep into the soil
- C) Hold the plant up and carry water to the leaves
- D) Catch insects for food

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The stem supports the plant and works like a pipe, carrying water and food between roots and leaves.

### A8
**Stem:** A carrot that we pull from the ground is mainly which plant part?
- A) Leaf
- B) Flower
- C) Fruit
- D) Root

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A carrot is a swollen root that stores food. It grows under the soil.

### A9
**Stem:** When we eat spinach (palak), which plant part are we mostly eating?
- A) Leaf
- B) Root
- C) Stem
- D) Seed

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Spinach is made of soft green leaves. Leafy vegetables give us vitamins and fibre.

### A10
**Stem:** What do we usually find inside a ripe mango or apple?
- A) Roots
- B) Seeds
- C) Leaves
- D) Stems

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Fruits grow from flowers and usually protect seeds inside. Seeds can grow into new plants.

### A11
**Stem:** Germination means ______.
- A) A leaf falling off
- B) A flower changing colour
- C) A seed starting to grow into a young plant
- D) A fruit becoming sweet

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Germination is when a seed wakes up and begins to grow into a seedling.

### A12
**Stem:** Which three things do most seeds need to germinate?
- A) Soil, moonlight and sugar
- B) Only bright sunlight
- C) Salt, ice and wind
- D) Water, air and warmth

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Most seeds need water, air and warmth to germinate. Soil and sunlight help later as the seedling grows.

### A13
**Stem:** In germination, which part of the seedling usually comes out of the seed first?
- A) Root (radicle)
- B) Flower
- C) Fruit
- D) Leaf tip only

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The baby root, called the radicle, usually comes out first and grows downward into the soil.

### A14
**Stem:** Why do farmers grow neem, tulsi and other useful plants near homes and fields?
- A) Only for decoration
- B) They have many uses such as shade, medicine and clean air
- C) They stop rainfall
- D) They make the soil salty

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Many plants give shade, medicine, timber, fibre, food and fresher air. Neem and tulsi are useful in Indian homes.

### A15
**Stem:** Cotton clothes come mainly from which plant product?
- A) Wooden stems
- B) Tree roots
- C) Soft fibres around cotton seeds
- D) Fruit juice

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Cotton fibre grows around cotton seeds. It is spun into thread and woven into cloth.

### A16
**Stem:** Which of these is a plant product used as food in India?
- A) Plastic bottle
- B) Glass bangle
- C) Iron nail
- D) Rice grain

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Rice is a grain from the rice plant. Many of our everyday foods come from plants.

### A17
**Stem:** A potato grows under the soil and has "eyes" that can sprout. A potato is a ______.
- A) Underground stem
- B) Root
- C) Flower
- D) Fruit

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A potato is an underground stem that stores food. New shoots can grow from its eyes.

### A18
**Stem:** Flowers are important to plants mainly because they ______.
- A) Make the soil dry
- B) Help the plant make fruits and seeds
- C) Stop photosynthesis
- D) Turn into roots

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Flowers help plants reproduce. After pollination, flowers can grow into fruits that hold seeds.

### A19
**Stem:** Which of these plants has a thick woody stem called a trunk?
- A) Grass
- B) Wheat seedling
- C) Mango tree
- D) Moss on a wall

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Trees like mango have a thick woody stem called a trunk. Grass has a soft, thin stem.

### A20
**Stem:** Leaves have tiny openings that help gases move in and out. These openings are called ______.
- A) Roots
- B) Petals
- C) Seeds
- D) Stomata

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Stomata are tiny pores on leaves. Carbon dioxide enters and oxygen and water vapour can leave through them.

### A21
**Stem:** Riya puts dry moong seeds in a dry jar with no water. Will they germinate soon?
- A) No, because seeds usually need water to start growing
- B) Yes, because seeds never need water
- C) Yes, if the jar is painted green
- D) Yes, if she adds salt

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Without water, most seeds stay dormant and do not germinate. Water helps the seed swell and wake up.

### A22
**Stem:** Which plant part carries water from the roots up to the leaves?
- A) Petal
- B) Stem
- C) Seed coat
- D) Fruit skin

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Water travels up through tubes in the stem from the roots to the leaves.

### A23
**Stem:** Teak and sal wood used for furniture come from ______.
- A) Animal bones
- B) Sea sand
- C) Tree trunks
- D) Plastic bags

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Timber for furniture comes from the woody trunks of trees such as teak and sal.

### A24
**Stem:** Which statement about green plants is true?
- A) They only take in oxygen and never give any gas out
- B) They do not need sunlight at all
- C) They eat insects for all their food
- D) They make their own food and also help keep air fresh by giving out oxygen

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Green plants make food by photosynthesis and release oxygen, which helps living things breathe.

## Quiz Set B — 24 MCQs

### B1
**Stem:** Which of these is NOT a main job of roots?
- A) Making colourful petals
- B) Taking in water
- C) Holding the plant in soil
- D) Taking in minerals

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Roots take in water and minerals and anchor the plant. Making petals is a job of flowers.

### B2
**Stem:** Photosynthesis mainly happens in which plant part?
- A) Dry wooden bark
- B) Green leaves
- C) Hard seeds only
- D) Underground stones

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Green leaves contain chlorophyll and are the main place where photosynthesis happens.

### B3
**Stem:** During photosynthesis, plants need sunlight mainly to ______.
- A) Paint the leaves blue
- B) Freeze the water in roots
- C) Provide energy to make food
- D) Turn seeds into rocks

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Sunlight gives the energy that chlorophyll uses to make food from carbon dioxide and water.

### B4
**Stem:** Which of these is a fibrous root system plant?
- A) Carrot
- B) Radish
- C) Beetroot
- D) Grass

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Grass has many thin fibrous roots. Carrot, radish and beetroot have a thick tap root.

### B5
**Stem:** A mango grows from a flower on the tree. A mango is a ______.
- A) Fruit
- B) Root
- C) Leaf
- D) Stem bark

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A mango develops from a flower and holds the seed, so it is a fruit.

### B6
**Stem:** Which condition would most likely stop a healthy seed from germinating?
- A) A little water, air and a warm room
- B) Keeping it in a freezer with no warmth
- C) Placing it on wet cotton in a warm cupboard
- D) Sprinkling it lightly with water daily

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Most seeds need warmth as well as water and air. A freezer is too cold for germination.

### B7
**Stem:** The baby shoot that grows upward from a germinating seed is called the ______.
- A) Radicle
- B) Petiole
- C) Plumule
- D) Stamen

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The plumule is the baby shoot that grows up toward light. The radicle is the baby root.

### B8
**Stem:** Why do people plant trees along roads and in parks?
- A) Trees make the air dirtier
- B) Trees stop all rain forever
- C) Trees remove all soil
- D) Trees give shade, fresher air and homes for birds

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Trees give shade, release oxygen, reduce dust and heat, and provide homes for birds and insects.

### B9
**Stem:** Jute bags are made from plant ______.
- A) Fibres from jute stems
- B) Flowers only
- C) Animal fur
- D) Metal wires

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Jute fibre comes from the stem of the jute plant and is used to make bags and mats.

### B10
**Stem:** Which plant product is used as a medicine or home remedy in many Indian houses?
- A) Plastic wrapper
- B) Tulsi leaves
- C) Glass marble
- D) Rubber tyre only

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Tulsi (holy basil) leaves are used in many homes for herbal drinks and remedies.

### B11
**Stem:** Water from the soil reaches the leaves mainly by travelling through the ______.
- A) Flower petals
- B) Fruit juice
- C) Stem
- D) Seed coat

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The stem has tiny tubes that carry water upward from roots to leaves.

### B12
**Stem:** Which of these foods comes from a plant seed or grain?
- A) Egg
- B) Fish
- C) Milk
- D) Wheat roti

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Wheat grains are seeds of the wheat plant. Roti is made from wheat flour.

### B13
**Stem:** A cactus stores water in its thick stem. This helps it live in a ______ place.
- A) Dry desert
- B) Very wet swamp only
- C) Deep ocean
- D) Snowy polar ice only

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Desert plants like cactus store water and often have spines instead of broad leaves.

### B14
**Stem:** What do bees and butterflies often carry from flower to flower?
- A) Soil
- B) Pollen
- C) Roots
- D) Bark

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Insects carry pollen from flower to flower. This helps plants make fruits and seeds.

### B15
**Stem:** Which statement about germination is correct?
- A) Seeds need only moonlight to grow
- B) Seeds eat insects for energy
- C) A seed uses food stored inside it until leaves can make food
- D) Germination means a fruit ripening

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Until the seedling's leaves can photosynthesise, the young plant uses food stored in the seed.

### B16
**Stem:** Paper is mostly made from ______.
- A) Animal bones
- B) Pure plastic only
- C) Sea salt
- D) Plant fibres from wood pulp

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Most paper is made from wood pulp, which comes from plant fibres in trees.

### B17
**Stem:** Which plant part attracts insects with colour and scent?
- A) Flower
- B) Root tip
- C) Dry bark
- D) Underground potato eye

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Bright, scented flowers attract insects that help with pollination.

### B18
**Stem:** A tap root is best described as ______.
- A) Many equal thin roots with no main root
- B) One main thick root growing downward with smaller side roots
- C) A green leaf with veins
- D) A soft petal

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A tap root has one main root going deep, with thinner side roots. Carrot and radish show this.

### B19
**Stem:** Why should we not waste paper and cut trees carelessly?
- A) Paper grows on its own in rivers
- B) Trees are useless to people
- C) Trees and plant products are useful and trees help the environment
- D) Cutting all trees makes more rain instantly

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Trees give oxygen, shade, wood and homes for animals. Saving paper helps save trees.

### B20
**Stem:** Which of these is a use of plants for clothing?
- A) Silk from cotton only
- B) Iron from leaves
- C) Glass from roots
- D) Cotton and jute fibres made into cloth and bags

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Cotton and jute fibres from plants are used to make clothes and bags.

### B21
**Stem:** Arjun covers a green plant with a thick black box for many days. What is most likely?
- A) It will struggle to make food without light
- B) It will photosynthesise better
- C) It will turn into a rock
- D) It will need no water ever again

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Without light, photosynthesis slows or stops, so the plant cannot make food well.

### B22
**Stem:** Which gas do we (and other animals) give out that plants can use in photosynthesis?
- A) Oxygen only
- B) Carbon dioxide
- C) Ozone from machines
- D) Helium balloons

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** We breathe out carbon dioxide. Plants use carbon dioxide during photosynthesis.

### B23
**Stem:** Mustard oil and coconut oil are examples of ______ from plants.
- A) Metals
- B) Animal bones
- C) Useful products (oils)
- D) Rocks

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Many oils used in Indian cooking come from plant seeds or fruits, such as mustard and coconut.

### B24
**Stem:** Which order correctly shows a plant's life story from the start?
- A) Fruit → flower → seed → plant
- B) Leaf → root → only flower forever
- C) Stem → seed → root only
- D) Seed → seedling → plant → flower → fruit → seed

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A seed grows into a seedling and then a plant. Flowers can form fruits with new seeds.
