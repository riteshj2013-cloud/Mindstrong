# Chapter 6: Our Body

## Meta
- Grade: 4
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: human body, sense organs, eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin, bones, skeleton, muscles, joints, hygiene, exercise, sleep, posture
- Source basis: NCERT Class 4 EVS / typical olympiad themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 4 (clear, high visual/TTS)

## Pictorial notes
- Visual items: optional later; this pack ships text-first MCQs (24 + 24). Inline `**Diagram (SVG):**` pictorials may be added in a follow-up.

## Learning objectives
- I can name the five sense organs and the job of each.
- I can explain that bones form a skeleton for shape, support and protection.
- I can say that muscles pull on bones so the body can move at joints.
- I can list healthy habits: hygiene, exercise, balanced food, clean water, posture and sleep.
- I can give simple safety tips for eyes, ears and small cuts.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Five windows to the world
- **Visual:** Tara with glowing icons on eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin. Each icon plays a short sense clue.
- **TTS:**
  - "We learn with five sense organs."
  - "Eyes see. Ears hear. Nose smells."
  - "Tongue tastes. Skin feels touch, heat and pain."
  - "Sense messages go to the brain."
- **Interaction:** Match each clue card (school bell, hot cup, lemon, rose, chalkboard) to the correct sense organ.

### Scene 2: Bones and the skeleton
- **Visual:** A friendly skeleton outline. Skull, ribcage and backbone highlight when tapped.
- **TTS:**
  - "Bones join to make the skeleton."
  - "The skull protects the brain."
  - "The ribcage protects the heart and lungs."
  - "Joints are places where bones meet so we can bend."
- **Interaction:** Tap skull, ribs and spine to hear what each protects. Bend an elbow hinge in a simple animation.

### Scene 3: Muscles that pull
- **Visual:** An arm bending: muscle shortens and the forearm lifts a school bag.
- **TTS:**
  - "Muscles pull on bones to make movement."
  - "When a muscle contracts, it shortens."
  - "Walking, writing and smiling all use muscles."
  - "Exercise helps muscles grow stronger."
- **Interaction:** Slide to contract/relax the arm muscle and lift or lower the bag.

### Scene 4: Healthy habits checklist
- **Visual:** A daily checklist: wash hands, brush teeth, play outdoors, drink water, sit tall, sleep on time.
- **TTS:**
  - "Wash hands before eating."
  - "Brush teeth twice a day."
  - "Play, drink clean water and sleep well."
  - "Good posture cares for your backbone."
- **Interaction:** Tick each healthy habit to light a star. A wrap card reminds: senses, skeleton, muscles, habits.

## Quiz Set A — 24 MCQs

### A1
**Stem:** How many main sense organs do we usually learn about in Class 4?
- A) Five
- B) Two
- C) Three
- D) Ten

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The five sense organs are eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin.

### A2
**Stem:** Which sense organ helps you see the board in class?
- A) Ears
- B) Eyes
- C) Tongue
- D) Nose

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Eyes are the sense organs of sight. They help us see shapes, colours and light.

### A3
**Stem:** Which sense organ helps you hear the school bell?
- A) Eyes
- B) Tongue
- C) Ears
- D) Skin only

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Ears are for hearing. They collect sound and send messages to the brain.

### A4
**Stem:** Your nose mainly helps you ______.
- A) Taste sugar directly
- B) Hear music
- C) See colours
- D) Smell

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** The nose is the sense organ of smell. Smell also helps us enjoy food and notice danger like smoke.

### A5
**Stem:** Different areas of the tongue help you notice tastes such as sweet, sour, salty and bitter. The tongue is for ______.
- A) Taste
- B) Hearing
- C) Sight
- D) Balance only

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The tongue is the sense organ of taste. Taste buds detect different tastes in food.

### A6
**Stem:** Which sense organ helps you feel that a cup of chai is hot?
- A) Eyes only
- B) Skin
- C) Hair only
- D) Teeth enamel

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Skin is the sense organ of touch. It feels heat, cold, pressure and pain.

### A7
**Stem:** Bones joined together make up the ______.
- A) Digestive juice
- B) Only the skin
- C) Skeleton
- D) Only the hair

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The skeleton is the frame of bones that gives shape and support to the body.

### A8
**Stem:** Which bones protect the brain?
- A) Ribs only
- B) Finger bones only
- C) Toe nails
- D) Skull bones

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** The skull is a bony case that protects the brain.

### A9
**Stem:** The ribcage mainly protects ______.
- A) The heart and lungs
- B) Only the toes
- C) Only the hair
- D) Only the tongue

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Ribs form a cage around the chest that helps protect the heart and lungs.

### A10
**Stem:** Muscles help us ______.
- A) Only grow hair
- B) Move body parts by pulling on bones
- C) Make blood from air
- D) Replace the skeleton completely

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Muscles pull on bones to create movement. We use muscles to walk, write and smile.

### A11
**Stem:** Bones are hard and give the body ______.
- A) Only colour
- B) Only taste
- C) Shape, support and protection
- D) Only smell

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Bones support the body, give it shape and protect soft organs inside.

### A12
**Stem:** A joint is a place where ______.
- A) Hair grows from skin only
- B) Blood becomes bone
- C) Taste buds sit on the ear
- D) Two bones meet and movement can happen

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Joints are meeting places of bones. Different joints allow bending, turning or little movement.

### A13
**Stem:** Which habit helps keep your body strong and healthy?
- A) Playing outdoors and exercising regularly
- B) Never sleeping
- C) Eating only chips every day
- D) Skipping all meals

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Regular exercise and outdoor play strengthen muscles and bones and improve fitness.

### A14
**Stem:** Why should you wash your hands with soap before eating?
- A) To change hand colour
- B) To remove dirt and germs that can make you ill
- C) To make bones longer instantly
- D) Because soap is a food

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Soap and water wash away germs. Clean hands help stop illness from spreading to your mouth.

### A15
**Stem:** Brushing teeth twice a day mainly helps ______.
- A) Grow taller overnight
- B) Sharpen hearing
- C) Keep teeth and gums clean and healthy
- D) Change eye colour

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Brushing removes food bits and helps prevent tooth decay and gum problems.

### A16
**Stem:** Sleep is important because it helps the body ______.
- A) Stop needing food forever
- B) Turn bones into muscle overnight only
- C) Remove the need for water
- D) Rest, repair and stay ready for the next day

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** During sleep the body rests and recovers. Children need enough sleep to grow and learn well.

### A17
**Stem:** Sitting and standing with a straight back is good for ______.
- A) Posture and spine health
- B) Stopping all breathing
- C) Removing the need for food
- D) Closing the ears

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Good posture keeps the backbone healthier and helps you breathe and move comfortably.

### A18
**Stem:** Which food habit supports strong bones?
- A) Only sugary drinks
- B) Including milk, curd or other calcium-rich foods with a balanced diet
- C) Never drinking water
- D) Eating soil

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Calcium-rich foods such as milk and curd help build strong bones and teeth, along with a balanced diet.

### A19
**Stem:** If you get a small cut, you should ______.
- A) Rub it with dirty mud
- B) Ignore heavy bleeding always
- C) Clean it and keep it covered as an adult advises
- D) Put chalk dust from the board on it

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Cleaning a cut and keeping it covered helps prevent germs from entering. Ask a trusted adult for help.

### A20
**Stem:** We should not poke sharp objects into our ears because ______.
- A) Ears do not matter
- B) It improves eyesight
- C) It makes bones stronger
- D) We can damage the ear and harm hearing

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** The ear is delicate. Sharp objects can injure it and affect hearing. Clean outer ears gently.

### A21
**Stem:** Reading in dim light for a long time is a bad habit mainly for the ______.
- A) Eyes
- B) Knees
- C) Hair roots only
- D) Toe nails

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Eyes need enough light to see comfortably. Dim light can strain the eyes.

### A22
**Stem:** Drinking clean water every day helps the body ______.
- A) Stop all organ work
- B) Stay hydrated and work properly
- C) Turn into a fish
- D) Remove the need for sleep forever

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Water is essential for life. Clean drinking water helps every system of the body work well.

### A23
**Stem:** Which activity is a healthy habit?
- A) Watching screens all night with no sleep
- B) Skipping bath for many days
- C) Playing kho-kho or cycling and then resting well
- D) Sharing used handkerchiefs when sick without care

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Active play plus enough rest supports a healthy body. Hygiene and sleep matter too.

### A24
**Stem:** Sense organs send messages to the ______ so we can understand the world.
- A) Only the fingernails
- B) Only the stomach
- C) Only the hair
- D) Brain

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Sense organs detect changes and send signals to the brain, which helps us understand and respond.

## Quiz Set B — 24 MCQs

### B1
**Stem:** Which pair is correctly matched?
- A) Tongue — taste
- B) Eyes — hearing
- C) Skin — sight
- D) Nose — hearing

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The tongue is for taste. Eyes see, ears hear, nose smells and skin feels touch.

### B2
**Stem:** Closing your eyes tightly mainly blocks which sense?
- A) Smell
- B) Sight
- C) Taste
- D) Hearing completely forever

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Eyelids can block light from entering the eyes, so you cannot see.

### B3
**Stem:** A cold with a blocked nose can make food seem less tasty because ______.
- A) Teeth disappear
- B) Eyes stop working
- C) Smell and taste work closely together
- D) Bones melt

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Smell helps us enjoy flavour. When the nose is blocked, food can seem bland.

### B4
**Stem:** Which part of the body has many bones that let fingers move in many ways?
- A) The forehead only
- B) The tongue tip
- C) A single tooth
- D) The hand

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Hands have many small bones and joints so we can grip, write and play.

### B5
**Stem:** Muscles work by ______.
- A) Contracting (pulling) and then relaxing
- B) Pushing bones only like magnets from afar
- C) Turning into water
- D) Making sunlight

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A muscle shortens when it contracts and pulls a bone; then it relaxes.

### B6
**Stem:** The backbone (spine) is important because it ______.
- A) Only stores food like a stomach
- B) Supports the body and protects the spinal cord
- C) Is used only for tasting
- D) Pumps blood like the heart

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The backbone supports us upright and protects the spinal cord, which carries messages.

### B7
**Stem:** Which is a safety habit for the eyes?
- A) Looking straight at the bright sun
- B) Rubbing eyes with dirty hands often
- C) Wearing protection for bright sun or sparks when adults advise, and resting screens
- D) Reading only in total darkness

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Protect eyes from harsh glare and give them rest. Never stare at the sun.

### B8
**Stem:** Covering your mouth when you sneeze helps ______.
- A) Make bones longer
- B) Improve night vision
- C) Clean the tongue automatically
- D) Stop germs from spreading in the air to others

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Sneezes spray tiny droplets. Covering the mouth and nose is a kind, healthy habit.

### B9
**Stem:** Which drink is the healthiest everyday choice after play?
- A) Clean water or coconut water
- B) Too many sugary colas
- C) Only energy drinks every hour
- D) Unclean pond water

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Clean water replaces sweat. Coconut water can also help; sugary colas are occasional treats.

### B10
**Stem:** Strong muscles and bones both need ______.
- A) Only sitting still all day
- B) Activity, good food, and rest
- C) No sleep ever
- D) Only junk food

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Moving your body, eating nourishing food and resting help muscles and bones stay strong.

### B11
**Stem:** The sense of touch can warn you when something is sharp or hot so that you ______.
- A) Ignore danger always
- B) Stop breathing
- C) Pull away and stay safer
- D) Lose all other senses

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Pain and heat signals help protect the body from injury.

### B12
**Stem:** Which habit cares for your ears?
- A) Listening to extremely loud headphones for hours
- B) Pouring hot oil without adult help
- C) Hitting the ears hard for fun
- D) Keeping volume comfortable and avoiding sharp objects in the ear

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Loud sound and poking can harm hearing. Keep volumes moderate and ears gently clean outside.

### B13
**Stem:** We have different kinds of joints. The elbow mainly works like a ______.
- A) Hinge that bends and straightens
- B) Fixed joint that never bends
- C) Wheel that spins the head fully like an owl always
- D) Pump that only beats

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** The elbow is a hinge joint. It mainly bends and straightens the arm.

### B14
**Stem:** A balanced day for a Class 4 child should include ______.
- A) Only screens and no meals
- B) Study, play, meals, water, hygiene and sleep
- C) No play at all
- D) Staying awake past midnight every night

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Healthy routines mix learning, active play, nourishing food, cleanliness and enough sleep.

### B15
**Stem:** Why do doctors advise vaccines (as guided by adults and health workers)?
- A) To replace the need for food
- B) To change eye colour
- C) To help the body fight certain diseases
- D) To stop all exercise

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Vaccines help the body's defence system recognise and fight some serious diseases.

### B16
**Stem:** Sweating during play is normal. Afterwards you should ______.
- A) Stay in wet clothes all evening if possible
- B) Never bathe again
- C) Eat only chalk
- D) Drink water and freshen up when you can

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Replacing water and cleaning up after sweaty play are healthy habits.

### B17
**Stem:** Nails should be kept clean and trimmed because dirty nails can ______.
- A) Carry germs to your mouth and food
- B) Make you taller
- C) Improve hearing
- D) Replace brushing teeth

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Germs can hide under nails. Clean nails are part of good hygiene.

### B18
**Stem:** Which statement is true?
- A) Bones are soft like insects' wings always
- B) Muscles and bones work together so we can move
- C) Sense organs never send messages to the brain
- D) Sleep is useless for children

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Bones provide a frame; muscles pull on them. Together they create movement.

### B19
**Stem:** If smoke smells strong in the kitchen, your ______ sense warns you first.
- A) Sight only always
- B) Taste of sugar
- C) Smell
- D) Hearing of colours

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The nose detects smells such as smoke and can warn of danger.

### B20
**Stem:** Wearing clean clothes and bathing regularly are habits of ______.
- A) Skipping all exercise
- B) Avoiding all water
- C) Damaging the skin on purpose
- D) Personal hygiene

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Personal hygiene means keeping the body and clothes clean to stay healthier and fresher.

### B21
**Stem:** Which organ pair is part of the five senses?
- A) Eyes and ears
- B) Liver and kidney as sense organs
- C) Only femur and tibia
- D) Only stomach and intestine

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Eyes and ears are two of the five sense organs. Liver and kidneys are not sense organs.

### B22
**Stem:** Bending your knee uses ______.
- A) Only skin colour
- B) Bones, joints and muscles working together
- C) Only hair follicles
- D) Only toenails

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Movement at the knee needs bones meeting at a joint and muscles that pull.

### B23
**Stem:** A healthy plate for growing children should usually include ______.
- A) Only fried snacks every meal
- B) Only cold drinks
- C) A mix of energy foods, body-building foods and fruits or vegetables
- D) No water at all

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A balanced Indian meal mixes grains, dal or other proteins, and protective fruits and vegetables.

### B24
**Stem:** Taking short breaks from long screen time helps mainly the ______.
- A) Sense of smell only
- B) Skeleton to disappear
- C) Tongue to hear
- D) Eyes and overall rest

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Screen breaks reduce eye strain and help you move, stretch and rest your mind.
