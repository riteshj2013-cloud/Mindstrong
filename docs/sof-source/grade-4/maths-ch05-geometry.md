## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Lines, rays, segments
- **tts**:
  - A line goes forever both ways. A ray has one start. A segment has two ends.
- **on_screen**: Three diagrams labelled line / ray / segment.
- **check**: Two end points? — Answer: Line segment

### step_2: Angles and vertex
- **tts**:
  - An angle has two arms and a vertex.
  - Compare with a notebook corner for a right angle.
- **on_screen**: Child taps arms and vertex; square corner glows 90°.
- **check**: Right angle measure? — Answer: 90°

### step_3: Acute, right, obtuse, straight
- **tts**:
  - Acute is less than 90. Obtuse is between 90 and 180. Straight is 180.
- **on_screen**: Angle sorter game into four bins.
- **check**: 35° is? — Answer: Acute

### step_4: 2D shapes
- **tts**:
  - Triangles have 3 sides. Quadrilaterals have 4. Circles are round with no straight sides.
- **on_screen**: Shape cards: triangle, square, rectangle, pentagon, hexagon, circle.
- **check**: 5-sided shape? — Answer: Pentagon

### step_5: Special rectangles and squares
- **tts**:
  - Rectangles have opposite sides equal and four right angles.
  - Squares have four equal sides too.
- **on_screen**: Highlight equal sides and square corners on a window frame.
- **check**: Square sides? — Answer: 4 equal

### step_6: Perimeter idea
- **tts**:
  - Perimeter is the distance around.
  - Walk the fence to find it.
- **on_screen**: Ant walks around a rectangular garden; lengths add up.
- **check**: Perimeter means? — Answer: Distance around

### step_7: Perimeter formulas
- **tts**:
  - Square: four times one side. Rectangle: two times length plus breadth.
- **on_screen**: Numbers appear on sides; child computes 2×(l+b).
- **check**: Square side 5, perimeter? — Answer: 20

### step_8: Parallel and meeting lines
- **tts**:
  - Parallel lines never meet. Intersecting lines cross at a point.
- **on_screen**: Railway tracks vs crossing roads.
- **check**: Never meet? — Answer: Parallel

## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: A closed shape with 3 straight sides is a…
- **options**:
  - A) Quadrilateral
  - B) Triangle
  - C) Circle
  - D) Pentagon
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A triangle has exactly three straight sides.
- **skill**: shapes

### Q02
- **stem**: How many sides does a square have?
- **options**:
  - A) 3
  - B) 4
  - C) 5
  - D) 6
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A square has 4 equal sides and 4 right angles.
- **skill**: lines

### Q03
- **stem**: Which shape has all points at the same distance from the centre?
- **options**:
  - A) Square
  - B) Triangle
  - C) Circle
  - D) Rectangle
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: In a circle, every point on the rim is the same distance from the centre.
- **skill**: angles

### Q04
- **stem**: A rectangle has…
- **options**:
  - A) 3 sides
  - B) 4 equal sides only
  - C) 4 sides with opposite sides equal
  - D) 5 sides
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A rectangle has 4 sides; opposite sides are equal and all angles are right angles.
- **skill**: perimeter

### Q05
- **stem**: Which of these is a closed shape?
- **options**:
  - A) A ray
  - B) A line segment
  - C) A square
  - D) An open curve like a C
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A square joins end to end with no gap, so it is closed.
- **skill**: polygons

### Q06
- **stem**: A line that goes on forever in both directions is a…
- **options**:
  - A) Ray
  - B) Line segment
  - C) Line
  - D) Angle
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A line has no end points; it extends both ways.
- **skill**: solid-faces

### Q07
- **stem**: A ray has…
- **options**:
  - A) Two end points
  - B) One end point and goes forever one way
  - C) No end points
  - D) Three end points
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A ray starts at a point and goes on forever in one direction.
- **skill**: shapes

### Q08
- **stem**: An angle is formed by…
- **options**:
  - A) One straight line only
  - B) Two rays sharing a common end point
  - C) Three circles
  - D) Four parallel lines
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Two rays (arms) with a common end point (vertex) make an angle.
- **skill**: lines

### Q09
- **stem**: A right angle measures…
- **options**:
  - A) 45°
  - B) 90°
  - C) 180°
  - D) 360°
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A right angle is exactly 90° — like a square corner.
- **skill**: angles

### Q10
- **stem**: Which angle is smaller than a right angle?
- **options**:
  - A) Obtuse angle
  - B) Straight angle
  - C) Acute angle
  - D) Complete angle
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: An acute angle is less than 90°.
- **skill**: perimeter

### Q11
- **stem**: An obtuse angle is…
- **options**:
  - A) Less than 90°
  - B) Exactly 90°
  - C) Between 90° and 180°
  - D) Exactly 180°
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Obtuse angles are greater than 90° but less than 180°.
- **skill**: polygons

### Q12
- **stem**: A square has a perimeter of 20 cm. What is the length of one side?
- **options**:
  - A) 4 cm
  - B) 5 cm
  - C) 10 cm
  - D) 20 cm
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Perimeter = 4 × side, so side = 20 ÷ 4 = 5 cm.
- **skill**: solid-faces

### Q13
- **stem**: Perimeter means…
- **options**:
  - A) The space inside a shape
  - B) The distance around a shape
  - C) The number of diagonals
  - D) The centre point
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Perimeter is the total length of the boundary around a shape.
- **skill**: shapes

### Q14
- **stem**: A rectangular garden is 8 m long and 5 m wide. What is its perimeter?
- **options**:
  - A) 13 m
  - B) 26 m
  - C) 40 m
  - D) 18 m
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Perimeter = 2 × (length + breadth) = 2 × (8 + 5) = 26 m.
- **skill**: lines

### Q15

- **options**:

- **explanation**: Rectangles (and squares) have four right angles.
- **skill**: angles

### Q16
- **stem**: Parallel lines…
- **options**:
  - A) Meet at one point
  - B) Meet at two points
  - C) Never meet and stay the same distance apart
  - D) Form a triangle
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Parallel lines never meet and keep a constant distance.
- **skill**: perimeter

### Q17
