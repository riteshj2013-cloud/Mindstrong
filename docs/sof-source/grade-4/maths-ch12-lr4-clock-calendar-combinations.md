## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: The Clock Face as a Turn
- **tts**:
  - The 12 numbers split a full turn of 360 degrees into 12 equal gaps, so each gap is 30 degrees.
  - The minute hand goes all the way round in 60 minutes, so it turns 6 degrees every minute and one gap every 5 minutes.
- **on_screen**: A clock face lights up its 12 gaps one by one while a counter adds 30 degrees each time.
- **check**: How many degrees does the minute hand turn in 15 minutes? — Answer: 90 degrees (a right angle)

### step_2: The Hour Hand Moves Too
- **tts**:
  - The hour hand moves one gap, 30 degrees, every hour, which is half a degree every minute.
  - At half past an hour the hour hand is halfway between two numbers, not on a number.
- **on_screen**: The minute hand sweeps round once while the short hour hand creeps from 2 to 3.
- **check**: At half past 6, where is the hour hand? — Answer: Halfway between 6 and 7

### step_3: Angles Between the Hands
- **tts**:
  - Find how far each hand is from 12, going clockwise, and take the smaller from the larger.
  - At 3 o'clock and at 9 o'clock the hands make a right angle; at 6 o'clock they make a straight line.
- **on_screen**: Two hands spin to a time and the part between them fills with colour as its size is read out.
- **check**: What is the angle between the hands at 4 o'clock? — Answer: 120 degrees

### step_4: Days of the Week Repeat
- **tts**:
  - The days of the week repeat every 7 days, so the same weekday comes back 7, 14, 21 or 28 days later.
  - To jump many days, take away whole weeks and move on only by the days left over.
- **on_screen**: A strip of seven day names rolls forward; whole weeks fade away and the leftover days step on.
- **check**: If today is a Monday, what day is it 15 days later? — Answer: Tuesday

### step_5: Months and Leap Years
- **tts**:
  - Months have 30 or 31 days, and February has 28 days, or 29 in a leap year.
  - Leap years come every 4 years, such as 2024 and 2028. A 31-day month has three weekdays that come five times.
- **on_screen**: A calendar page flips month by month and February grows an extra day in a leap year.
- **check**: How many days does February 2028 have? — Answer: 29

### step_6: Counting Choices
- **tts**:
  - If you choose one thing from each group, multiply: 3 shirts and 2 pairs of shorts make 3 × 2 outfits.
  - If you choose from one group or another but not both, add the two counts.
- **on_screen**: Shirt and shorts icons pair up with lines, and each new line adds one outfit to the count.
- **check**: How many ways can you choose one of 4 fruits and one of 2 juices? — Answer: 8

### step_7: Listing Carefully
- **tts**:
  - Make an ordered list so that nothing is missed and nothing is counted twice, for example for handshakes or orders in a row.
  - A handshake is shared by two people, but a card sent from one friend to another is not; and a number cannot start with 0.
- **on_screen**: Names in a ring are joined by lines one at a time, and each line is ticked off a list.
- **check**: How many handshakes are there among 3 friends? — Answer: 3

### Q02
- **id**: g4-maths-ch12-a-02
- **stem**: Rudra watches the minute hand of the station clock. How long does the minute hand take to turn through a straight angle?
- **options**:
  - A) 30 minutes
  - B) 15 minutes
  - C) 60 minutes
  - D) 45 minutes
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: The minute hand makes a full turn (360°) in 60 minutes. A straight angle is half a turn (180°), so it takes half of 60 minutes, which is 30 minutes. 60 minutes is tempting, but in 60 minutes the hand goes all the way round, which is two straight angles.
- **hint**: A straight angle is half of a full turn. Think how long the minute hand needs to go all the way round the station clock.
- **skill**: minute_hand_straight_angle_time
- **difficulty**: easy
- **type**: logical
- **pictorial**: no

### Q03
- **id**: g4-maths-ch12-a-03
- **stem**: Aarohi's dance class starts at half past 4. At that moment, where does the hour hand of a clock point?
- **options**:
  - A) exactly at 4
  - B) exactly at 5
  - C) halfway between 3 and 4

- **answer**: D

- **hint**: The hour hand moves steadily from one number to the next during an hour. How far along has it gone when Aarohi's class starts?
