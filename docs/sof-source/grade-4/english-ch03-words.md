## Interactive lesson outline
- L1: Hello, word gardener! Today we're going to grow some wonderful words.
- L2: Some words are opposites, like hot and cold. Some mean almost the same, like big and large.
- L3: Rhyming words end with the same sound. Cat, hat, mat. Can you hear it?
- L4: Homophones sound the same but mean different things. Sun in the sky, and son, a boy in a family.
- L5: We can also join two words to make a new one. Rain plus bow makes rainbow!
- L6: (optional) Little parts change a word's meaning too. "Un" means not, so unhappy means not happy. "Ful" means full of, so helpful means full of help.
- L7: Let's try one together. Listen: "The puppy was fearless. It ran straight into the big puddle!"
- L8: "Fearless" is "fear" plus "less." "Less" means without. So fearless means without fear.
- L9: And the clue? The puppy ran right in. It wasn't scared at all!
- L10: Don't forget the punctuation helpers: capital letters, full stops, question marks and commas.
- L11: (optional) And kind words, like please, thank you, sorry and excuse me, make everyone smile.
- L12: Now it's your turn. Read each question carefully and pick the best word.
- L13: If two answers look alike, say them out loud and listen.
- L14: Wonderful work, word gardener! Your word garden is blooming beautifully.

## Passage bank (for quizzes)

### P1 — Rain on the Roof (original poem)
Pitter-patter on the roof,
The rain is here, I have the proof!
The frogs say croak, the peacocks cry,
Grey clouds are sailing in the sky.

I float my boat of paper white,
It wobbles left, it wobbles right.
Amma calls, "Come in, it's late!"
But puddles, puddles cannot wait!

### P2 — At the Library (dialogue)
**Librarian:** Good morning, Tara. How can I help you?
**Tara:** Good morning, ma'am. May I borrow a book about stars, please?
**Librarian:** Of course. Here is one with lots of pictures.
**Tara:** Thank you! When should I return it?
**Librarian:** Please bring it back next Monday.
**Tara:** I will. Sorry, ma'am, I returned my last book late.
**Librarian:** That's all right. Thank you for telling me.
**Tara:** Thank you, ma'am. Have a nice day!

### P3 — Bunty's Birthday
It was Bunty's birthday. In the morning, he saw a big box on the table. Inside was a red football! Bunty was very thankful. He ran to the playground with his friends. They played until sunset. On the way home, Bunty saw a little puppy sitting alone. It looked hungry and helpless. Bunty gave it some of his birthday cake. The puppy wagged its tail happily. "This is the best birthday ever," said Bunty.
