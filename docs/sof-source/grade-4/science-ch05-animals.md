## Learning objectives
- I can name habitats and match animals to suitable homes.
- I can sort animals as herbivores, carnivores or omnivores.
- I can explain simple adaptations such as gills, wings, fur, humps and camouflage.
- I can order stages in butterfly, frog and hen life cycles.
- I can read a short food chain: plant → plant eater → animal eater.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Where do they live?
- **Visual:** Four habitat cards: desert, forest, pond and icy shore. Animals wait to be placed.
- **TTS:**
  - "A habitat is an animal's natural home."
  - "Deserts are hot and dry. Forests have many trees."
  - "Ponds and oceans are water habitats."
  - "Polar places are cold, so animals need thick fur or fat."
- **Interaction:** Drag camel, deer, frog and penguin onto the matching habitat card.

### Scene 2: What do they eat?
- **Visual:** Three baskets labelled Herbivore, Carnivore and Omnivore with food icons.
- **TTS:**
  - "Herbivores eat plants."
  - "Carnivores eat other animals."
  - "Omnivores eat both."
  - "Teeth, beaks and claws give clues about food."
- **Interaction:** Sort cow, tiger and crow into the three baskets. Bonus: match flat teeth to grass.

### Scene 3: Body tricks for survival
- **Visual:** Close-ups: camel hump, fish gills, bird wings, white polar fur, duck webbed feet.
- **TTS:**
  - "Adaptations help animals survive."
  - "Gills take oxygen from water."
  - "Wings help birds fly. Webbed feet help ducks swim."
  - "Camouflage helps animals hide."
- **Interaction:** Match each adaptation card to the job it does: breathe, swim, stay warm, hide, store energy.

### Scene 4: Growing up stages
- **Visual:** Butterfly cycle and frog cycle on two spinning wheels; a hen with eggs and chicks beside them.
- **TTS:**
  - "A life cycle shows how an animal grows."
  - "Butterfly: egg, caterpillar, pupa, adult."
  - "Frog: egg, tadpole, then frog."
  - "Hens lay eggs; chicks hatch and grow."
- **Interaction:** Put the butterfly and frog stages in order. Tap the hen cycle to hear each step.

## Quiz Set A — 24 MCQs

### A1
**Stem:** A habitat is ______.
- A) The natural place where an animal lives
- B) Only a zoo cage
- C) A type of food
- D) A kind of bone

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A habitat is an animal's natural home, such as a forest, desert, pond or ocean.

### A2
**Stem:** Which animal is best suited to living in water most of the time?
- A) Camel
- B) Fish
- C) Eagle
- D) Camel cricket

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Fish have gills and fins suited to living in water. Camels are desert animals; eagles fly in air.

### A3
**Stem:** Animals that eat only plants are called ______.
- A) Carnivores
- B) Omnivores
- C) Herbivores
- D) Producers

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Herbivores eat plants. Cows, deer and goats are herbivores.

### A4
**Stem:** A tiger mainly eats other animals. A tiger is a ______.
- A) Herbivore
- B) Producer
- C) Seed
- D) Carnivore

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Carnivores eat other animals. Tigers, lions and eagles are carnivores.

### A5
**Stem:** A crow eats grains and also insects or leftover food. A crow is an ______.
- A) Omnivore
- B) Herbivore only
- C) Carnivore only
- D) Producer

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Omnivores eat both plant and animal food. Crows, bears and humans are omnivores.

### A6
**Stem:** Which adaptation helps a camel live in the desert?
- A) Gills for breathing underwater
- B) Storing fat in its hump and going long without water
- C) Bright feathers for swimming
- D) Webbed feet for ice skating

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Camels store fat in the hump and can travel long distances with little water in hot deserts.

### A7
**Stem:** Polar bears have thick fur and a layer of fat. This mainly helps them ______.
- A) Stay cool in a desert
- B) Fly between trees
- C) Stay warm in a cold habitat
- D) Breathe underwater forever

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Thick fur and fat keep polar bears warm in icy polar habitats.

### A8
**Stem:** A frog can live both in water and on land. Such animals are called ______.
- A) Only fish
- B) Only birds
- C) Insects with six wings
- D) Amphibians

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Amphibians such as frogs can live in water and on land at different times of life.

### A9
**Stem:** Which is the correct order in a simple butterfly life cycle?
- A) Egg → larva (caterpillar) → pupa → adult butterfly
- B) Adult → egg → pupa → larva
- C) Pupa → adult → egg → rock
- D) Larva → adult → egg → pupa → seed

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A butterfly grows from egg to larva (caterpillar), then pupa (chrysalis), then adult butterfly.

### A10
**Stem:** A tadpole grows into a ______.
- A) Butterfly
- B) Frog
- C) Hen
- D) Snake egg

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Frogs lay eggs in water. Tadpoles hatch and slowly change into frogs.

### A11
**Stem:** Which habitat is hot and dry, with little rainfall?
- A) Rainforest
- B) Pond
- C) Desert
- D) Ocean deep

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Deserts are hot and dry with very little rain. Camels and cactus are desert survivors.

### A12
**Stem:** Deer live in forests and eat leaves and grass. Their habitat and food match because ______.
- A) Forests have no plants
- B) Deer eat only fish
- C) Deer need icebergs
- D) Forests provide plants to eat and cover to hide

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Forest plants give deer food, and trees and bushes help them hide from predators.

### A13
**Stem:** Fish breathe underwater using ______.
- A) Gills
- B) Lungs like ours only
- C) Feathers
- D) Humps

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Gills take oxygen dissolved in water so fish can breathe underwater.

### A14
**Stem:** A bird's wings are an adaptation mainly for ______.
- A) Digging deep burrows only
- B) Flying
- C) Storing desert water
- D) Chewing grass like a cow

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Wings help most birds fly, which helps them find food, escape danger and migrate.

### A15
**Stem:** Which animal has a long sticky tongue useful for catching insects?
- A) Cow
- B) Fish with no tongue use
- C) Frog or chameleon (insect catchers)
- D) Camel hump

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Frogs and chameleons use long sticky tongues to catch insects quickly.

### A16
**Stem:** In a simple food chain: grass → rabbit → fox. The rabbit is a ______.
- A) Producer
- B) Only a carnivore
- C) Non-living thing
- D) Herbivore (plant eater)

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** The rabbit eats grass, so it is a herbivore. The fox that eats the rabbit is a carnivore.

### A17
**Stem:** Which animal is adapted to life in trees with a long tail for balance?
- A) Monkey
- B) Fish
- C) Earthworm
- D) Camel

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Many monkeys live in trees. A long tail helps with balance while climbing and jumping.

### A18
**Stem:** A hen's life cycle includes eggs. Chicks hatch from eggs. This means hens are ______.
- A) Animals that never have young
- B) Animals that lay eggs (oviparous birds)
- C) Plants
- D) Only underwater animals

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Birds like hens lay eggs. The chick develops inside the egg and then hatches.

### A19
**Stem:** Webbed feet help a duck mainly to ______.
- A) Run on desert sand only
- B) Dig metal
- C) Swim in water
- D) Make honey

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Webbed feet act like paddles and help ducks swim well in water.

### A20
**Stem:** Which of these is a nocturnal animal (active mainly at night)?
- A) Crow at noon only
- B) Butterfly in bright sun only
- C) Hen in the afternoon only
- D) Owl

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Owls are mostly nocturnal. They hunt at night and have eyes and ears suited to low light.

### A21
**Stem:** Thick scales on a crocodile help it ______.
- A) Protect its body and live well in watery habitats
- B) Fly long distances
- C) Make milk
- D) Photosynthesise

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Tough scales protect the crocodile's body as it lives in and near water.

### A22
**Stem:** Which animal would you most expect in a freshwater pond habitat?
- A) Polar bear
- B) Frog
- C) Camel
- D) Penguin on ice only

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Frogs and many insects, fish and plants live in or near freshwater ponds.

### A23
**Stem:** An adaptation is ______.
- A) A random toy
- B) Only a human invention
- C) A body feature or behaviour that helps an animal survive in its habitat
- D) A type of rock

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Adaptations are features or behaviours that help living things survive where they live.

### A24
**Stem:** Why do many desert animals stay in burrows during the hot day?
- A) To find ice cream
- B) To grow gills
- C) To become plants
- D) To escape the extreme heat

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Burrows stay cooler. Staying underground by day helps desert animals avoid deadly heat.

## Quiz Set B — 24 MCQs

### B1
**Stem:** Which habitat has many tall trees, shade and high rainfall?
- A) Forest (especially rainforest or dense forest)
- B) Desert
- C) Icy polar desert only
- D) Classroom

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Forests have many trees. Rainforests are wet and full of plants and animals.

### B2
**Stem:** A lion lives mainly in grasslands or forests and hunts other animals. Its food habit is ______.
- A) Herbivore
- B) Carnivore
- C) Producer
- D) Only nectar feeder

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Lions are carnivores. They hunt and eat other animals.

### B3
**Stem:** Which animal is an herbivore?
- A) Eagle
- B) Shark
- C) Goat
- D) Tiger

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Goats eat plants such as grass and leaves, so they are herbivores.

### B4
**Stem:** Sharp claws and strong beaks in eagles are adaptations for ______.
- A) Eating only grass
- B) Making honey
- C) Breathing underwater
- D) Catching and tearing animal food

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Eagles are carnivores. Sharp claws and hooked beaks help them catch and tear meat.

### B5
**Stem:** A penguin's flipper-like wings and waterproof feathers help it ______.
- A) Swim and stay warm in cold ocean habitats
- B) Fly like an eagle over deserts
- C) Climb tall mango trees
- D) Store water in a hump

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Penguins are adapted for cold seas. Flippers help them swim; feathers help keep water out and stay warm.

### B6
**Stem:** In the butterfly life cycle, the caterpillar is the ______ stage.
- A) Egg
- B) Larva
- C) Adult only
- D) Seed

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The caterpillar is the larva stage. It eats a lot before becoming a pupa.

### B7
**Stem:** During metamorphosis, a tadpole grows ______.
- A) Wings first like a bird
- B) A camel hump
- C) Legs and lungs as it becomes a frog
- D) Feathers

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Tadpoles live in water and slowly grow legs; lungs develop as they become frogs that can live on land.

### B8
**Stem:** Which food chain is in a sensible order?
- A) Tiger → grass → deer
- B) Deer → grass → tiger
- C) Tiger → deer → grass as food for tiger first
- D) Grass → deer → tiger

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Producers (grass) are eaten by herbivores (deer), which may be eaten by carnivores (tiger).

### B9
**Stem:** Hollow bones and light bodies help many birds to ______.
- A) Fly more easily
- B) Sink quickly in mud
- C) Store months of desert water
- D) Dig coal mines

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Light, hollow bones make flying easier for many birds.

### B10
**Stem:** Which animal uses echolocation (sound echoes) to find its way in the dark?
- A) Cow
- B) Bat
- C) Goldfish in a bowl only
- D) Butterfly

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Bats send out sounds and listen to echoes to find objects and prey in the dark.

### B11
**Stem:** A fish's streamlined body shape helps it ______.
- A) Walk on land easily
- B) Fly between clouds
- C) Move smoothly through water
- D) Store fat in a hump

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A pointed, smooth shape reduces water resistance so fish can swim efficiently.

### B12
**Stem:** Animals that are active during the day are called ______.
- A) Nocturnal
- B) Amphibians only
- C) Producers
- D) Diurnal

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Diurnal animals are active by day. Nocturnal animals are active at night.

### B13
**Stem:** Which feature helps a polar animal hide in snow?
- A) White fur that blends with snow
- B) Bright red colour always
- C) Green leaves on its back
- D) Metal shell

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** White fur is camouflage in snowy habitats, helping animals hide from prey or predators.

### B14
**Stem:** Cows have flat teeth mainly to ______.
- A) Tear meat like a tiger
- B) Grind plant food
- C) Catch fish
- D) Drill wood

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Flat grinding teeth suit herbivores that chew grass and other plants.

### B15
**Stem:** A life cycle shows ______.
- A) Only one day in an animal's life
- B) Only the habitat map
- C) The stages an animal passes through as it grows and reproduces
- D) A food menu only

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A life cycle includes stages from young to adult and often how the next generation begins.

### B16
**Stem:** Which animal is best matched to a burrow habitat under the ground?
- A) Eagle nesting only on cliffs
- B) Shark
- C) Penguin on open ice only
- D) Rabbit

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Rabbits often live in burrows that shelter them from weather and predators.

### B17
**Stem:** Gills : fish :: lungs : ______.
- A) Human (and many land animals)
- B) Shark only
- C) Leaf
- D) Rock

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Fish use gills in water; humans and many land animals use lungs to breathe air.

### B18
**Stem:** Why do birds build nests?
- A) To store cars
- B) To lay eggs and raise chicks safely
- C) To photosynthesise
- D) To hunt whales

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Nests give a safe place for eggs and young chicks.

### B19
**Stem:** A chameleon changing colour is mainly useful for ______.
- A) Making food from sunlight
- B) Flying to the moon
- C) Camouflage and signalling in its habitat
- D) Breathing underwater only

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Colour change can help a chameleon blend in (camouflage) and communicate.

### B20
**Stem:** Which animal group typically has six legs?
- A) Birds
- B) Fish
- C) Snakes
- D) Insects

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Adult insects typically have six legs. Spiders have eight and are not insects.

### B21
**Stem:** Migrating birds travel long distances mainly to ______.
- A) Find better weather and food at different times of year
- B) Become fish
- C) Lose their wings
- D) Turn into eggs permanently

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Migration helps birds find warmer places and more food when seasons change.

### B22
**Stem:** Which is an aquatic habitat?
- A) Desert dune
- B) Ocean or river
- C) Classroom desk
- D) Mountain cave with no water

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Aquatic habitats are water habitats such as oceans, rivers, lakes and ponds.

### B23
**Stem:** A thick fat layer under a seal's skin is an adaptation to ______.
- A) Keep cool in a hot desert
- B) Help it climb trees
- C) Keep warm in cold water
- D) Help it eat only leaves

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Blubber (fat) insulates seals so they stay warm in cold seas.

### B24
**Stem:** Humans are omnivores because we ______.
- A) Eat only grass
- B) Never eat plants
- C) Breathe only with gills
- D) Can eat both plant foods and animal foods

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** An Indian thali may include rice and vegetables (plants) and also curd, egg or fish (animal foods).
