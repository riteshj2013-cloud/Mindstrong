# Chapter 1: Food and Nutrition

## Meta
- Grade: 4
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: food, nutrition, nutrients, balanced diet, vitamins, proteins, carbohydrates, fats, fibre, water
- Source basis: NCERT Class 4 EVS / typical NSO themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 4 (clear, high visual/TTS)

## Learning objectives
- I can explain why we need food: for energy, growth, repair and fighting illness.
- I can sort foods into energy-giving, body-building and protective groups.
- I can name foods rich in carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals and fibre.
- I can tell why water and fibre are important for my body.
- I can build a balanced Indian meal plate.
- I can make smart choices about junk food and keep my food clean and safe.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Why Do We Eat?
- **Visual:** A cheerful girl, Tara, on a bright playground. Four glowing bubbles float above her: a lightning bolt (energy), a growing ruler (growth), a bandage (repair) and a shield (fighting illness). A big steel thali sits on a mat nearby.
- **TTS:**
  - "Hi! I am Tara. Let us find out why we eat food."
  - "Food gives us energy to run, play and think."
  - "Food helps us grow taller and stronger every year."
  - "Food repairs our body when we get a cut or a scrape."
  - "Good food also helps our body fight germs and illness."
  - "The useful parts of food are called nutrients."
- **Interaction:** Tap each glowing bubble to hear what food does. Then drag four action pictures (running, growing, healing knee, sneezing child getting better) to the matching bubble.

### Scene 2: Go Foods and Grow Foods
- **Visual:** Two colourful shelves in a kitchen. The yellow "Go" shelf has rice, roti, potato, sugar, honey and banana. The red "Grow" shelf has dal, milk, egg, fish, paneer and a bowl of sprouts. Tara's muscles puff up playfully near the Grow shelf.
- **TTS:**
  - "Carbohydrates are energy-giving foods. Think of them as go foods."
  - "Rice, roti, potato, sugar and honey give us lots of energy."
  - "Proteins are body-building foods. Think of them as grow foods."
  - "Dal, milk, egg, fish, paneer and sprouts are full of protein."
  - "Proteins build muscles and help repair our body."
  - "A drop of iodine turns starchy food like potato blue-black."
- **Interaction:** Sort game. Food cards fall one by one. Drag each card to the Go shelf or the Grow shelf. Bonus: tap a potato slice to add an iodine drop and watch it turn blue-black.

### Scene 3: Protective Foods and Little Bit of Fat
- **Visual:** A rainbow fruit-and-vegetable market: orange, amla, guava, carrot, spinach, papaya, tomato. Each item wears a tiny superhero cape. In a small corner, a little bowl of ghee, butter and oil with a "small amounts" tag.
- **TTS:**
  - "Fruits and vegetables are protective foods. They keep us healthy."
  - "They give us vitamins and minerals."
  - "Carrots and papaya have vitamin A. It is good for our eyes."
  - "Oranges, amla and guava have vitamin C. It keeps gums healthy."
  - "Milk gives calcium for strong bones and teeth."
  - "Spinach and jaggery give iron for healthy blood."
  - "Sunlight helps our skin make vitamin D."
  - "Ghee, butter and oil give fats. We need only small amounts."
- **Interaction:** Match game. Drag each "superhero food" to the body part it helps: eyes (carrot), gums (amla), bones (milk), blood (spinach). Then use a slider to pour ghee on a roti. A gentle beep says "Just a little is enough!" when too much is added.

### Scene 4: Fibre and Water Helpers
- **Visual:** A friendly cartoon tummy with a smiling face. Leafy vegetables, whole fruits, whole wheat roti and sprouts slide gently through a slide inside it. A tall glass of water and a coconut with a straw stand beside it. A water meter on Tara's body shows about two-thirds full.
- **TTS:**
  - "Fibre is also called roughage."
  - "Fibre helps food move smoothly through our tummy."
  - "Whole fruits, leafy vegetables and whole grains have lots of fibre."
  - "About two-thirds of our body is made of water."
  - "We lose water when we sweat, so we must drink more."
  - "Water and coconut water are the best drinks after play."
- **Interaction:** Tap foods to feed the tummy. High-fibre foods make the slide run smoothly; low-fibre foods make it slow. Then fill Tara's water glass by tapping, after a sunny football game.

### Scene 5: My Balanced Thali
- **Visual:** An empty steel thali with sections. Around it: roti, rice, dal, rajma, paneer sabzi, mixed vegetables, curd, salad, a fruit, and a small spoon of ghee. On the side, a "sometimes" box with chips, cola and jalebi, shown smiling but small.
- **TTS:**
  - "A balanced diet has all nutrients in the right amounts."
  - "Let us build a balanced thali together."
  - "Add roti or rice for energy."
  - "Add dal, curd or paneer to help you grow."
  - "Add sabzi, salad and fruit to stay protected."
  - "Chips and cola are sometimes foods, not everyday foods."
  - "Always wash your hands with soap before you eat."
- **Interaction:** Drag foods to fill the thali. A star lights up for each group added: Go, Grow, Protect, Water. When all stars glow, Tara cheers. Placing three "sometimes" foods shows a kind tip: "Tasty! But try a fruit instead today."

## Quiz Set A — 24 MCQs

### A1 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which nutrient mainly gives our body energy?
- A) Proteins
- B) Carbohydrates
- C) Vitamins
- D) Minerals

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Carbohydrates are energy-giving foods. Rice, roti and potato are full of them.

### A2 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which of these is a body-building food?
- A) Sugar
- B) Rice
- C) Butter
- D) Dal

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Dal is rich in protein. Proteins help our body grow and repair itself.

### A3 (Recall)
**Stem:** Oranges, amla and lemons are rich in which vitamin?
- A) Vitamin C
- B) Vitamin D
- C) Vitamin A
- D) Vitamin K

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Citrus fruits and amla are great sources of vitamin C. It keeps our gums healthy.

### A4 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which part of food helps it move smoothly through our stomach and intestines?
- A) Fat
- B) Protein
- C) Fibre
- D) Sugar

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Fibre, also called roughage, helps food move along and keeps our tummy happy.

### A5 (Recall)
**Stem:** Our skin can make which vitamin with the help of sunlight?
- A) Vitamin A
- B) Vitamin C
- C) Vitamin D
- D) Vitamin B

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** When gentle sunlight falls on our skin, the body makes vitamin D for strong bones.

### A6 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which mineral helps make our bones and teeth strong?
- A) Calcium
- B) Iodine
- C) Iron
- D) Vitamin C

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Calcium builds strong bones and teeth. Milk, curd and paneer have lots of it.

### A7 (Recall)
**Stem:** Fruits and vegetables are called which kind of foods?
- A) Energy-giving foods
- B) Body-building foods
- C) Fatty foods
- D) Protective foods

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Fruits and vegetables give vitamins and minerals. These protect us from illness.

### A8 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which of these foods is rich in fat?
- A) Cucumber
- B) Ghee
- C) Spinach
- D) Watermelon

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ghee is a fat. Fats give energy, but we need only small amounts.

### A9 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which food is mostly carbohydrate?
- A) Potato
- B) Egg
- C) Fish
- D) Paneer

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Potato is full of starch, a carbohydrate. Egg, fish and paneer are rich in protein.

### A10 (Recall)
**Stem:** About how much of our body is made of water?
- A) One-tenth
- B) One-quarter
- C) About two-thirds
- D) All of it

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** About two-thirds of our body is water. That is why drinking water every day matters.

### A11 (Apply)
**Stem:** Riya feels tired in the middle of a football match. Which snack will give her energy quickly?
- A) A cucumber slice
- B) A banana
- C) A pinch of salt
- D) A glass of plain water

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A banana has natural sugars, which are carbohydrates. They give quick energy.

### A12 (Apply)
**Stem:** Kabir's gums bleed when he brushes. Which food should he eat more of?
- A) Butter
- B) White rice
- C) Sugar
- D) Guava and lemon

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Bleeding gums can mean too little vitamin C. Guava and lemon are rich in vitamin C.

### A13 (Apply)
**Stem:** Arjun's mother adds moong sprouts to his breakfast to help him grow. Sprouts mainly give him:
- A) Fats
- B) Sugar
- C) Iodine
- D) Proteins

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Sprouts are rich in protein, the body-building nutrient. They help children grow.

### A14 (Apply)
**Stem:** Which lunch plate is balanced?
- A) Rice, potato and roti
- B) Roti, dal, sabzi, curd and salad
- C) Puri, jalebi and chips
- D) Only apples

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** This plate has energy food, body-building food and protective food. That makes it balanced.

### A15 (Apply)
**Stem:** Rohan often finds it hard to pass stool. What should he add to his meals?
- A) More biscuits
- B) More fried chips
- C) Whole fruits, leafy vegetables and water
- D) More sweets

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Fibre and water help food move smoothly. Fruits and leafy vegetables are full of fibre.

### A16 (Apply)
**Stem:** Meena cannot see well in dim light at night. Which food can help her eyes?
- A) Carrot
- B) White bread
- C) Toffee
- D) Cola

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Trouble seeing in dim light can mean too little vitamin A. Carrots are rich in vitamin A.

### A17 (Apply)
**Stem:** Which kind of salt helps prevent swelling in the neck called goitre?
- A) Sugar
- B) Iodised salt
- C) Baking soda
- D) Chilli powder

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Iodised salt has iodine. Our body needs a little iodine to stay healthy.

### A18 (Apply)
**Stem:** After playing in the hot sun, Dev is very thirsty. What is the best drink for him?
- A) Cola
- B) Packed sweet juice
- C) Tea
- D) Water or coconut water

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Our body loses water as sweat. Water or coconut water puts it back in a healthy way.

### A19 (Apply)
**Stem:** Asha puts a drop of iodine on a raw potato slice. What colour will the spot become?
- A) Blue-black
- B) Red
- C) Green
- D) It stays yellow

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Iodine turns blue-black when starch is present. Potato is full of starch.

### A20 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Fats give us lots of energy. So why should we eat only small amounts of them?
- A) Fats have no use at all
- B) Fats are only for grown-ups
- C) Extra fat gets stored in the body and can harm the heart
- D) Fats make our bones weak

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A little fat is useful. Too much gets stored in the body and is not good for the heart.

### A21 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Raju eats only rice and potatoes every day. What problem might he face?
- A) He will get too little energy
- B) He will get too much protein
- C) He may not get enough proteins, vitamins and minerals
- D) He will get too much fibre

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Rice and potato give energy, but not enough of the other nutrients. Raju needs a balanced diet.

### A22 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Why should we wash vegetables before cutting them, not after?
- A) Some vitamins and minerals wash away from cut pieces
- B) Washing first makes vegetables sweeter
- C) Washing first makes vegetables grow bigger
- D) Washing first changes their colour

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Some vitamins and minerals dissolve in water. Cut pieces lose them faster when washed.

### A23 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Which pair has one energy-giving food and one protective food?
- A) Dal and egg
- B) Rice and roti
- C) Milk and paneer
- D) Roti and spinach

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Roti gives energy. Spinach is a protective leafy vegetable. The other pairs are from the same group.

### A24 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Meera loves chips. What is the wisest advice for her?
- A) Eat chips at every meal
- B) Enjoy chips once in a while and eat home food most days
- C) Drink cola instead of water
- D) Skip breakfast to eat more chips later

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Chips have lots of salt and oil but few nutrients. A small treat sometimes is fine.

## Quiz Set B — 24 DIFFERENT MCQs

### B1 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which of these protein foods comes from an animal?
- A) Rice
- B) Wheat
- C) Fish
- D) Carrot

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Fish comes from an animal and is rich in protein. Rice, wheat and carrot come from plants.

### B2 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which nutrient helps build muscles and repair our body?
- A) Proteins
- B) Fats
- C) Fibre
- D) Water

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Proteins are body-building nutrients. They help us grow and heal.

### B3 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which foods are good sources of iron, which keeps our blood healthy?
- A) Sugar and toffee
- B) Butter and cream
- C) Salt and pepper
- D) Spinach and jaggery

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Leafy greens like spinach and jaggery give iron. Iron helps our blood carry oxygen.

### B4 (Recall)
**Stem:** What is another name for fibre?
- A) Starch
- B) Roughage
- C) Protein
- D) Vitamin

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Fibre is also called roughage. It helps our tummy work well.

### B5 (Recall)
**Stem:** Honey and sugar are mainly which nutrient?
- A) Carbohydrates
- B) Proteins
- C) Fats
- D) Fibre

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Sugars are a kind of carbohydrate. They give energy, but we should not eat too much.

### B6 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which of these is a pulse?
- A) Mango
- B) Cabbage
- C) Rice
- D) Moong

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Moong is a pulse. Pulses like moong, chana and masoor are rich in protein.

### B7 (Recall)
**Stem:** A diet that has all nutrients in the right amounts is called a:
- A) Junk diet
- B) Fast diet
- C) Balanced diet
- D) Sweet diet

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A balanced diet gives our body everything it needs in the right amounts.

### B8 (Recall)
**Stem:** Vitamin A is especially good for our:
- A) Hair colour
- B) Eyes
- C) Ears
- D) Nails

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Vitamin A keeps our eyes healthy. Carrots, papaya and mangoes have it.

### B9 (Recall)
**Stem:** Too little vitamin D in children can cause soft, bent bones. This is called:
- A) Goitre
- B) Scurvy
- C) Anaemia
- D) Rickets

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Rickets happens when bones do not get enough vitamin D. Sunlight and milk help.

### B10 (Recall)
**Stem:** Which of these is rich in oil, a kind of fat?
- A) Groundnuts
- B) Tomato
- C) Lemon
- D) Lettuce

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Groundnuts are full of oil. That is why groundnut oil is made from them.

### B11 (Apply)
**Stem:** Sonu's plate has only roti and rice. What should he add to make it balanced?
- A) More rice
- B) A jalebi
- C) Dal, sabzi and curd
- D) A packet of chips

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Dal and curd add protein. Sabzi adds vitamins, minerals and fibre.

### B12 (Apply)
**Stem:** Mangoes, guavas and papayas belong to which food group?
- A) Body-building foods
- B) Protective foods
- C) Fatty foods
- D) Junk foods

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Fruits give vitamins and minerals. These protect our body from illness.

### B13 (Apply)
**Stem:** Lata often feels tired and looks pale. The doctor says she needs more iron. What should she eat?
- A) Cola
- B) Leafy greens, jaggery and dates
- C) Chips
- D) Toffees

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Leafy greens, jaggery and dates give iron. Iron helps her blood and energy.

### B14 (Apply)
**Stem:** Priya does not eat eggs. Which food can give her protein instead?
- A) Cucumber
- B) Apple
- C) Paneer
- D) Rice

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Paneer is made from milk and is rich in protein. It is a great choice for vegetarians.

### B15 (Apply)
**Stem:** Dal was cooked at noon. In which case is it most likely to spoil by night?
- A) Left open on a warm kitchen shelf
- B) Kept covered in the fridge
- C) Kept in the freezer
- D) Eaten hot at lunch

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Germs grow fast in warm places. Open dal on a warm shelf spoils quickly.

### B16 (Apply)
**Stem:** Mango pickle stays good for many months. What helps keep it from spoiling?
- A) Water
- B) Ice
- C) Rain
- D) Salt and oil

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Salt and oil stop germs from growing in pickles. This keeps them good for long.

### B17 (Apply)
**Stem:** Which tiffin box is the healthiest for school?
- A) Vegetable paratha, curd and a guava
- B) Noodles and cola
- C) Chips and biscuits
- D) A slice of cream cake

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** This tiffin has energy food, protein and a fruit. It keeps you strong all day.

### B18 (Apply)
**Stem:** What should you do just before eating?
- A) Play in the mud
- B) Eat with unwashed hands
- C) Wash your hands with soap
- D) Cough over the food

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Washing hands with soap removes germs. Clean hands keep our food safe.

### B19 (Apply)
**Stem:** Why should we not overcook vegetables?
- A) They become too green
- B) They grow bigger
- C) They become heavier
- D) Some vitamins are lost with too much heat

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Long cooking destroys some vitamins. Cook vegetables just enough, with a lid on.

### B20 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Who needs the most energy-giving food?
- A) A person sleeping
- B) A farmer ploughing a field all day
- C) A child watching TV
- D) A person sitting at a desk

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Hard physical work uses lots of energy. The farmer needs more energy food.

### B21 (Think harder)
**Stem:** A mango milkshake is made with milk and mango. What does it give us?
- A) Only fibre
- B) Only fat
- C) Only water
- D) Protein and calcium from milk, vitamins from mango

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Mixing foods mixes their nutrients. Milk gives protein and calcium. Mango gives vitamins.

### B22 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Why should we drink more water on a hot day?
- A) Our body loses water as sweat and needs it back
- B) Water makes us taller
- C) Water is full of protein
- D) Water can take the place of food

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** On hot days we sweat more. Drinking water replaces what our body loses.

### B23 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Which of these has the most fibre?
- A) White bread
- B) Whole wheat roti
- C) Sugar
- D) Butter

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Whole wheat keeps the outer bran layer, which is full of fibre. White bread loses most of it.

### B24 (Think harder)
**Stem:** Anu says, "Fruits give us no energy at all." What is correct?
- A) Anu is right
- B) Fruits give only fat
- C) Fruits give some energy from natural sugars, plus vitamins and fibre
- D) Fruits give only protein

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Fruits have natural sugars for energy. They also give vitamins, minerals and fibre.
