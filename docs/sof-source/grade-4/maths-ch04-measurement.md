## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Choose the unit
- **tts**:
  - Length, mass and capacity use different units.
  - Pick a unit that fits the size of what you measure.
- **on_screen**: Icons for pencil (cm), road (km), apple (g), milk (ml/l) light up when matched.
- **check**: Best unit for a pencil? — Answer: Centimetre

### step_2: Length ladder
- **tts**:
  - One metre is one hundred centimetres. One centimetre is ten millimetres.
  - One kilometre is one thousand metres.
- **on_screen**: Metric ladder animation: mm → cm → m → km.
- **check**: How many cm in 1 m? — Answer: 100

### step_3: Mass ladder
- **tts**:
  - One kilogram is one thousand grams.
  - A balance compares how heavy things are.
- **on_screen**: 1 kg weight splits into 1,000 g tiles; balance pans tip then level.
- **check**: How many grams in 1 kg? — Answer: 1,000

### step_4: Capacity ladder
- **tts**:
  - One litre is one thousand millilitres.
  - Bottles and cans show ml and l.
- **on_screen**: Jug fills from ml marks up to 1 litre.
- **check**: 1 litre = ? ml — Answer: 1,000

### step_5: Convert and combine
- **tts**:
  - Change to the same unit before you add or compare.
  - Regroup 100 cm as 1 m and 1,000 g as 1 kg.
- **on_screen**: Child converts 3 m 40 cm → 340 cm; then adds masses with regrouping.
- **check**: 3 m 40 cm in cm? — Answer: 340 cm

### step_6: Indian traditional units
- **tts**:
  - A hath is about elbow to fingertip.
  - A tola is a traditional mass unit used for jewellery.
- **on_screen**: Simple illustrations of hath along an arm and tola weights in a jewellery shop.
- **check**: Jewellery mass unit? — Answer: Tola

### step_7: Sensible estimates
- **tts**:
  - A door is about 2 metres high. A water bottle is about 500 millilitres.
  - Wild units make wild answers.
- **on_screen**: Child rejects door height in mm or km; picks metres.
- **check**: Door height about? — Answer: 2 m

### step_8: Story problems
- **tts**:
  - Read what is asked: convert, add, subtract or share into equal bottles.
  - Change units first when needed.
- **on_screen**: Shop and kitchen stories with rice sacks and lemonade jugs.
- **check**: 1 litre into 250 ml glasses? — Answer: 4

## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Which unit is best for measuring the length of a pencil?
- **options**:
  - A) Kilometre
  - B) Metre
  - C) Centimetre
  - D) Kilogram
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A pencil is short, so centimetres (cm) are the right unit.
- **skill**: length-units
