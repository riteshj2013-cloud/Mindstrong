## Interactive lesson outline
- L1: Hello, word worker! Welcome to the Word Workshop, where we build sentences together.
- L2: Every word has a job. Let's meet the team!
- L3: Nouns are naming words: Ravi, school, mango, Delhi.
- L4: Pronouns take a noun's place, so we don't repeat it. He, she, it, we, they.
- L5: Adjectives are describing words. A big tiger. A sweet mango.
- L6: Verbs are doing words. Run, eat, sing. They also tell us when: I eat, I ate, I am eating.
- L7: (optional) Little words like a, an and the come before nouns. Use "an" before a vowel sound: an apple, an owl.
- L8: Let's try one together. Listen: "Priya is eating an orange under the tree."
- L9: Priya is a noun, a name. "Eating" is a verb, and "is eating" means it's happening right now.
- L10: "An orange," because orange begins with a vowel sound. And "under" tells us where she is.
- L11: (optional) Now you can spot the jobs of the words, just like a real word worker!
- L12: Your turn. For each question, ask, "What job is this word doing?"
- L13: If you're not sure, say the sentence out loud. The right answer usually sounds right.
- L14: Super work! You are building strong sentences already.

## Passage bank (for quizzes)

### P1 — A Day at the Zoo
Last Sunday, Ravi and his aunt went to the zoo in Mysuru. They saw two elephants, three monkeys and a big tiger. The monkeys were jumping from branch to branch. A baby elephant was spraying water on its mother. Ravi laughed loudly. His aunt bought him an ice cream. At noon, they sat under a tree and ate their lunch. Today, Ravi has a new notebook. He is drawing the animals in it. He is very happy.

### P2 — At the Fruit Shop (dialogue)
**Meena:** Mummy, are these mangoes sweet?
**Mother:** Yes, they are. The shopkeeper says they are from Ratnagiri.
**Meena:** I want an apple too.
**Mother:** We have apples at home. Let's buy bananas instead.
**Meena:** Okay! I am carrying the bag.
**Mother:** Thank you, beta. It is heavy, so hold it with both hands.
**Shopkeeper:** Here are six bananas. They are fresh and yellow.
**Mother:** Thank you. How much are they?
**Shopkeeper:** Forty rupees, please.

### P3 — Our New Classroom
Our new classroom is on the first floor. There is a big window next to my desk. A neem tree stands outside the window. Every morning, a bird sits on its branch and sings. Our teacher, Ms. Lata, keeps a globe on her table. The books are in the cupboard behind the door. We have a small garden in front of the classroom. The guard locks the room at five o'clock every evening.
