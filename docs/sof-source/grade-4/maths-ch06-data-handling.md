## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: What is data?
- **tts**:
  - Data are facts and numbers we collect.
  - We organise them to answer questions.
- **on_screen**: Clipboard survey in class; ticks become numbers.
- **check**: Data means? — Answer: Collected facts/numbers

### step_2: Tally marks
- **tts**:
  - Group strokes in fives: four upright and one slash.
  - Gates make counting faster.
- **on_screen**: Child converts ticks to tally gates live.
- **check**: One tally gate = ? — Answer: 5

### step_3: Tables
- **tts**:
  - Tables use rows and columns.
  - Each category gets a count.
- **on_screen**: Fruit likes table fills as names are tapped.
- **check**: Largest count means? — Answer: Most popular

### step_4: Pictographs and keys
- **tts**:
  - Each picture stands for a number in the key.
  - Half a picture means half the key value.
- **on_screen**: Key card: 1 flower = 5; child builds a row.
- **check**: 3 icons × 5 = ? — Answer: 15

### step_5: Bar graphs
- **tts**:
  - Bar height shows the value.
  - Read the title and the scale first.
- **on_screen**: Bars grow for sports votes; scale 0, 2, 4…
- **check**: Taller bar means? — Answer: Larger number

### step_6: Compare and difference
- **tts**:
  - Most, least, total and how many more are common questions.
  - Subtract to find the difference.
- **on_screen**: Two bars highlighted; difference animates.
- **check**: 14 vs 9: how many more? — Answer: 5

### step_7: Choose a display
- **tts**:
  - Tallies help while counting. Graphs help comparing.
  - Always keep a clear title.
- **on_screen**: Child drags the same data into tally, table and bar views.
- **check**: Best for comparing hobbies? — Answer: Bar/pictograph

### step_8: Check your graph
- **tts**:
  - Bars should match the table.
  - Missing keys make pictographs unreadable.
- **on_screen**: Mismatch alert when bar height disagrees with table.
- **check**: No pictograph key? — Answer: Cannot know values

### Q01
- **stem**: In a tally chart, one complete tally gate (|||| with a slash) stands for…
- **options**:
  - A) 4
  - B) 5
  - C) 10
  - D) 1
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A tally gate of five strokes (four upright + one diagonal) means 5.
- **skill**: tally
