## Interactive lesson outline
- L1: Welcome to the writers' desk! Different writing jobs need different shapes.
- L2: A notice is short and factual: school name, heading, date, key details, and a signed name.
- L3: A friendly letter needs address and date, Dear…, a body with news, and a warm closing.
- L4: An email adds a clear Subject line so the reader knows the purpose at once.
- L5: A phone message notes For, From, time, and the main point — then who took it.
- L6: Sequencing matters too. First, then, finally help readers follow steps.
- L7: Let's try: "wrote / a / postcard / Meera / to / her / cousin" → "Meera wrote a postcard to her cousin."
- L8: (optional) Formal school letters use Respected Sir/Ma'am and a respectful closing.
- L9: Ask for each item: Who will read this? What must they know or do?
- L10: Great work — formats help your words land clearly. On to practice!
