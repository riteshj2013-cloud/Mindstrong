## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Equal Parts
- **tts**:
  - A fraction is a part of a whole. But the parts must be equal!
  - Two equal pieces? Each one is a half.
- **on_screen**: A roti is torn into two pieces of different sizes, and a red cross appears. Then a knife cuts it neatly down the middle, and both equal halves glow green with the label 1/2.
- **check**: A roti is broken into 2 pieces of different sizes. Is each piece a half? — Answer: No

### step_2: Halves and Quarters
- **tts**:
  - Fold a paper in half, then fold it again. Now you have four equal quarters.
  - Two quarters make one half.
- **on_screen**: A square paper folds once (2 halves) and then again (4 quarters); child taps to shade 1 quarter, then 2 quarters, and sees 2 quarters line up exactly with 1 half.
- **check**: How many quarters make one whole? — Answer: 4

### step_3: Thirds and Three-Quarters
- **tts**:
  - Three equal parts are called thirds.
  - Shade three of the four quarters, and you have three-quarters!
- **on_screen**: A chocolate bar splits into 3 equal pieces (each labelled 1/3); then a square in 4 equal parts, where the child shades 3 to make 3/4.
- **check**: A square is cut into 4 equal parts and 3 are shaded. What fraction is shaded? — Answer: 3/4

### step_4: Numerator and Denominator
- **tts**:
  - The bottom number, the denominator, tells how many equal parts.
  - The top number, the numerator, tells how many parts we take.
- **on_screen**: The fraction 2/5 sits beside a bar in 5 equal parts with 2 shaded; tapping the 5 highlights all parts, and tapping the 2 highlights only the shaded ones.
- **check**: In 2/5, what is the denominator? — Answer: 5

### step_5: Comparing Unit Fractions
- **tts**:
  - A unit fraction has one on top. More parts means smaller pieces!
  - So one half is bigger than one third.
- **on_screen**: Three same-size laddoos are shared among 2, 3 and 4 friends; the pieces shrink as more friends join. Child drags 1/2, 1/3 and 1/4 onto a podium from biggest to smallest.
- **check**: Which is bigger, 1/3 or 1/5? — Answer: 1/3

### step_6: Same-Size Pieces
- **tts**:
  - Two quarters cover the same space as one half.
  - When pieces are the same size, more pieces means more.
- **on_screen**: Two bars are placed one above the other: one in halves with 1 shaded, one in quarters with 2 shaded, and the shaded parts line up (2/4 = 1/2). Then bars showing 2/6 and 5/6 for the child to compare.
- **check**: Which is bigger, 2/6 or 5/6? — Answer: 5/6

### step_7: Fraction of a Collection
- **tts**:
  - To find half of twelve, share twelve into two equal groups.
  - Each group has six, so half of twelve is six.
- **on_screen**: 12 mangoes are dealt into 2 baskets (6 each); then 20 marbles are dealt into 4 cups, and the child taps one cup to find 1/4 of 20.
- **check**: What is 1/4 of 20? — Answer: 5

### step_8: Fraction Stories
- **tts**:
  - Read the story carefully. Find the whole first, then find the part.
  - Three-quarters of sixteen is twelve.
- **on_screen**: A class photo of 24 children; the child splits them into 3 equal rows, then highlights 1 row as "children wearing glasses". A second story shares 16 pencils into quarters and colours 3 groups.
- **check**: 1/3 of a class of 24 children wear glasses. How many children wear glasses? — Answer: 8

## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: A paratha is cut into 4 equal pieces. What fraction of the paratha is each piece?
- **options**:
  - A) 1/2
  - B) 1/3
  - C) 4/1
  - D) 1/4
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: 4 equal pieces means each piece is one quarter, written 1/4.
- **skill**: equal_parts
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: In the fraction 3/4, what is the denominator?
- **options**:
  - A) 3
  - B) 4
  - C) 7
  - D) 1
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The denominator is the bottom number; it tells us the whole has 4 equal parts.
- **skill**: numerator_denominator
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: Which picture shows 1/2 shaded?
- **options**:
  - A) A circle cut into 2 equal parts with 1 part shaded
  - B) A circle cut into 2 unequal parts with the smaller part shaded
  - C) A square cut into 4 equal parts with 1 part shaded
  - D) A circle cut into 3 equal parts with 1 part shaded
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: A half needs 2 EQUAL parts with 1 of them shaded.
- **skill**: identify_diagram
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q22

  - C) Both ate the same amount
  - D) Sam, because quarters are bigger pieces

- **skill**: compare
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q23

  - D) 5
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: 1 litre fills 4 quarter-litre glasses and half a litre fills 2 more, so 4 + 2 = 6.
- **skill**: equivalent
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q24
- **stem**: A box has 18 laddoos. 2/3 of them are besan laddoos and the rest are coconut laddoos. How many coconut laddoos are there?

  - A) 12
  - B) 6
  - C) 9
  - D) 3
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 2/3 of 18 is 12 besan laddoos, so 18 − 12 = 6 are coconut.

- **difficulty**: hard
