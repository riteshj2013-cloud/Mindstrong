# Chapter 3: Water — Sources, Uses and the Water Cycle

## Meta
- Grade: 4
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: water, sources, uses, water cycle, evaporation, condensation, rain, conservation
- Source basis: NCERT Class 4 EVS / typical NSO themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 4 (clear, high visual/TTS)

## Learning objectives
- I can explain why all living things need water to stay alive.
- I can name sources of water such as rain, rivers, lakes, wells, handpumps and taps.
- I can list ways we use water at home, on farms and in factories.
- I can describe the water cycle using the words evaporation, condensation and precipitation.
- I can tell why dirty water can make us sick and how to make water safe.
- I can show simple habits that help save water every day.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Water Means Life
- **Visual:** A bright split screen: a thirsty wilting plant, a panting dog, and a child holding an empty glass. A water drop mascot named "Boondi" bounces in and fills each one, and they all perk up.
- **TTS:** Plants, animals and people all need water. Without water, no living thing can stay alive.
- **Interaction:** Tap each thirsty living thing to give it a drop of water. Each one smiles and grows a little.

### Scene 2: Where Does Water Come From?
- **Visual:** A village map with rain clouds, a river, a lake, a deep well, a handpump and a home tap. A cut-away view shows water stored under the soil, feeding the well and handpump.
- **TTS:** Water comes from rain, rivers, lakes and wells. Some water hides under the ground as groundwater.
- **Interaction:** Drag-and-drop: sort cards into "Water on the ground" (river, lake, pond) and "Water under the ground" (well, handpump). Tap the tap to follow the pipe back to its source.

### Scene 3: How Do We Use Water?
- **Visual:** Five animated mini-rooms: a kitchen pot of dal, a glass of drinking water, a bucket of clothes, a green farm field with water channels, and a factory with cooling pipes.
- **TTS:** We use water for drinking, cooking, cleaning, growing crops and making things in factories.
- **Interaction:** Matching game: link each picture (glass, pot, bucket, field, factory) to its use label. Correct links make the room light up.

### Scene 4: The Amazing Water Cycle
- **Visual:** Boondi rides a looping path: the Sun warms a lake, Boondi floats up as invisible vapour, cools high in the sky, joins friends to form a cloud, and falls back as rain into the lake. Arrows label Evaporation, Condensation and Precipitation.
- **TTS:** The Sun heats water. It rises as vapour, cools into clouds, then falls back as rain.
- **Interaction:** Put four step cards in the right order around the circle: Sun heats water → Evaporation → Condensation (clouds) → Precipitation (rain). A wrong order makes Boondi wobble and try again.

### Scene 5: Keep Water Clean and Save Every Drop
- **Visual:** Two glasses side by side, one muddy and one clear. A friendly doctor shows boiling and filtering through clean cloth. Then a home scene: a dripping tap, a running tap while brushing, a bucket of rinse water, and a rooftop pipe filling a rain tank.
- **TTS:** Dirty water can make us sick. Boil water to stay safe, and never waste a drop.
- **Interaction:** "Water Hero" hunt: tap to turn off the running tap, fix the leaky tap, pour rinse water on plants, and connect the roof pipe to the rain tank. Earn a Water Hero badge.

## Quiz Set A — 24 MCQs
*(Q1–10 Easy, Q11–19 Medium, Q20–24 Challenge)*

### Q1
**Stem:** Which of these do all living things need to stay alive?
- A) Gold
- B) Water
- C) Toys
- D) Plastic

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Plants, animals and people all need water to live. Gold, toys and plastic are not needed for life.

### Q2
**Stem:** Which of these is a natural source of water?
- A) Plastic bottle
- B) Bucket
- C) Water tank
- D) River

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A river is made by nature. Bottles, buckets and tanks only store water that comes from natural sources.

### Q3
**Stem:** Water that falls from the clouds as drops is called —
- A) Rain
- B) Fog
- C) Steam
- D) Ice cube

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Rain is water falling from clouds as drops. Fog floats near the ground, and steam comes from boiling water.

### Q4
**Stem:** Which of these helps us pull up water from under the ground?
- A) Kite
- B) Umbrella
- C) Handpump
- D) Fan

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A handpump brings up groundwater when we push its handle up and down.

### Q5
**Stem:** How does water from the sea taste?
- A) Sweet
- B) Sour
- C) Salty
- D) Bitter like medicine

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Sea water has a lot of salt mixed in it, so it tastes salty and is not safe to drink.

### Q6
**Stem:** Which of these is a use of water for cleaning?
- A) Washing clothes
- B) Drinking juice
- C) Cooking rice
- D) Watering plants

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Washing clothes uses water to remove dirt. The other choices use water for drinking, cooking and growing plants.

### Q7
**Stem:** Farmers use a lot of water mainly to —
- A) Paint their houses
- B) Fly kites
- C) Make roads shine
- D) Grow crops

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Crops need water to grow. Farmers water their fields using rain, canals, wells and pumps.

### Q8
**Stem:** What makes water in a pond slowly dry up on a hot day?
- A) The Moon
- B) The heat of the Sun
- C) The stars
- D) Shadows of trees

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Sun's heat turns pond water into water vapour, which rises into the air. This is evaporation.

### Q9
**Stem:** What are clouds made of?
- A) Tiny drops of water
- B) Cotton
- C) Smoke from fires
- D) Sand

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Clouds are made of many tiny water drops (or ice bits) floating high in the sky.

### Q10
**Stem:** What is the best habit while brushing your teeth?
- A) Keep the tap running
- B) Use a hose pipe
- C) Turn off the tap until you rinse
- D) Fill a bathtub

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Turning off the tap while brushing saves many litres of water every day.

### Q11
**Stem:** When water changes into water vapour, it is called —
- A) Freezing
- B) Evaporation
- C) Melting
- D) Condensation

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Evaporation is when liquid water becomes water vapour, usually when it is heated.

### Q12
**Stem:** When water vapour cools down and changes into tiny drops of water, it is called —
- A) Evaporation
- B) Melting
- C) Boiling
- D) Condensation

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Condensation is when water vapour cools and turns back into liquid drops. This is how clouds form.

### Q13
**Stem:** Water stored in the soil and rocks under the ground is called —
- A) Rainwater
- B) Seawater
- C) Tap water
- D) Groundwater

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Rainwater soaks into the soil and collects under the ground. This is groundwater, which wells and handpumps use.

### Q14
**Stem:** Meera keeps a glass of ice-cold water on the table. Soon, water drops appear on the outside of the glass. Where do they come from?
- A) The glass is leaking
- B) Water vapour in the air cools on the cold glass
- C) The ice melts through the glass
- D) From Meera's hands

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Air has invisible water vapour. When it touches the cold glass, it cools and condenses into drops.

### Q15
**Stem:** What is a safe way to kill germs in drinking water at home?
- A) Add salt
- B) Stir it fast
- C) Boil it and let it cool
- D) Add food colour

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Boiling water kills most germs. After it cools, it is safer to drink.

### Q16
**Stem:** Which illness can spread by drinking dirty water?
- A) Cholera
- B) A broken bone
- C) Sunburn
- D) Short eyesight

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Cholera is caused by germs in dirty water. Drinking clean, boiled or filtered water helps prevent it.

### Q17
**Stem:** Water left after washing vegetables can be reused to —
- A) Drink
- B) Cook dal
- C) Water plants
- D) Brush teeth

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Rinse water is not clean enough to drink or cook with, but it is perfect for watering plants.

### Q18
**Stem:** Collecting and storing rainwater so we can use it later is called —
- A) Rainwater harvesting
- B) Evaporation
- C) Flooding
- D) Melting

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Rainwater harvesting means catching rainwater from rooftops or the ground and saving it in tanks, pits or ponds.

### Q19
**Stem:** A tap that keeps dripping all day —
- A) Wastes no water at all
- B) Wastes a lot of water over time
- C) Wastes only hot water
- D) Wastes water only at night

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Each drop is small, but a dripping tap can waste many buckets of water in a day. Leaks should be fixed.

### Q20
**Stem:** Which is the correct order of the water cycle?
- A) Rain → Sun heats water → clouds form → evaporation
- B) Clouds form → evaporation → rain → Sun heats water
- C) Rain → clouds form → evaporation → water cools
- D) Sun heats water → evaporation → condensation into clouds → rain

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** First the Sun heats water, which evaporates. The vapour cools and condenses into clouds, then falls as rain.

### Q21
**Stem:** Where is most of the water on Earth found?
- A) Oceans and seas
- B) Rivers
- C) Wells
- D) Ponds

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Oceans and seas hold almost all of Earth's water. Rivers, wells and ponds hold only a tiny part.

### Q22
**Stem:** On which day will wet clothes dry the fastest?
- A) A cool day, kept inside a cupboard
- B) A rainy day, hung outside
- C) Any day, kept folded in a bag
- D) A sunny, windy day, spread out on a line

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Sunlight, wind and spreading clothes out all help water evaporate faster.

### Q23
**Stem:** Riya covers a pan of boiling water with a lid. Later, she sees water drops on the inside of the lid. Which two changes happened?
- A) Melting and freezing
- B) Freezing and evaporation
- C) Evaporation and condensation
- D) Melting and condensation

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The hot water evaporated into vapour. The vapour touched the cooler lid and condensed into drops.

### Q24
**Stem:** Why does rainwater harvesting help wells and handpumps in a village?
- A) It makes more rain fall
- B) It helps refill the groundwater
- C) It makes the water salty
- D) It stops evaporation forever

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Saved rainwater soaks into the ground and refills groundwater, so wells and handpumps do not dry up.

## Quiz Set B — 24 DIFFERENT MCQs
*(Q1–10 Easy, Q11–19 Medium, Q20–24 Challenge)*

### Q1
**Stem:** Which of these brings water into our homes through pipes?
- A) Chimney
- B) Window
- C) Tap
- D) Light switch

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Water travels through pipes and comes out of a tap when we open it.

### Q2
**Stem:** A large body of still water with land all around it is called a —
- A) Lake
- B) River
- C) Waterfall
- D) Stream

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A lake has land all around it and its water stays mostly still. Rivers and streams keep flowing.

### Q3
**Stem:** Which of these foods needs water to be cooked?
- A) A fresh apple
- B) A raw carrot
- C) A banana
- D) Boiled rice

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Rice is cooked by boiling it in water. Apples, raw carrots and bananas can be eaten without cooking.

### Q4
**Stem:** A deep hole dug into the ground to get water is called a —
- A) Tunnel
- B) Well
- C) Garbage pit
- D) Burrow

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A well is dug deep until it reaches groundwater. People pull the water up with a bucket and rope or a pump.

### Q5
**Stem:** Which living thing has its home in water?
- A) Fish
- B) Camel
- C) Sparrow
- D) Cow

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Fish live in water and breathe using gills. Water is home for many plants and animals.

### Q6
**Stem:** Water changes into ice when it is —
- A) Heated
- B) Stirred
- C) Poured
- D) Made very cold

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** When water becomes very cold, it freezes and turns into solid ice, like in a freezer.

### Q7
**Stem:** When ice changes into water, it is called —
- A) Freezing
- B) Melting
- C) Condensation
- D) Raining

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ice melts into water when it gets warm. Freezing is the opposite change.

### Q8
**Stem:** Water that we drink should always be —
- A) Muddy
- B) Smelly
- C) Clean and safe
- D) Coloured

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Drinking water must be clean and safe so that germs do not make us sick.

### Q9
**Stem:** Plants take in water from the soil through their —
- A) Flowers
- B) Fruits
- C) Seeds
- D) Roots

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Roots grow into the soil and soak up water for the whole plant.

### Q10
**Stem:** Which is a good habit to save water?
- A) Washing a car with a bucket and cloth
- B) Leaving the tap open while talking
- C) Playing with a running hose pipe
- D) Throwing leftover drinking water down the drain

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A bucket uses much less water than a running hose. Leftover drinking water can be given to plants.

### Q11
**Stem:** Water falling from clouds as rain, snow or hail is called —
- A) Evaporation
- B) Condensation
- C) Precipitation
- D) Germination

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Precipitation is any water that falls from clouds to the ground, such as rain, snow or hail.

### Q12
**Stem:** Which of these gets its water from groundwater?
- A) Rain gauge
- B) Handpump
- C) Cloud
- D) Ocean

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A handpump has a pipe going deep into the ground to bring up groundwater.

### Q13
**Stem:** A puddle on the road disappears on a sunny afternoon. This is mainly because the water —
- A) Turns into sand
- B) Evaporates into the air
- C) Becomes salt
- D) Turns into ice

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The Sun's heat turns the puddle water into water vapour, which goes into the air.

### Q14
**Stem:** Factories mainly use water for —
- A) Watching TV
- B) Flying planes
- C) Reading books
- D) Cooling machines and making things

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Factories use water to cool hot machines and to make things like paper, cloth and food.

### Q15
**Stem:** Which of these shows water being wasted?
- A) Leaving the tap open while soaping your hands
- B) Closing the tap tightly
- C) Getting a leaking pipe fixed
- D) Bathing with a bucket and mug

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Water flows away for nothing while we soap our hands. We should close the tap and open it only to rinse.

### Q16
**Stem:** Muddy water is collected in a pot. What should be done first to clean it before boiling?
- A) Add more mud
- B) Shake it hard
- C) Let the mud settle, then pour it through a clean cloth
- D) Add cooking oil

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Letting mud settle and filtering through a clean cloth removes dirt. Boiling afterwards kills germs.

### Q17
**Stem:** Water vapour high in the sky cools down and forms —
- A) Clouds
- B) Sand
- C) Smoke
- D) Stones

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** When water vapour cools high up, it condenses into tiny drops that join together to make clouds.

### Q18
**Stem:** Arjun sees tiny water drops on the grass early in the morning, though it did not rain. Why?
- A) The grass sweats like we do
- B) Someone always waters it at night
- C) Water vapour in the air cools on the cool grass and forms drops
- D) The drops fall from the Moon

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** These drops are called dew. Water vapour in the air condenses on the cool grass during the night.

### Q19
**Stem:** Which of these does NOT help save water?
- A) Fixing leaking taps
- B) Reusing rinse water for plants
- C) Collecting rainwater in tanks
- D) Washing the courtyard daily with a running hose

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A running hose wastes a lot of water. Sweeping first and using a bucket is a better choice.

### Q20
**Stem:** Which of these is fresh water, not salty water?
- A) Sea water
- B) River water
- C) Ocean water
- D) Water from a salt pan

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** River water is fresh water. Sea water, ocean water and salt pan water have lots of salt in them.

### Q21
**Stem:** About how much of the Earth's surface is covered with water?
- A) One-tenth
- B) Half
- C) About three-fourths
- D) One-fourth

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** About three out of every four parts of Earth's surface is water, mostly salty oceans and seas.

### Q22
**Stem:** Two same plates have the same amount of water. One is kept in the sun and one in the shade. What will happen?
- A) The plate in the sun will dry first
- B) The plate in the shade will dry first
- C) Both will dry at exactly the same time
- D) Neither plate will ever dry

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Heat from the Sun makes water evaporate faster, so the plate in the sun dries first.

### Q23
**Stem:** Clouds keep collecting water drops and become dark and heavy. Which step of the water cycle comes next?
- A) Evaporation
- B) Precipitation
- C) Condensation
- D) Melting

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** When drops in a cloud become too heavy, they fall as rain. This step is called precipitation.

### Q24
**Stem:** In some dry places, people built stepwells and ponds long ago. How did these help them?
- A) They brought the sea closer
- B) They turned salty water sweet
- C) They stopped all rain from falling
- D) They stored rainwater for the dry months

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Stepwells and ponds collected rainwater in the rainy season, so people had water when it did not rain.
