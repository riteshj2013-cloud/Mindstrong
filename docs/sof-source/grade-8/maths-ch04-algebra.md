## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Terms, Factors and Coefficients
- **tts**:
  - An algebraic expression is built from numbers and letters joined by plus and minus.
  - Each chunk separated by plus or minus is a term, and the number in front of a variable is its coefficient.
- **on_screen**: Expression 3x² − 5xy + 7 splits into three term cards; child taps each to reveal coefficient and factors.
- **check**: How many terms are in 3x² − 5xy + 7? — Answer: 3

### step_2: Like and Unlike Terms
- **tts**:
  - Like terms have the same variables with the same powers, so you can add or subtract them.
  - Unlike terms stay separate; you cannot combine 3x² with 5x.
- **on_screen**: Sorting game: cards such as 4a²b, −a²b, 2ab² and 7 go into Like / Unlike bins relative to a²b.
- **check**: Are 4a²b and −a²b like terms? — Answer: Yes

### step_3: Adding and Subtracting Expressions
- **tts**:
  - Line up like terms, then add or subtract their coefficients.
  - When you subtract an expression, change every sign inside the brackets first.
- **on_screen**: Vertical workspace for (5x − 2) − (3x + 4); the minus flips +4 into −4 before combining.
- **check**: What is (5x − 2) − (3x + 4)? — Answer: 2x − 6

### step_4: Multiplying a Monomial by a Polynomial
- **tts**:
  - Multiply the coefficient numbers, then multiply the variable parts using laws of exponents.
  - Distribute: every term inside the brackets gets multiplied.
- **on_screen**: Animation spreads 2x across (x + 3) to make 2x² + 6x.
- **check**: Expand 2x(x + 3). — Answer: 2x² + 6x

### step_5: Multiplying Two Binomials
- **tts**:
  - Use FOIL or an area model: first, outer, inner, last.
  - Then combine any like terms that appear.
- **on_screen**: Area rectangle for (x + 2)(x + 3) fills four tiles labelled x², 3x, 2x and 6.
- **check**: What is (x + 2)(x + 3)? — Answer: x² + 5x + 6

### step_6: Standard Identities
- **tts**:
  - Remember three workhorses: (a + b)², (a − b)² and a² − b².
  - They save time and reduce careless expansion errors.
- **on_screen**: Identity flashcards flip to show a² + 2ab + b², a² − 2ab + b² and (a + b)(a − b).
- **check**: Expand (a + b)². — Answer: a² + 2ab + b²

### step_7: Using Identities to Compute
- **tts**:
  - Rewrite numbers near a round base, then apply an identity.
  - For example, 102² becomes (100 + 2)².
- **on_screen**: Number pad: child enters 102, the app rewrites it as (100 + 2)² and expands step by step to 10404.
- **check**: Find 102² using an identity. — Answer: 10404

### step_8: Factorising with Identities
- **tts**:
  - Spot a² − b² patterns and rewrite them as a product of two brackets.
  - Perfect-square trinomials reverse the (a ± b)² identities.
- **on_screen**: Factor machine takes x² − 16 and outputs (x + 4)(x − 4); child checks by expanding back.
- **check**: Factor x² − 16. — Answer: (x + 4)(x − 4)


## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Which of these is a monomial?
- **options**:
  - A) 2x + 3
  - B) 5xy
  - C) x + y + z
  - D) a² − b²
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A monomial is a single term; 5xy has one term, while the others have two or more.
- **skill**: terms
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: How many terms are in 3x² − 5xy + 7?
- **options**:
  - A) 2
  - B) 3
  - C) 4
  - D) 1
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The terms are 3x², −5xy and 7, so there are three terms.
- **skill**: terms
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: What is the coefficient of x² in 4x² − 3x + 1?
- **options**:
  - A) −3
  - B) 1
  - C) 4
  - D) x²
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: The coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying x², which is 4.
- **skill**: terms
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: Add: (2x + 3) + (5x − 1)
- **options**:
  - A) 7x + 2
  - B) 7x + 4
  - C) 3x + 2
  - D) 7x − 2
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Like terms: 2x + 5x = 7x and 3 + (−1) = 2, so the sum is 7x + 2.
- **skill**: add_subtract
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: Subtract: (5x − 2) − (3x + 4)
- **options**:
  - A) 2x − 6
  - B) 2x + 2
  - C) 8x − 6
  - D) 2x − 2
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Distribute the minus: 5x − 2 − 3x − 4 = 2x − 6.
- **skill**: add_subtract
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: Multiply: 3x × 4y
- **options**:
  - A) 7xy
  - B) 12xy
  - C) 12x + y
  - D) 12x²y
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Multiply coefficients and variables: 3 × 4 = 12 and x × y = xy.
- **skill**: multiply
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: What is (x + 2)(x + 3)?
- **options**:
  - A) x² + 5x + 6
  - B) x² + 6
  - C) x² + 5
  - D) 2x + 5
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: FOIL: x² + 3x + 2x + 6 = x² + 5x + 6.
- **skill**: multiply
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q08
- **stem**: Expand: 2(x − 4)
- **options**:
  - A) 2x − 4
  - B) 2x − 8
  - C) x − 8
  - D) 2x + 8
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Distribute: 2 × x − 2 × 4 = 2x − 8.
- **skill**: multiply
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q09

- **options**:
  - A) a² + b²
  - B) a² + 2ab + b²
  - C) a² − 2ab + b²
  - D) a² − b²
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The square of a sum is a² + 2ab + b².
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q10
- **stem**: (a − b)² equals…

  - A) a² − b²
  - B) a² + 2ab + b²
  - C) a² − 2ab + b²
  - D) a² + b²
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: The square of a difference is a² − 2ab + b².
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q14

- **difficulty**: medium

### Q18

  - B) (x + 4)²
  - C) (x + 4)(x − 4)
  - D) x(x − 16)

- **skill**: identity

### Q19
- **stem**: If (a + b)² = 49 and ab = 10, find a² + b².
- **options**:
  - A) 29
  - B) 39
  - C) 59
  - D) 9
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b² = 49, so a² + b² = 49 − 2·10 = 29.
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q20
- **stem**: Simplify: (x + y)² − (x − y)²
- **options**:
  - A) 4xy
  - B) 2x² + 2y²
  - C) 0
  - D) 2xy
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Expand: (x² + 2xy + y²) − (x² − 2xy + y²) = 4xy.
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q21
- **stem**: Expand: (x + 2)(x − 2)(x² + 4)
- **options**:
  - A) x⁴ − 16
  - B) x⁴ + 16
  - C) x⁴ − 8
  - D) x⁴ − 4
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: (x + 2)(x − 2) = x² − 4, then (x² − 4)(x² + 4) = x⁴ − 16.
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q22

  - C) 4a² − 6ab − 9b²
  - D) 4a² − 12ab + 9b²
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Difference of squares: (2a)² − (3b)² = 4a² − 9b².
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q23

- **options**:
  - A) 10404
  - B) 10000
  - C) 10400
  - D) 10204
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 102² = (100 + 2)² = 10000 + 400 + 4 = 10404.
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q24
- **stem**: Simplify: 3(x − 2) − 2(x + 5) + 4
