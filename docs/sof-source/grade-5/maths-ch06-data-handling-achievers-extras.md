### Q02
- **id**: g5-maths-ch06-ach-x-02
- **stem**: Shivansh records the shoe sizes in his class: size 3 is worn by 4 children, size 4 by 7 children, size 5 by 9 children and size 6 by 5 children. How many children wear a size bigger than the most common size?
- **options**:
  - A) 14
  - B) 9
  - C) 21
  - D) 5
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: The most common size (the mode) is size 5, worn by 9 children. Only size 6 is bigger, and 5 children wear it, so the answer is 5. 14 is tempting because it adds the 9 children who wear size 5 as well, but they wear the most common size itself, not a bigger one.
- **hint**: Find which size is most common in Shivansh's class first, then add only the children with larger sizes.
- **skill**: count_above_the_mode
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers

### Q04
- **id**: g5-maths-ch06-ach-x-04
- **stem**: Tisha's football team played 10 matches. They scored 0 goals in 2 matches, 1 goal in 3 matches, 2 goals in 4 matches and 3 goals in 1 match. What is the average number of goals per match?
- **options**:
  - A) 1.5
  - B) 1.4
  - C) 2.5
  - D) 1.2
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Total goals = 0 × 2 + 1 × 3 + 2 × 4 + 3 × 1 = 0 + 3 + 8 + 3 = 14. Shared over 10 matches, the average is 14 ÷ 10 = 1.4 goals. 1.5 is tempting because it is the average of the goal numbers 0, 1, 2 and 3, but those numbers happened in different numbers of matches, so each must be multiplied by its count first.
- **hint**: Find the total goals Tisha's team scored across all the matches before sharing them out equally.
- **skill**: average_from_a_frequency_list
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers

### Q05
- **id**: g5-maths-ch06-ach-x-05
- **stem**: Urja draws a bar graph of the old newspapers collected by four classes: 56 kg, 44 kg, 72 kg and 28 kg. Her bars use 1 cm for every 8 kg. How much longer is the longest bar than the shortest bar?
- **options**:
  - A) 5.5 cm
  - B) 44 cm
  - C) 9 cm
  - D) 3.5 cm
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: The longest bar is 72 ÷ 8 = 9 cm and the shortest is 28 ÷ 8 = 3.5 cm, so the difference is 9 − 3.5 = 5.5 cm. 44 cm is tempting because 72 − 28 = 44, but that is a difference in kilograms, and it still has to be divided by 8 to turn it into centimetres.
- **hint**: Change each of Urja's amounts into centimetres using her scale, or find the difference in kilograms and then use the scale.
- **skill**: bar_length_difference_from_scale
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers
