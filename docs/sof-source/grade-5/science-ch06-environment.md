# Chapter 6: Our Environment and Natural Resources

## Meta
- Grade: 5
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: environment, natural resources, renewable, non-renewable, pollution, conservation, forests, 3Rs, pictorial, svg-diagrams
- Source basis: NCERT Class 5 EVS / typical olympiad themes (original items only)

## Pictorial notes
Visual items include resource cards P–S, pollution scene A/B, 3 R bins, forest versus cleared hill, energy sources P–R, and water-cycle arrows.

## Learning objectives
- List natural resources (air, water, soil, forests, minerals, fuels) and say why they matter.
- Sort renewable and non-renewable resources with everyday examples.
- Identify air, water, soil and noise pollution from simple scenes.
- Explain reduce, reuse and recycle with home examples.
- Describe how forests help us and what deforestation risks.
- Compare cleaner renewable energy ideas (sun, wind) with fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas).

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Gifts from nature
- **Visual:** Sun, river, forest, soil and mineral cards.
- **TTS:** Natural resources come from nature. We depend on them for life and work.
- **Interaction:** Tap each card to hear how people use that resource.

### Scene 2: Renewable or not?
- **Visual:** Sun and wind versus coal and oil barrels.
- **TTS:** Renewable resources can be replaced in a human lifetime. Fossil fuels cannot.
- **Interaction:** Swipe each resource into Renewable or Non-renewable.

### Scene 3: Pollution problems
- **Visual:** Smoky chimney, dirty pipe into river, loud speaker, littered soil.
- **TTS:** Pollution is harmful waste in air, water, soil or as loud noise.
- **Interaction:** Match each scene to air, water, soil or noise pollution.

### Scene 4: The 3 R's
- **Visual:** Three bins: use less, use again, remake.
- **TTS:** Reduce, reuse and recycle cut waste and save resources.
- **Interaction:** Sort action cards into reduce, reuse or recycle.

### Scene 5: Forests and clean energy
- **Visual:** Healthy forest beside cleared hill; solar and wind versus coal plant.
- **TTS:** Forests give oxygen and homes. Sun and wind can power us with less smoke than coal.
- **Interaction:** Choose the greener energy card and one forest-care action.

**Options:**
A) natural resources
B) only plastic toys
C) artificial gravity
D) exam papers
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Natural resources include air, water, soil, forests, minerals and fuels that come from nature.

B) Only smoke
C) Only nitrogen from factories
D) Helium balloons only

**Options:**
A) conservation

**Explanation:** Conservation means careful use so resources last longer and less fuel is burned at power stations.

### Q13
**Stem:** Which is a non-renewable resource?
**Options:**
A) Sunlight
B) Wind
C) Petroleum (crude oil)
D) Flowing river water used carefully
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Petroleum formed over millions of years and cannot be replaced quickly once we burn it.

### Q14
**Stem:** Planting new trees on a bare hill is called —
**Options:**
A) deforestation
B) air pollution
C) afforestation (or reforestation)
D) mining
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Afforestation/reforestation means growing forests again, which helps soil, wildlife and air quality.

### Q15
**Stem:** Which habit best matches reduce?
**Options:**
A) Printing posters on both sides of scrap paper after first use only as art frames
B) Buying a new bottle every hour
C) Using a smaller amount of water while brushing teeth
D) Throwing intact jars straight to landfill without thought
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Using less water is reduce. Reusing jars is reuse; recycling paper is recycle.

### Q16
**Stem:** Soil is important because it —
**Options:**
A) is only useful as smoke
B) blocks all rain forever
C) helps plants grow and stores water and nutrients
D) is a fossil fuel like petrol
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Healthy soil supports crops and forests. Erosion and chemical waste damage this resource.

### Q17
**Stem:** Loudspeakers at very high volume late at night near homes can cause —
**Options:**
A) water purification
B) soil fertility
C) noise pollution
D) more rainfall
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Unwanted loud sound that disturbs people and animals is noise pollution.
