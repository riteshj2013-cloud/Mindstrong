## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: What Is an Angle?
- **tts**:
  - Open a book a little. Can you see the corner the two covers make? That is an angle!
  - An angle has two arms and one meeting point called the vertex.
- **on_screen**: Animated book opening; the two covers glow as "arms" and the spine glows as "vertex". Child taps the vertex.
- **check**: In angle ABC, which point is the vertex? — Answer: B

### step_2: Measuring Angles in Degrees
- **tts**:
  - We measure how wide an angle opens in degrees.
  - A square corner is ninety degrees. Half of it is forty-five degrees, and a straight line is one hundred eighty degrees.
- **on_screen**: A slider opens two arms; benchmark markers light up at 45°, 90° and 180°. Child drags the arm to make 90°.
- **check**: Drag the arm to show half of a right angle. — Answer: 45°

### step_3: Types of Angles
- **tts**:
  - Smaller than a square corner is acute. Exactly a square corner is right.
  - Wider than right but less than straight is obtuse. Bigger than straight is reflex.
- **on_screen**: Sorting game with four bins (Acute, Right, Obtuse, Straight/Reflex); angle cards of 30°, 90°, 140°, 180°, 220° slide in to be sorted.
- **check**: Which bin does 140° go into? — Answer: Obtuse

### step_4: Turning Clockwise and Anticlockwise
- **tts**:
  - Clockwise means turning the same way as clock hands. Anticlockwise is the opposite way.
  - A quarter turn is ninety degrees, a half turn is one hundred eighty, and a full turn is three hundred sixty.
- **on_screen**: A child avatar stands on a compass (N, E, S, W). Child taps "Quarter turn clockwise" or "anticlockwise" buttons and watches the avatar turn.
- **check**: Facing North, make a quarter turn clockwise. Where do you face? — Answer: East

### step_5: Angles on a Clock
- **tts**:
  - A clock has twelve numbers around a full turn, so each gap is thirty degrees.
  - At three o'clock the hands make a right angle. Can you find others?
- **on_screen**: Interactive clock; child sets times like 2:00, 3:00, 4:00 and the angle between the hands is shown and labelled.
- **check**: What angle do the hands make at 2 o'clock? — Answer: 60°

### step_6: Open Shapes, Closed Shapes and Polygons
- **tts**:
  - A closed shape has no gaps, like a fence that goes all the way round.
  - Closed shapes made of straight sides are polygons. Count the sides to name them!
- **on_screen**: Shape gallery (letter C, triangle, wavy line, pentagon, hexagon). Child first taps closed shapes, then matches each polygon to its name by counting sides.
- **check**: A closed shape has 5 straight sides. What is it? — Answer: Pentagon

### step_7: Putting Angles Together
- **tts**:
  - Two right angles side by side make a straight angle of one hundred eighty degrees.
  - All the angles around a point add up to three hundred sixty degrees.
- **on_screen**: Child drags angle pieces (90°, 90°, 60°, 120°) to fill a straight line and then a full circle; a meter shows the running total.
- **check**: A straight line is split into 100° and one more angle. What is the missing angle? — Answer: 80°

## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: An angle measures 35°. What type of angle is it?
- **options**:
  - A) Obtuse
  - B) Right
  - C) Acute
  - D) Straight
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Any angle greater than 0° and less than 90° is acute, and 35° is well below 90°.
- **skill**: angle_type
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: What angle is formed at the corner of a rectangular notebook page?
- **options**:
  - A) Right angle
  - B) Acute angle
  - C) Obtuse angle
  - D) Straight angle
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: The corners of a rectangle are square corners, and a square corner is a right angle of 90°.
- **skill**: angle_type
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: How many degrees are there in a straight angle?
- **options**:
  - A) 90°
  - B) 360°
  - C) 270°
  - D) 180°
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: A straight angle looks like a straight line and measures 180°, which is two right angles.
- **skill**: measure
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: What is the point where the two arms of an angle meet called?
- **options**:
  - A) Arm
  - B) Vertex
  - C) Side
  - D) Base
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The two arms of an angle start from one common point, and that point is called the vertex.
- **skill**: arms_vertex
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: What is a closed shape with 6 straight sides called?
- **options**:
  - A) Pentagon
  - B) Hexagon
  - C) Octagon
  - D) Quadrilateral
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: "Hexa" means six, so a hexagon has 6 sides, while a pentagon has only 5.
- **skill**: polygon
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: Which of these is a closed shape?
- **options**:
  - A) The letter C
  - B) The letter U
  - C) A wavy line
  - D) A circle with no gaps
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: A shape is closed when its boundary has no gap, which happens only when the pencil ends where it began.
- **skill**: open_closed
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: An angle measures 125°. What type of angle is it?
- **options**:
  - A) Obtuse
  - B) Right
  - C) Acute
  - D) Reflex
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: An obtuse angle is more than 90° but less than 180°, and 125° lies in that range.
- **skill**: angle_type
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q08
- **stem**: At exactly 3 o'clock, what angle do the hour hand and minute hand of a clock make?
- **options**:
  - A) 45°
  - B) 120°
  - C) 90°
  - D) 180°
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: The minute hand points to 12 and the hour hand to 3, which is a quarter of the clock face, or 90°.
- **skill**: clock_angle
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q09
- **stem**: What is the smallest number of straight sides a polygon can have?
- **options**:
  - A) 1
  - B) 2
  - C) 4
  - D) 3
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Two straight sides cannot close a shape, so the smallest polygon is a triangle with 3 sides.
- **skill**: polygon
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q10
- **stem**: How many degrees are there in a half turn?
- **options**:
  - A) 90°
  - B) 180°
  - C) 270°
  - D) 360°
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A full turn is 360°, so half a turn is 180°.
- **skill**: turns
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q11
- **stem**: An angle measures 200°. What type of angle is it?
- **options**:
  - A) Reflex
  - B) Straight
  - C) Obtuse
  - D) Acute
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: An angle greater than 180° but less than 360° is a reflex angle, so 200° is not obtuse.
- **skill**: angle_type
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q12
- **stem**: The minute hand of a clock moves clockwise from 12 to 9. Through how many degrees has it turned?
- **options**:
  - A) 90°
  - B) 180°
  - C) 270°
  - D) 360°
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Going clockwise from 12 passes 3, 6 and then 9, which is three quarter turns, or 270°, while 90° is the anticlockwise trap.
- **skill**: turns
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q13
- **stem**: Ravi is facing North. He turns 90° clockwise. Which direction is he facing now?
- **options**:
  - A) North
  - B) West
  - C) South
  - D) East
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Clockwise from North goes to East first, while West would be the result of an anticlockwise turn.
- **skill**: turns
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q14
- **stem**: Meena places two right angles side by side so they share one arm. What angle do the two outer arms make?
- **options**:
  - A) Acute angle
  - B) Straight angle
  - C) Right angle
  - D) Reflex angle
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Two right angles together make 90° + 90° = 180°, which is a straight angle.
- **skill**: combine_angles
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q15
- **stem**: What is the smaller angle between the hands of a clock at exactly 4 o'clock?
- **options**:
  - A) 90°
  - B) 100°
  - C) 120°
  - D) 150°
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Each gap between two numbers on a clock is 30°, and from 12 to 4 there are 4 gaps, so the angle is 4 × 30° = 120°.
- **skill**: clock_angle
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q16
- **stem**: In angle PQR, which point is the vertex?
- **options**:
  - A) Q
  - B) P
  - C) R
  - D) Both P and R
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: The middle letter in an angle's name always names the vertex, so Q is the vertex of angle PQR.
- **skill**: naming
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q17
- **stem**: A cupboard door is opened exactly halfway between fully closed and a right angle. What angle does the door make with the cupboard?
- **options**:
  - A) 30°
  - B) 135°
  - C) 60°
  - D) 45°
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Half of a right angle is 90° ÷ 2 = 45°.
- **skill**: measure
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q18
- **stem**: Asha draws one pentagon and one quadrilateral. How many sides has she drawn in all?
- **options**:
  - A) 7
  - B) 8
  - C) 9
  - D) 10
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A pentagon has 5 sides and a quadrilateral has 4, so together they have 5 + 4 = 9 sides.
- **skill**: polygon
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q19
- **stem**: A straight angle is split into two angles by a ray. One of them is 70°. What is the other?
- **options**:
  - A) 20°
  - B) 110°
  - C) 100°
  - D) 290°
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The two parts must add up to 180°, so the other angle is 180° − 70° = 110°.
- **skill**: combine_angles
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q20
- **stem**: At exactly 8 o'clock, what is the REFLEX angle between the hands of a clock?
- **options**:
  - A) 120°
  - B) 160°
  - C) 200°
  - D) 240°
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: The smaller angle is 4 gaps × 30° = 120°, so the reflex angle is 360° − 120° = 240°.
- **skill**: clock_angle
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q21
- **stem**: Sita faces East. She turns 90° anticlockwise and then 180° clockwise. Which direction is she facing now?
- **options**:
  - A) South
  - B) North
  - C) West
  - D) East
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Turning anticlockwise from East brings her to North, and a half turn from North brings her to South.
- **skill**: turns
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q22
- **stem**: Three angles together make a full turn around a point. Two of them are 90° and 150°. What is the third angle?
- **options**:
  - A) 60°
  - B) 140°
  - C) 120°
  - D) 210°
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Angles around a point add up to 360°, so the third angle is 360° − 90° − 150° = 120°.
- **skill**: combine_angles
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q23
- **stem**: A toy robot turns 45° at a time. How many such turns does it need to make a three-quarter turn?
- **options**:
  - A) 3
  - B) 6
  - C) 4
  - D) 8
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A three-quarter turn is 270°, and 270° ÷ 45° = 6 turns, while 8 turns would make a full turn.
- **skill**: turns
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q24
- **stem**: Through how many degrees does the minute hand turn between 2:00 and 2:20?
- **options**:
  - A) 20°
  - B) 100°
  - C) 140°
  - D) 120°
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: In 20 minutes the minute hand moves from 12 to 4, which is 4 gaps × 30° = 120°, not 20°.
- **skill**: clock_angle
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q22
- **stem**: Three angles lie together on a straight line. Two of them are equal and the third is 40°. What is the size of each equal angle?
- **options**:
  - A) 50°
  - B) 140°
  - C) 80°
  - D) 70°
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: The angles add up to 180°, so the two equal angles share 180° − 40° = 140°, which gives 70° each.
- **skill**: combine_angles
- **difficulty**: hard

- **explanation**: A straight angle is 180°, and 180° ÷ 30° = 6 turns, while 12 turns would make a full turn.
- **skill**: turns
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q24

- **explanation**: At 9:30 the minute hand is on 6 and the hour hand is halfway between 9 and 10, so the gap is 3½ × 30° = 105°.
- **skill**: clock_angle
- **difficulty**: hard
