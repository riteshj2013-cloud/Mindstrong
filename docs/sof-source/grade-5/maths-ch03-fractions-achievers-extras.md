- **options**:
  - A) 3/5
  - B) 29/48
  - C) 1 1/24
  - D) 1 5/24
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Slab 1 has 8 pieces and 3 are eaten, so 5/8 is left. Slab 2 has 12 pieces and 5 are eaten, so 7/12 is left. In twenty-fourths, 5/8 = 15/24 and 7/12 = 14/24, so 29/24 = 1 5/24 slabs are left. 3/5 is tempting because it counts 12 pieces left out of 20 pieces in all, but a piece of Slab 1 is an eighth of a slab while a piece of Slab 2 is only a twelfth, so the pieces cannot be counted together.
- **hint**: Write what is left of each of Dhriti's slabs as a fraction, then change both to the same denominator before adding.

### Q02
- **id**: g5-maths-ch03-ach-x-02
- **stem**: The water tank in Divyansh's building holds 240 L when full. On Monday, 1/4 of the full tank is used. On Tuesday, 2/3 of the water left after Monday is used. How many litres are left in the tank now?
- **options**:
  - A) 20 L
  - B) 60 L
  - C) 120 L
  - D) 40 L
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Monday uses 1/4 of 240 L = 60 L, leaving 180 L. Tuesday uses 2/3 of 180 L = 120 L, leaving 180 L − 120 L = 60 L. 20 L is tempting because it takes 1/4 + 2/3 = 11/12 of the full tank, but Tuesday's 2/3 is a fraction of the 180 L that was left, not of the full tank.
- **hint**: Find how much water stayed in Divyansh's tank after Monday first; Tuesday's fraction is of that amount, not of the full tank.
- **skill**: fraction_of_the_remainder
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers

### Q03
- **id**: g5-maths-ch03-ach-x-03
- **stem**: Jash's tulsi plant was 2/5 m tall in June and 1/2 m tall in August. In July its height was exactly halfway between those two heights. How tall was the plant in July?
- **options**:
  - A) 3/7 m
  - B) 9/10 m
  - C) 9/20 m
  - D) 1/10 m
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Write both heights in twentieths: 2/5 = 8/20 and 1/2 = 10/20. Exactly halfway between 8/20 and 10/20 is 9/20, so the plant was 9/20 m tall. 1/10 m is tempting because 10/20 − 8/20 = 2/20 = 1/10 is the gap between the two heights, but the July height is the June height plus half of that gap: 8/20 + 1/20 = 9/20 m.
- **hint**: Rewrite the June and August heights of Jash's plant with the same denominator, then find the fraction in the middle.
- **skill**: fraction_exactly_halfway
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers

### Q05
- **id**: g5-maths-ch03-ach-x-05
- **stem**: Mahi used 1/3 of a box of rangoli colour on Diwali and 1/4 of the same box on Bhai Dooj. Now 50 g of colour is left in the box. How much colour did the full box hold?
- **options**:
  - A) 70 g
  - B) 120 g
  - C) 100 g
  - D) 150 g
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Mahi used 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12 of the box, so 5/12 is left. If 5/12 of the box is 50 g, then 1/12 is 10 g and the full box held 12 × 10 = 120 g. 70 g is tempting because it uses 1/3 + 1/4 = 2/7 (adding tops and bottoms), so 5/7 would be 50 g, but unlike fractions must be changed to twelfths before adding.
- **hint**: Add the fractions Mahi used on the two festival days using twelfths, then find what part of the box the colour left makes.
- **skill**: whole_from_the_part_left
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers
