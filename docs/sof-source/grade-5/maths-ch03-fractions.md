## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Equal Parts of a Whole
- **tts**:
  - Imagine sharing one roti with three friends. To be fair, every piece must be the same size.
  - A fraction tells us about equal parts of a whole.
- **on_screen**: A roti is shown cut two ways: four equal pieces and four unequal pieces. Child taps the roti that is fairly shared.
- **check**: Is a roti cut into 4 pieces of different sizes showing quarters? — Answer: No, the parts must be equal

### step_2: Numerator and Denominator
- **tts**:
  - The number at the bottom, the denominator, tells how many equal parts the whole has.
  - The number on top, the numerator, tells how many parts we are talking about.
- **on_screen**: A chocolate bar with 8 equal pieces; child taps pieces to shade them, and the fraction (for example 3/8) updates live with the top and bottom labelled.
- **check**: Shade 3 of the 8 pieces. What fraction is shaded? — Answer: 3/8

### step_3: Unit, Proper, Improper and Mixed Fractions
- **tts**:
  - A unit fraction has one on top, like one by five.
  - When the top is bigger than the bottom, we have more than one whole, which can be written as a mixed number.
- **on_screen**: Cards 1/5, 3/4, 7/4 and 1 3/4 are sorted into bins; then 7 quarter-pieces are dragged to fill circles, showing 7/4 = 1 whole and 3/4.
- **check**: Write 7/4 as a mixed number. — Answer: 1 3/4

### step_4: Equivalent Fractions and Simplest Form
- **tts**:
  - Half a pizza is the same as two out of four slices, or four out of eight slices.
  - To simplify, divide the top and bottom by the same number until you cannot divide anymore.
- **on_screen**: Three identical pizzas cut into 2, 4 and 8 slices with the same amount shaded. Then a "divide both" button simplifies 6/8 step by step.
- **check**: Write 6/8 in simplest form. — Answer: 3/4

### step_5: Comparing Fractions
- **tts**:
  - If the bottoms are the same, the bigger top wins.
  - If the tops are the same, the smaller bottom wins, because the pieces are bigger!
- **on_screen**: Two pairs of bars: 5/9 vs 4/9 and 1/5 vs 1/8. Child taps the larger fraction in each pair, and the bars show why.
- **check**: Which is greater, 1/5 or 1/8? — Answer: 1/5

### step_6: Fractions on a Number Line
- **tts**:
  - Between zero and one, we can make equal jumps.
  - If there are six equal jumps, each jump is one by six.
- **on_screen**: A number line from 0 to 1 split into 6 equal parts; a frog hops along it and the child places the flag at 4/6.
- **check**: The line from 0 to 1 has 4 equal parts. What fraction is at the 3rd mark? — Answer: 3/4

### step_7: Adding and Subtracting Like Fractions
- **tts**:
  - When the bottoms are the same, just add or take away the tops.
  - The bottom stays the same, because the size of each piece does not change.
- **on_screen**: Two jugs filled 2/7 and 3/7 are poured into one 7-part jug; a warning pops up if the child tries to add the denominators.
- **check**: What is 2/7 + 3/7? — Answer: 5/7

### step_8: Fraction of a Collection and Word Problems
- **tts**:
  - To find two-fifths of twenty laddoos, first split them into five equal groups.
  - Then take two of those groups. That makes eight laddoos!
- **on_screen**: 20 laddoos are dragged into 5 plates; child selects 2 plates and the count shows 8. Then a short story about a cricket over has the child solve a question.
- **check**: A bowler has bowled 3 of the 6 balls in an over. What fraction of the over is done, in simplest form? — Answer: 1/2

## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: A roti is cut into 4 equal pieces and Ravi eats 1 piece. What fraction of the roti did he eat?
- **options**:
  - A) 1/3
  - B) 1/4
  - C) 3/4
  - D) 4/1
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: He ate 1 out of 4 equal pieces, so the fraction is 1/4, while 1/3 wrongly compares the eaten piece with the uneaten pieces.
- **skill**: fraction_basics
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: What is the denominator of the fraction 5/8?
- **options**:
  - A) 5
  - B) 3
  - C) 13
  - D) 8
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: The denominator is the bottom number, which tells how many equal parts the whole has, so it is 8.
- **skill**: numerator_denominator
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: A chocolate bar has 10 equal pieces and 3 pieces are eaten. What fraction of the bar is left?
- **options**:
  - A) 7/10
  - B) 3/10
  - C) 7/3
  - D) 10/7
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 10 − 3 = 7 pieces are left out of 10 equal pieces, so 7/10 is left, while 3/10 is the part eaten.
- **skill**: fraction_basics
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: Which of these is a unit fraction?
- **options**:
  - A) 9/1
  - B) 2/9
  - C) 1/9
  - D) 9/9
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A unit fraction has 1 as its numerator, and among these only 1/9 does.
- **skill**: fraction_types
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: Which of these shows exactly 1/2 shaded?
- **options**:
  - A) A square cut into one big part and one small part, with the small part shaded
  - B) A circle cut into 3 equal parts, with 1 part shaded
  - C) A circle cut into 2 equal parts, with 1 part shaded
  - D) A rectangle cut into 4 equal parts, with 1 part shaded
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: A half means 1 of 2 EQUAL parts, so a shape cut into unequal parts does not show 1/2.
- **skill**: fraction_basics
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: What is 2/7 + 3/7?
- **options**:
  - A) 5/7
  - B) 5/14
  - C) 6/7
  - D) 1/7
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: For like fractions we add only the numerators and keep the denominator, so 2/7 + 3/7 = 5/7, not 5/14.
- **skill**: add_subtract
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: Which is greater: 5/9 or 4/9?
- **options**:
  - A) 4/9
  - B) They cannot be compared
  - C) Both are equal
  - D) 5/9
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: When the denominators are the same, the fraction with the bigger numerator is greater, so 5/9 is greater.
- **skill**: compare
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q08
- **stem**: Which fraction is equivalent to 1/2?
- **options**:
  - A) 2/3
  - B) 3/6
  - C) 1/4
  - D) 2/6
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Multiplying both parts of 1/2 by 3 gives 3/6, so they show the same amount.
- **skill**: equivalent
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q09
- **stem**: What is 1/4 of 20?
- **options**:
  - A) 4
  - B) 80
  - C) 16
  - D) 5
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: To find 1/4 of 20, split 20 into 4 equal groups, and each group has 20 ÷ 4 = 5.
- **skill**: fraction_of_collection
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q10
- **stem**: Which of these is an improper fraction?
- **options**:
  - A) 7/4
  - B) 4/7
  - C) 3/4
  - D) 1/4
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: An improper fraction has a numerator bigger than or equal to its denominator, and 7 is bigger than 4.
- **skill**: fraction_types
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q11
- **stem**: What is 12/18 in its simplest form?
- **options**:
  - A) 6/9
  - B) 4/6
  - C) 2/3
  - D) 3/2
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Dividing both 12 and 18 by their biggest common factor, 6, gives 2/3, while 6/9 and 4/6 are only partly simplified.
- **skill**: simplest_form
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q12
- **stem**: Which is greater: 1/5 or 1/8?
- **options**:
  - A) 1/8
  - B) 1/5
  - C) Both are equal
  - D) They cannot be compared
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: With the same numerator, the smaller denominator means bigger pieces, so 1/5 is greater even though 8 is bigger than 5.
- **skill**: compare
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q13
- **stem**: In a class of 30 students, 2/5 wear glasses. How many students wear glasses?
- **options**:
  - A) 6
  - B) 18
  - C) 15
  - D) 12
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: 1/5 of 30 is 6, so 2/5 of 30 is 2 × 6 = 12, while 6 is the trap of stopping at 1/5.
- **skill**: fraction_of_collection
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q14
- **stem**: What is 9/10 − 4/10 in its simplest form?
- **options**:
  - A) 13/20
  - B) 5/20
  - C) 1/2
  - D) 1/5
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: 9/10 − 4/10 = 5/10, which simplifies to 1/2 when we divide the top and bottom by 5.
- **skill**: add_subtract
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q15
- **stem**: Which improper fraction is equal to the mixed number 2 1/3?
- **options**:
  - A) 7/3
  - B) 5/3
  - C) 6/3
  - D) 3/7
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 2 wholes make 6 thirds, and 1 more third gives 7/3, while 5/3 wrongly adds 2 + 3 for the top.
- **skill**: fraction_types
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q16

  - D) 6/4

- **difficulty**: medium

### Q17
- **stem**: Which list shows 3/11, 8/11 and 5/11 from smallest to largest?

  - A) 8/11, 5/11, 3/11
  - B) 3/11, 8/11, 5/11
  - C) 5/11, 3/11, 8/11
  - D) 3/11, 5/11, 8/11

### Q18
- **stem**: Which number goes in the box: 3/4 = ☐/20?

  - A) 12
  - B) 15

- **difficulty**: medium

### Q19
- **stem**: A cricket over has 6 balls. A bowler has bowled 4 balls of the over. What fraction of the over has he bowled, in simplest form?
- **options**:
  - A) 4/10
  - B) 1/3
  - C) 2/3
  - D) 3/2
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: He has bowled 4 of 6 balls, which is 4/6 = 2/3, while 1/3 is the part still left.
- **skill**: word_problem
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q20
- **stem**: A rangoli uses 48 diyas. 1/4 of them are red, 1/3 are blue and the rest are yellow. How many diyas are yellow?
- **options**:
  - A) 20
  - B) 16
  - C) 24
  - D) 28
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Red is 48 ÷ 4 = 12 and blue is 48 ÷ 3 = 16, so yellow is 48 − 12 − 16 = 20.
- **skill**: word_problem
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q21
- **stem**: Which of these fractions is the greatest?
- **options**:
  - A) 2/3
  - B) 5/8
  - C) 7/12
  - D) 3/4
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Writing all of them with denominator 24 gives 18/24, 15/24, 14/24 and 16/24, so 3/4 is the greatest.
- **skill**: compare
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q22
- **stem**: In a class of 40 students, 3/8 are girls. How many more boys than girls are there?
- **options**:
  - A) 10
  - B) 5
  - C) 15
  - D) 25
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: There are 3/8 × 40 = 15 girls and 40 − 15 = 25 boys, so there are 25 − 15 = 10 more boys.
- **skill**: word_problem
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q23
- **stem**: Meera ate 2/9 of a cake and her brother ate 4/9 of it. What fraction of the cake is left, in simplest form?
- **options**:
  - A) 2/3
  - B) 6/9
  - C) 1/3
  - D) 3/18
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Together they ate 6/9, so 9/9 − 6/9 = 3/9 is left, which simplifies to 1/3, while 2/3 is the part eaten.
- **skill**: add_subtract
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q24
- **stem**: Which fraction lies between 1/3 and 1/2 on the number line?
- **options**:
  - A) 1/4
  - B) 5/12
  - C) 2/3
  - D) 3/5
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: In twelfths, 1/3 = 4/12 and 1/2 = 6/12, so 5/12 lies between them.
- **skill**: number_line
- **difficulty**: hard
