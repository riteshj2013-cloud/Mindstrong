## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Nutrition
- **tts**: Autotrophs make food; heterotrophs consume it. Digestion breaks food for absorption.
- **check**: Where does starch digestion begin? — Answer: Mouth

### step_2: Respiration & transport
- **tts**: Oxygen helps release energy from food. Blood and xylem/phloem move materials.
- **check**: Where is oxygen mainly absorbed in lungs? — Answer: Alveoli

### step_3: Excretion
- **tts**: Kidneys filter blood; nephrons make urine.
- **check**: Main nitrogenous waste in humans? — Answer: Urea

## Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: The process of obtaining and using food in living organisms is called —
- **options**:
  - A) phototropism
  - B) nutrition
  - C) transpiration only
  - D) excretion only
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Nutrition covers intake and utilisation of food.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q02
- **stem**: Autotrophs —
- **options**:
  - A) lack chlorophyll always
  - B) only eat animals
  - C) cannot use sunlight

- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Green plants and some bacteria fix energy into food.
