## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Reflection
- **tts**: Angle in equals angle out. Plane mirrors flip left and right.
- **check**: i = 30°, r = ? — Answer: 30°

### step_2: Spherical mirrors & lenses
- **tts**: Concave mirrors and convex lenses can converge light to real images.
- **check**: f related to R for a spherical mirror? — Answer: f = R/2

### step_3: Refraction & eyes
- **tts**: Light bends when speed changes. Lenses correct myopia and hypermetropia.
- **check**: Myopia corrected by? — Answer: Concave lens

## Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: The angle of incidence equals the angle of reflection. This is —
- **options**:
  - A) a law of reflection
  - B) Ohm's law
  - C) Snell's law only
  - D) Hooke's law
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: i = r, and incident ray, reflected ray, normal are coplanar.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q02
- **stem**: A ray along the normal to a plane mirror reflects —
- **options**:
  - A) at 90° to the normal
  - B) parallel to the mirror
  - C) not at all
  - D) back on itself
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: i = 0 ⇒ r = 0.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q03
- **stem**: Image in a plane mirror is —
- **options**:
  - A) smaller always
  - B) real and inverted
  - C) magnified always
  - D) virtual, erect, same size, laterally inverted
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Plane mirrors make virtual, same-size, left-right flipped images.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q04
- **stem**: Focal length f of a spherical mirror relates to radius R by —
- **options**:
  - A) f = R²
  - B) f = 2R
  - C) f = R/2
  - D) f = R
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: For spherical mirrors (paraxial), f = R/2.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q05
- **stem**: A concave mirror can form a real image when the object is —
- **options**:
  - A) inside the mirror material
  - B) beyond the focus
  - C) at the pole
  - D) between pole and focus only
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Object outside F can give real inverted images on a screen.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q06
- **stem**: Magnification m = h′/h equals —
- **options**:
  - A) u/v always positive only
  - B) f/R
  - C) −v/u for mirrors (sign convention)
  - D) R/f
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: m = h′/h = −v/u in the New Cartesian convention for mirrors.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q07
- **stem**: A ray through the centre of curvature of a concave mirror —
- **options**:
  - A) reflects back on itself
  - B) stops
  - C) passes through focus only after
  - D) grazes the pole without reflecting
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: It hits along the normal (radius), so i=0.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q08
- **stem**: Refraction is —
- **options**:
  - A) bouncing from a mirror
  - B) charging of electrons
  - C) bending of light when speed changes across a boundary
  - D) sound echo
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Light changes direction with optical density change.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q09
- **stem**: Absolute refractive index n = —
- **options**:
  - A) v/c
  - B) 1/c
  - C) c/v
  - D) c×v
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: n = speed in vacuum / speed in medium.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q10
- **stem**: Snell's law: n₁ sin i = —
- **options**:
  - A) n₁ / n₂
  - B) n₂ sin r
  - C) n₂ / sin r
  - D) sin i / n₂
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: n₁ sin i = n₂ sin r at a boundary.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q11
- **stem**: A convex lens is a —
- **options**:
  - A) diverging lens
  - B) converging lens
  - C) plane mirror
  - D) prism only
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Thicker in the middle; brings parallel rays to a focus.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q12
- **stem**: A concave lens is a —
- **options**:
  - A) optical fibre core only
  - B) diverging lens
  - C) spherical mirror
  - D) converging lens
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Thinner in the middle; spreads parallel rays.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q13
- **stem**: Power of a lens P = —
- **options**:
  - A) R/2
  - B) 1/f (f in metres), unit dioptre
  - C) f in cm
  - D) v−u
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: P = 1/f with f in m; unit D (dioptre).
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q14
- **stem**: Total internal reflection needs —
- **options**:
  - A) a rough surface
  - B) no boundary
  - C) light in denser medium, i > critical angle
  - D) always i = 0
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: From denser to rarer, beyond critical angle, light reflects fully.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q15
- **stem**: Critical angle is the incidence angle in the denser medium for which —
- **options**:
  - A) refraction angle in rarer medium is 90°
  - B) n becomes 1
  - C) reflection is zero

- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: At critical angle, refracted ray grazes the surface.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q16

  - A) only diffraction in air without water

- **explanation**: Droplets refract, reflect, and disperse sunlight.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q17

- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q18

### Q19

### Q20

### Q21
- **stem**: A real image —
- **options**:
  - A) needs no light
  - B) cannot exist for lenses

### Q22
- **stem**: Lateral inversion means —
- **options**:
  - A) left-right reversal in a plane mirror image
  - B) colour change
  - C) upside-down always
  - D) magnification >1 always
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Your left hand appears as the image's right.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q23
- **stem**: If u = −30 cm, f = −20 cm for a concave mirror, v equals —
- **options**:
  - A) −60 cm
  - B) −12 cm
  - C) +60 cm
  - D) +30 cm
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 1/v = 1/f − 1/u = −1/20 + 1/30 = −1/60 → v=−60 cm (real).
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q24
- **stem**: White light splits into colours through a prism because of —
- **options**:

- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Different wavelengths refract by different amounts.
- **difficulty**: mixed
