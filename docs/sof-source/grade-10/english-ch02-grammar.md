## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Tense and aspect
- **tts**: Match the verb form to time and whether the action is complete or ongoing.
- **check**: Yesterday → which tense family? — Answer: Past

### step_2: Voice and report
- **tts**: Passive moves the object forward. Reported speech often steps the tense back.
- **check**: "I am late," she said → she said she ___ late. — Answer: was

### step_3: Agreement and patterns
- **tts**: Singular subjects need singular verbs; learn fixed prepositions and conditionals.
- **check**: The news ___ good. — Answer: is

## Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Choose the correct form: She ____ to the market yesterday.
- **options**:
  - A) went
  - B) go
  - C) gone
  - D) going
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Past simple for a finished time (yesterday).
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q02
- **stem**: Identify the tense: "They have finished the project."
- **options**:
  - A) present perfect
  - B) past perfect
  - C) simple past
  - D) future continuous
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: have/has + past participle = present perfect.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q03
- **stem**: Passive of "The chef cooks the meal" is —
- **options**:
  - A) The meal is cooked by the chef
  - B) The meal cooked the chef
  - C) The chef is cooked by the meal
  - D) Meal cooks
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Object becomes subject; be + V3 + by-agent.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q04
- **stem**: Reported speech: She said, "I am tired." → She said that she ____ tired.
- **options**:
  - A) was
  - B) is
  - C) am
  - D) were
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Backshift: present → past after a past reporting verb.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q05
- **stem**: Choose the correct determiner: ____ of the students were present.
- **options**:
  - A) Most
  - B) Much
  - C) Little
  - D) Each one of much
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Most of + plural countable works; much is for uncountables.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q06
- **stem**: Fill in: Neither of the answers ____ correct.
- **options**:
  - A) is

  - C) were being
  - D) have
- **answer**: A

- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q01
- **stem**: Tense: "I had left before she arrived" is —
- **options**:
  - A) past perfect + simple past
  - B) present perfect
  - C) future perfect

- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Earlier past = past perfect; later past = simple past.

### Q02

- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Present continuous passive: is/are being + V3.
