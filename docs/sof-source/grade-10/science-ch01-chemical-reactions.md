## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Evidence of a reaction
- **tts**: Colour change, gas, precipitate, or heat can signal a chemical change.
- **check**: Is melting ice chemical? — Answer: No

### step_2: Types of reactions
- **tts**: Combination, decomposition, displacement, double displacement.
- **check**: CaCO₃ heat → CaO + CO₂ is which type? — Answer: Decomposition

### step_3: Redox and corrosion
- **tts**: Oxidation and reduction travel together. Rust is slow oxidation.
- **check**: Does painting iron slow rust? — Answer: Yes

## Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: A chemical reaction in which heat is absorbed is called —
- **options**:
  - A) displacement
  - B) exothermic
  - C) neutralisation
  - D) endothermic
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Endothermic reactions take in heat from the surroundings.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q02
- **stem**: Rusting of iron is an example of —
- **options**:
  - A) sublimation
  - B) oxidation / corrosion
  - C) reduction only
  - D) distillation
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Iron reacts with oxygen and moisture to form hydrated iron oxide (rust).
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q03
- **stem**: In the reaction Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu, zinc —
- **options**:
  - A) acts as an acid
  - B) is reduced
  - C) sublimes
  - D) displaces copper
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: More reactive Zn displaces Cu from CuSO₄.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q04
- **stem**: Balanced equation for hydrogen burning in oxygen is —
- **options**:
  - A) H₂ + O → H₂O
  - B) H₂ + O₂ → H₂O
  - C) 2H₂ + O₂ → H₂O₂
  - D) 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Two H₂ molecules need one O₂ to make two H₂O.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q05
- **stem**: Quicklime reacts with water to give —
- **options**:
  - A) limestone
  - B) CO₂ only
  - C) CaO₂
  - D) slaked lime (Ca(OH)₂) with heat release
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: CaO + H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ is highly exothermic.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q06
- **stem**: Which is a decomposition reaction?
- **options**:
  - A) Fe + S → FeS
  - B) 2Pb(NO₃)₂ → 2PbO + 4NO₂ + O₂
  - C) NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H₂O
  - D) Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: One compound breaks into simpler products.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q07
- **stem**: Oxidation is —
- **options**:
  - A) only gain of hydrogen

  - C) gain of oxygen or loss of hydrogen/electrons

- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Classical and electronic definitions of oxidation.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q08

### Q09

### Q18
