## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Audience and purpose
- **tts**: Who reads this, and what should they do or feel?
- **check**: Letter to Commissioner → formal or informal? — Answer: Formal

### step_2: Forms
- **tts**: Letters, notices, articles, stories, and data paragraphs each have a shape.
- **check**: Notice needs name and designation? — Answer: Yes

### step_3: Revise
- **tts**: Check task points, then grammar and spelling.
- **check**: Plan before you draft? — Answer: Yes

## Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: A formal letter to the Municipal Commissioner should open with —
- **options**:
  - A) Hey!
  - B) Sir/Madam (or Respected Sir/Madam)
  - C) Yo
  - D) Dearest friend
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Formal register uses Sir/Madam.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q02
- **stem**: The subject line of a formal letter should be —
- **options**:
  - A) a long story
  - B) only emojis
  - C) omitted always
  - D) brief and specific
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: A clear subject helps the official file and act.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q03
- **stem**: Closing a formal letter to an unknown official: —
- **options**:
  - A) Yours faithfully
  - B) See ya
  - C) Yours lovingly

- **explanation**: Unknown recipient → Yours faithfully; named → Yours sincerely.
- **difficulty**: mixed
