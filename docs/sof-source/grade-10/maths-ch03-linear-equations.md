## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Graphs of lines
- **tts**: Each linear equation is a straight line. Solutions are common points.
- **check**: How many intersections can two distinct lines have? — Answer: 0 or 1

### step_2: Consistency conditions
- **tts**: Compare ratios of coefficients to classify the system.
- **check**: x+y=1 and 2x+2y=2 — Answer: Infinite solutions

### step_3: Algebraic methods
- **tts**: Substitution and elimination find the intersection without graphing.
- **check**: x+y=5, x−y=1 → x? — Answer: 3

## Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: The graph of a linear equation in two variables is —
- **options**:
  - A) a straight line
  - B) a parabola
  - C) a circle
  - D) a point only
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: ax+by+c=0 graphs as a straight line.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q02
- **stem**: The pair x + y = 5 and 2x + 2y = 10 has —
- **options**:
  - A) infinitely many solutions
  - B) no solution
  - C) unique solution
  - D) exactly two solutions
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Second is 2× the first: coincident lines.
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q03
- **stem**: The pair x + y = 5 and x + y = 7 has —
- **options**:
  - A) no solution
  - B) unique solution
  - C) infinitely many
  - D) x=0 only
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Parallel distinct lines (same left side, different constants).
- **difficulty**: mixed

### Q05

### Q14

### Q01
