# Chapter 3: Our Sense Organs

## Meta
- Grade: 3
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: sense organs, eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin, senses
- Source basis: NCERT Class 3 EVS / typical NSO themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 3 (short sentences, high visual/TTS)

## Learning objectives
- I can name our five sense organs: eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin.
- I can match each sense organ to its sense: see, hear, smell, taste and touch.
- I can name tastes like sweet, salty, sour and bitter, with a food for each.
- I can tell what my skin feels: hot, cold, soft, rough, a push and pain.
- I can share simple ways to keep my eyes, ears and hands safe and clean.
- I can explain how my senses help me stay safe and enjoy the world.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Meet Your Five Senses
- **Visual:** A smiling child in a sunny park. Five glowing labels pop up one by one: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, skin (hand).
- **TTS:** "We have five senses. They help us know the world around us."
- **Interaction:** Tap each glowing body part to hear its name. A star fills up after all five are tapped.

### Scene 2: Eyes See, Ears Hear
- **Visual:** A rainbow, a red ball and a blue kite on one side. A bell, a drum and a singing bird on the other.
- **TTS:** "Eyes help us see colours and shapes. Ears help us hear sounds."
- **Interaction:** Drag each picture to the eye basket or the ear basket. Tap the bird to hear it sing.

### Scene 3: Nose Smells, Tongue Tastes
- **Visual:** A jasmine flower, hot pakoras, a lemon, honey, a pinch of salt and a karela on a kitchen table.
- **TTS:** "Nose helps us smell. Tongue tastes sweet, salty, sour and bitter food."
- **Interaction:** Sort foods into four taste jars: Sweet (honey), Salty (salt), Sour (lemon), Bitter (karela). Tap the flower for a "smell" sparkle.

### Scene 4: Skin Feels
- **Visual:** A hand touching ice, a warm cup, soft cotton, rough tree bark and a gentle push from a friend.
- **TTS:** "Skin helps us feel hot, cold, soft, rough, a push and pain."
- **Interaction:** Mystery bag game. Tap the bag, read the clue ("It feels cold and wet"), and pick the right object.

### Scene 5: Keep Your Senses Safe
- **Visual:** A child washing hands, wiping the outer ear with a soft cloth, reading in good light, and covering ears near loud fireworks. A child smells smoke and calls a grown-up.
- **TTS:** "Keep senses safe. Never poke eyes or ears. Wash hands before eating."
- **Interaction:** Thumbs-up or thumbs-down cards: "Poke ear with a pencil" (down), "Wash hands before food" (up), "Very loud music all day" (down), "Tell an adult if you smell smoke" (up).

## Quiz Set A — 24 MCQs

### A1 (Easy)
**Stem:** Which sense organ helps us see?
- A. Ears
- B. Eyes
- C. Nose
- D. Tongue

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** We see colours, shapes and things with our eyes.

### A2 (Easy)
**Stem:** We hear music with our ______.
- A. Ears
- B. Skin
- C. Eyes
- D. Nose

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Ears catch sounds, so we can hear music.

### A3 (Easy)
**Stem:** Which organ helps you smell a rose?
- A. Tongue
- B. Ears
- C. Nose
- D. Eyes

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The nose helps us smell things like flowers.

### A4 (Easy)
**Stem:** Which organ tells you that a lemon is sour?
- A. Eyes
- B. Ears
- C. Nose
- D. Tongue

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** The tongue helps us taste. Lemon tastes sour.

### A5 (Easy)
**Stem:** Which sense organ covers our whole body?
- A. Skin
- B. Eyes
- C. Tongue
- D. Ears

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Skin covers our whole body. It helps us feel touch.

### A6 (Easy)
**Stem:** How many main sense organs do we have?
- A. Three
- B. Four
- C. Five
- D. Six

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** We have five: eyes, ears, nose, tongue and skin.

### A7 (Easy)
**Stem:** When you hold a piece of ice, your skin feels ______.
- A. hot
- B. cold
- C. sweet
- D. loud

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Ice is cold. Our skin feels the cold.

### A8 (Easy)
**Stem:** The sense of hearing goes with which organ?
- A. Nose
- B. Tongue
- C. Eyes
- D. Ears

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** We hear with our ears.

### A9 (Easy)
**Stem:** Which of these tastes sweet?
- A. Lemon
- B. Karela (bitter gourd)
- C. Honey
- D. Salt

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Honey is sweet. Lemon is sour, karela is bitter, salt is salty.

### A10 (Easy)
**Stem:** We should wash our hands before ______.
- A. eating food
- B. looking at the sky
- C. hearing a song
- D. smelling rain

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Clean hands keep germs away from our food.

### A11 (Medium)
**Stem:** Riya smells smoke coming from the kitchen. Which organ warned her?
- A. Eyes
- B. Ears
- C. Tongue
- D. Nose

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Her nose smelled the smoke. Then she can tell a grown-up.

### A12 (Medium)
**Stem:** What is the safe way to clean your ears?
- A. Poke inside with a pencil
- B. Gently wipe the outer ear with a soft cloth
- C. Pour hot water inside
- D. Use a hairpin

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Never put sharp things in the ear. Just wipe the outside gently.

### A13 (Medium)
**Stem:** A car horn honks behind you, so you step aside. Which sense helped you?
- A. Hearing
- B. Taste
- C. Smell
- D. Touch

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Your ears heard the horn. Hearing kept you safe.

### A14 (Medium)
**Stem:** You touch a hot cup and pull your hand back fast. Which sense helped you?
- A. Taste
- B. Smell
- C. Hearing
- D. Touch

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Your skin felt the heat. Touch helped you move away.

### A15 (Medium)
**Stem:** Which habit is good for your eyes?
- A. Rubbing them with dirty hands
- B. Looking straight at the Sun
- C. Reading in good light
- D. Reading in the dark

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Good light lets your eyes see without strain.

### A16 (Medium)
**Stem:** What taste does karela (bitter gourd) have?
- A. Sweet
- B. Bitter
- C. Salty
- D. Sour

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Karela tastes bitter. That is how it got its name, bitter gourd.

### A17 (Medium)
**Stem:** Which sense helps you tell a red ball from a blue ball?
- A. Touch
- B. Smell
- C. Hearing
- D. Sight

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Our eyes see colours. Both balls may feel the same.

### A18 (Medium)
**Stem:** Which of these can you NOT find out by smell?
- A. The colour of a flower
- B. Burnt toast
- C. A ripe mango
- D. Fresh soap

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** We see colour with our eyes, not our nose.

### A19 (Medium)
**Stem:** Very loud sounds for a long time can ______.
- A. make our ears sharper
- B. hurt our ears
- C. help our eyes see
- D. make food tasty

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Loud sounds can hurt our ears. Keep the sound low.

### A20 (Medium)
**Stem:** Which pair is matched correctly?
- A. Nose – taste
- B. Ears – see
- C. Skin – touch
- D. Tongue – hear

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** Skin helps us feel touch. The other pairs are mixed up.

### A21 (Hard)
**Stem:** When your nose is blocked from a cold, food seems less tasty. Why?
- A. Smell helps us taste food
- B. Our ears stop working
- C. Our eyes close
- D. Our skin gets cold

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Smell and taste work together. A blocked nose makes food taste less.

### A22 (Hard)
**Stem:** Some people who cannot see read raised dots called Braille. Which sense do they use?
- A. Sight
- B. Hearing
- C. Touch
- D. Taste

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** They feel the bumpy dots with their fingertips. That is touch.

### A23 (Hard)
**Stem:** Which group has ONLY things you can hear?
- A. Rainbow, star, moon
- B. Rose, soap, perfume
- C. Sugar, salt, lemon
- D. Bell, drum, whistle

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A bell, a drum and a whistle all make sounds.

### A24 (Hard)
**Stem:** Which sense organ can warn us that milk has gone bad, before we drink it?
- A. Ears
- B. Nose
- C. Knees
- D. Hair

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Bad milk smells sour. The nose warns us before we taste it.

- D. Eyes

### B19 (Medium)
**Stem:** Which sense helps you enjoy a bird's song?
- A. Hearing
- B. Taste
- C. Touch
- D. Smell

**Answer:** A

**Stem:** Why should our hands be clean before we eat?

- B. Dirty hands can carry germs into food

- A. Ears
- B. Tongue
- C. Nose
- D. Eyes

**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** The tongue tastes sweet. It can also feel cold.

### B22 (Hard)
**Stem:** Seema closes her eyes. She gets a bell, a rose, a lemon and a soft toy. Which can she find by hearing?
- A. The bell
- B. The rose
- C. The lemon
- D. The soft toy

**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A bell makes a sound when shaken. The others make no sound.

- A. Nose
- B. Tongue
- C. Ears
- D. Eyes

**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Our eyes see colour, shape and size together.

- A. Eating food that smells bad
- B. Touching a hot iron
- C. Smelling smoke and telling an adult
- D. Playing music very loud

**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** The nose smells smoke. Telling an adult keeps everyone safe.
