## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Equal groups
- **tts**:
  - Multiplying joins equal groups quickly. Four plates of six idlis become one total.
  - Repeated addition like five plus five plus five plus five is the same as five times four.
- **on_screen**: Four tiffin plates with six idlis each; tiles merge into 4 × 6.
- **check**: What is 4 × 6? — Answer: 24

### step_2: Arrays are rows times columns
- **tts**:
  - An array shows equal rows. Count one row, then multiply by the number of rows.
  - Three rows of seven ladoos make twenty-one.
- **on_screen**: Tray grid 3 by 7; rows and columns glow as 3 × 7.
- **check**: 3 rows of 7 = ? — Answer: 21

### step_3: Order can swap
- **tts**:
  - Eight times three equals three times eight. The product stays the same.
  - Swapping the factors does not change the total.
- **on_screen**: Two arrays flip: 8×3 and 3×8 both show 24.
- **check**: Which equals 8 × 3? — Answer: 3 × 8

### step_4: Times zero and times one
- **tts**:
  - Any number times one stays the same. Any number times zero is zero.
  - Seven groups of nothing is nothing.
- **on_screen**: Cards for 9 × 1 = 9 and 7 × 0 = 0.
- **check**: What is 7 × 0? — Answer: 0

### step_5: Skip-counting and times facts
- **tts**:
  - Skip-counting on a number line matches a times table.
  - Five jumps of five land on twenty-five — that is five times five.
- **on_screen**: Number line hops 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 with 5 × 5 label.
- **check**: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 is which multiplication? — Answer: 5 × 5

### step_6: Money stories with rupees
- **tts**:
  - Same price each time means multiply. Notebooks, tickets and sweets use rupees.
  - Remember the rupee sign — never dollars!
- **on_screen**: Kirana price tag ₹8; five notebooks total ₹40.
- **check**: 5 notebooks at ₹8 each = ? — Answer: ₹40

### step_7: Missing factors
- **tts**:
  - If seven times something makes fifty-six, think of the seven times table to find eight.
  - You can also think: how many sevens fit into fifty-six?
- **on_screen**: Blank factor card: 7 × ___ = 56.
- **check**: 7 × ___ = 56. What fills the blank? — Answer: 8

### step_8: Two-step stories
- **tts**:
  - Some stories multiply first, then take some away. Read carefully and do one step at a time.
  - You are ready for Set A and Set B!
- **on_screen**: Crayon story: 3 packs of 6, give 4 away; child taps each step.
- **check**: 3 × 6 − 4 = ? — Answer: 14

## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: What is 6 × 4?
- **options**:
  - A) 24
  - B) 10
  - C) 28
  - D) 20
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 6 groups of 4 make 24. Or skip-count: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24.
- **skill**: times_table
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: Which multiplication matches 5 + 5 + 5 + 5?
- **options**:
  - A) 5 × 3
  - B) 5 × 4
  - C) 4 × 4
  - D) 5 + 4
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Five is added four times, so 5 × 4 = 20. Multiplication is a short way to write repeated addition.
- **skill**: repeated_addition
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: A tray has 3 rows of 7 ladoos. How many ladoos are there in all?

- **explanation**: An array of 3 rows with 7 in each row is 3 × 7 = 21 ladoos.
- **skill**: arrays
- **difficulty**: easy
