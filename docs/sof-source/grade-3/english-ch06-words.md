## Interactive Lesson Outline

### step_1: Spelling Patterns
- **tts**:
  - Some words have double letters, like rabbit and letter.
  - When we add -ing, a silent e often drops: ride becomes riding. A short word like hop doubles its last letter: hopping.
- **on_screen**: Letter blocks build 'ride' + 'ing'. The e pops off like a bubble to make 'riding'. Then 'hop' + 'ing' grows a second p to make 'hopping'.
- **check**: Add -ing to 'smile'. — Answer: smiling

### step_2: Sound-Alike Words
- **tts**:
  - Some words sound the same but have different spellings and meanings.
  - Sun and son sound alike. The sun shines; a son is a boy in a family.
- **on_screen**: Two cards flip: a bright sun with the word 'sun', and a family picture with the word 'son'. The child drags each word into the right sentence.
- **check**: 'The ___ is shining.' Which word fits: sun or son? — Answer: sun

### step_3: Odd One Out and Word Pairs
- **tts**:
  - In an odd one out, three words belong to one group and one does not.
  - In a word pair, find how the first two words are linked, then make the same link.
- **on_screen**: Cards 'red, blue, green, chair' appear; 'chair' wobbles and drops out. Then 'Fish is to water as bird is to ___' fills in with 'air'.
- **check**: Which is the odd one out: rose, lily, lotus, spoon? — Answer: spoon

### step_4: Capital Letters and End Marks
- **tts**:
  - Begin every sentence, and every name of a person, place or day, with a capital letter.
  - End telling sentences with a full stop, questions with a question mark, and strong feelings with an exclamation mark.
- **on_screen**: The sentence 'on sunday we visited agra' gets three capital letters dropped into place. Then three cards show '.', '?' and '!' sliding onto different sentences.
- **check**: Which mark ends this: 'Where is my bag'? — Answer: a question mark (?)

### step_5: Commas, Apostrophes and Speech Marks
- **tts**:
  - Commas separate things in a list, like pens, pencils and paints.
  - An apostrophe shows a missing letter, as in can't, or who owns something, as in Rani's book. Speech marks hold the words someone says.
- **on_screen**: A list 'peas carrots beans' gets one comma. 'can not' squeezes into 'can't'. Then speech marks open and close around 'Hello!' in 'Ravi said, "Hello!"'.
- **check**: Write the short form of 'can not'. — Answer: can't

### step_6: Words with Many Meanings
- **tts**:
  - Some words have more than one meaning.
  - Read the whole sentence to find out which meaning fits.
- **on_screen**: The word 'bark' sits between a dog picture and a tree picture. The sentence 'The tree's bark is rough' lights up the tree.
- **check**: 'The dog began to bark.' Is this bark a sound or part of a tree? — Answer: a sound

### step_7: Words That Go Together
- **tts**:
  - Some words like to go together, like a glass of water or a cup of tea.
  - We say 'take a bath' and 'brush your teeth'.
- **on_screen**: Word magnets snap together: 'a loaf of' + 'bread', 'a swarm of' + 'bees', 'fly' + 'a kite'.
- **check**: Which word fits: 'a ___ of bread'? — Answer: loaf
