## Learning objectives
- I can name animals that eat only plants. They are called herbivores.
- I can name animals that eat other animals. They are called carnivores.
- I can name animals that eat both plants and animals. They are called omnivores.
- I can use teeth and beak clues to guess what an animal eats.
- I can match animals to their homes, like nest, den, burrow, hive, stable, kennel and sty.
- I can tell why animals need homes: to stay safe, beat the weather and care for babies.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Plant eaters
- **Visual:** A sunny green field. A cow munches grass. A deer nibbles leaves. A rabbit chews a carrot. A goat eats from a bush. Each one gets a green leaf badge when tapped.
- **TTS:**
  - "Some animals eat only plants."
  - "They eat grass, leaves, fruits and seeds."
  - "We call them herbivores. Cow, deer, rabbit and goat are herbivores."
- **Interaction:** Tap-to-feed. The child drags grass, a carrot and leaves to the right animal. Each correct feed plays a happy munch sound.

### Scene 2: Animal eaters
- **Visual:** A forest edge. A lion rests on a rock. A tiger walks through tall grass. An eagle flies high with sharp eyes. A snake curls near a rat hole. Each gets a red paw badge.
- **TTS:**
  - "Some animals eat other animals."
  - "We call them carnivores."
  - "Lion, tiger, eagle and snake are carnivores. They hunt for their food."
- **Interaction:** Sort game. Animal cards drop down. The child swipes each into the Plant Eater basket or the Animal Eater basket.

### Scene 3: Eat-everything animals
- **Visual:** A crow pecks a roti and then a worm. A bear catches a fish and then licks honey. A child eats rice, dal and egg curry. Each gets a half-green, half-red badge.
- **TTS:**
  - "Some animals eat both plants and animals."
  - "We call them omnivores."
  - "Crow, bear and we humans are omnivores."
- **Interaction:** Plate builder. The child picks foods for the crow, the bear and the child. A chime plays when the plate has a plant food and an animal food.

### Scene 4: Teeth and beak clues
- **Visual:** Big friendly close-ups. A cow's flat teeth grind grass. A lion's long pointed teeth tear meat. An eagle's hooked beak, a parrot's curved beak and a sunbird's long thin beak sit side by side.
- **TTS:**
  - "Teeth and beaks give us clues about food."
  - "Flat teeth grind grass. Sharp pointed teeth tear meat."
  - "A hooked beak tears meat. A long thin beak sips nectar."
- **Interaction:** Clue detective. The child sees a mouth or beak and taps the food it fits: grass, meat, nuts or flower nectar.

### Scene 5: Home, sweet home
- **Visual:** A village map with homes that light up. A nest in a tree. A den in a rocky cave. A burrow under the ground. A hive on a branch. A stable for horses, a kennel for a dog and a sty for pigs. Rain starts to fall, and every animal runs home.
- **TTS:**
  - "Animals need homes, just like us."
  - "Birds live in nests. Lions live in dens. Rabbits live in burrows."
  - "Bees live in hives. Horses live in stables."
  - "A dog lives in a kennel. A pig lives in a sty."
  - "Homes keep animals safe from enemies, rain, heat and cold."
  - "Homes also keep baby animals safe and warm."
- **Interaction:** Match-the-home. The child drags each animal to its home before the rain cloud reaches it. A recap card shows three ideas: plant eaters, animal eaters, and homes.

## Quiz Set A

### Q1
**Stem:** Which animal eats only plants?
**Options:**
A) Lion
B) Cow
C) Eagle
D) Snake
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A cow eats grass and leaves. It does not eat other animals, so it is a herbivore.

### Q2
**Stem:** What do we call animals that eat only plants?
**Options:**
A) Herbivores
B) Carnivores
C) Omnivores
D) Insects
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Herbivores are plant eaters. Cows, goats and deer are herbivores.
