# Chapter 2: Animals — Food and Homes

## Meta
- Grade: 3
- Subject: Science
- Theme tags: animals, herbivore, carnivore, omnivore, animal homes, shelter
- Source basis: NCERT Class 3 EVS / typical NSO themes (original items only)
- Language: Grade 3 (short sentences, high visual/TTS)

## Learning objectives
- I can name animals that eat only plants. They are called herbivores.
- I can name animals that eat other animals. They are called carnivores.
- I can name animals that eat both plants and animals. They are called omnivores.
- I can use teeth and beak clues to guess what an animal eats.
- I can match animals to their homes, like nest, den, burrow, hive, stable, kennel and sty.
- I can tell why animals need homes: to stay safe, beat the weather and care for babies.

## Interactive lesson outline

### Scene 1: Plant eaters
- **Visual:** A sunny green field. A cow munches grass. A deer nibbles leaves. A rabbit chews a carrot. A goat eats from a bush. Each one gets a green leaf badge when tapped.
- **TTS:**
  - "Some animals eat only plants."
  - "They eat grass, leaves, fruits and seeds."
  - "We call them herbivores. Cow, deer, rabbit and goat are herbivores."
- **Interaction:** Tap-to-feed. The child drags grass, a carrot and leaves to the right animal. Each correct feed plays a happy munch sound.

### Scene 2: Animal eaters
- **Visual:** A forest edge. A lion rests on a rock. A tiger walks through tall grass. An eagle flies high with sharp eyes. A snake curls near a rat hole. Each gets a red paw badge.
- **TTS:**
  - "Some animals eat other animals."
  - "We call them carnivores."
  - "Lion, tiger, eagle and snake are carnivores. They hunt for their food."
- **Interaction:** Sort game. Animal cards drop down. The child swipes each into the Plant Eater basket or the Animal Eater basket.

### Scene 3: Eat-everything animals
- **Visual:** A crow pecks a roti and then a worm. A bear catches a fish and then licks honey. A child eats rice, dal and egg curry. Each gets a half-green, half-red badge.
- **TTS:**
  - "Some animals eat both plants and animals."
  - "We call them omnivores."
  - "Crow, bear and we humans are omnivores."
- **Interaction:** Plate builder. The child picks foods for the crow, the bear and the child. A chime plays when the plate has a plant food and an animal food.

### Scene 4: Teeth and beak clues
- **Visual:** Big friendly close-ups. A cow's flat teeth grind grass. A lion's long pointed teeth tear meat. An eagle's hooked beak, a parrot's curved beak and a sunbird's long thin beak sit side by side.
- **TTS:**
  - "Teeth and beaks give us clues about food."
  - "Flat teeth grind grass. Sharp pointed teeth tear meat."
  - "A hooked beak tears meat. A long thin beak sips nectar."
- **Interaction:** Clue detective. The child sees a mouth or beak and taps the food it fits: grass, meat, nuts or flower nectar.

### Scene 5: Home, sweet home
- **Visual:** A village map with homes that light up. A nest in a tree. A den in a rocky cave. A burrow under the ground. A hive on a branch. A stable for horses, a kennel for a dog and a sty for pigs. Rain starts to fall, and every animal runs home.
- **TTS:**
  - "Animals need homes, just like us."
  - "Birds live in nests. Lions live in dens. Rabbits live in burrows."
  - "Bees live in hives. Horses live in stables."
  - "A dog lives in a kennel. A pig lives in a sty."
  - "Homes keep animals safe from enemies, rain, heat and cold."
  - "Homes also keep baby animals safe and warm."
- **Interaction:** Match-the-home. The child drags each animal to its home before the rain cloud reaches it. A recap card shows three ideas: plant eaters, animal eaters, and homes.

## Quiz Set A

### Q1
**Stem:** Which animal eats only plants?
**Options:**
A) Lion
B) Cow
C) Eagle
D) Snake
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A cow eats grass and leaves. It does not eat other animals, so it is a herbivore.

### Q2
**Stem:** What do we call animals that eat only plants?
**Options:**
A) Herbivores
B) Carnivores
C) Omnivores
D) Insects
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Herbivores are plant eaters. Cows, goats and deer are herbivores.

### Q3
**Stem:** What does a lion eat?
**Options:**
A) Grass
B) Fruits
C) Other animals
D) Seeds
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A lion hunts other animals, like deer and zebras. It is a carnivore.

### Q4
**Stem:** Where does a bird lay its eggs?
**Options:**
A) Den
B) Kennel
C) Hive
D) Nest
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Birds build nests with twigs, grass and leaves. They lay eggs and keep babies there.

### Q5
**Stem:** Where do honeybees live?
**Options:**
A) Hive
B) Stable
C) Sty
D) Burrow
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Bees live together in a hive. They make honey there too.

### Q6
**Stem:** A crow eats grains and also eats worms. What kind of eater is a crow?
**Options:**
A) Herbivore
B) Carnivore
C) Omnivore
D) Insect
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A crow eats both plant food and animal food. So it is an omnivore.

### Q7
**Stem:** People make a small house for their pet dog. What is it called?
**Options:**
A) Nest
B) Kennel
C) Hive
D) Den
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A kennel is a dog's home. People build it to keep the dog safe and dry.

### Q8
**Stem:** Which of these animals is a carnivore?
**Options:**
A) Deer
B) Goat
C) Rabbit
D) Tiger
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A tiger hunts and eats other animals. Deer, goats and rabbits eat plants.

### Q9
**Stem:** Where does a horse live?
**Options:**
A) Stable
B) Sty
C) Hive
D) Nest
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A stable is a home people make for horses. It keeps them safe from rain and sun.

### Q10
**Stem:** A rabbit digs a hole under the ground to live in. What is this home called?
**Options:**
A) Den
B) Nest
C) Burrow
D) Kennel
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A burrow is a hole dug in the ground. Rabbits hide and sleep in burrows.

### Q11
**Stem:** Sharp pointed teeth help an animal tear meat. Which animal has such teeth?
**Options:**
A) Cow
B) Lion
C) Goat
D) Deer
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** A lion eats meat, so it has sharp pointed teeth. Cows, goats and deer have flat teeth for plants.

### Q12
**Stem:** Flat teeth are good for grinding. What food do flat teeth help to chew?
**Options:**
A) Meat
B) Grass and leaves
C) Fish
D) Insects
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Plant eaters like cows have flat teeth. They grind grass and leaves into small bits.

### Q13
**Stem:** Where does a pig live on a farm?
**Options:**
A) Stable
B) Kennel
C) Sty
D) Hive
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A sty is a pig's home. Farmers build it for their pigs.

### Q14
**Stem:** An eagle has a strong hooked beak. How does this beak help it?
**Options:**
A) To crack nuts
B) To sip nectar
C) To tear meat
D) To dig soil
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** An eagle eats small animals. Its hooked beak works like a hook to tear meat.

### Q15
**Stem:** Which group has only omnivores?
**Options:**
A) Crow, bear, human
B) Lion, tiger, eagle
C) Cow, goat, deer
D) Rabbit, horse, camel
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** Crows, bears and humans eat both plants and animals. The other groups eat only one kind of food.

### Q16
**Stem:** A lion rests in a cave with its cubs. What is a lion's home called?
**Options:**
A) Den
B) Nest
C) Hive
D) Sty
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A den is a lion's home. It is often a cave or a hidden spot in rocks.

### Q17
**Stem:** Why does a bird build a nest?
**Options:**
A) To lay eggs and keep its babies safe
B) To store water for summer
C) To play games with friends
D) To hide its food from people
**Answer:** A
**Explanation:** A nest is a safe, soft place. Birds lay eggs there and care for their babies.

### Q18
**Stem:** Find the odd one out: Cow, Goat, Tiger, Deer.
**Options:**
A) Cow
B) Goat
C) Tiger
D) Deer
**Answer:** C
**Explanation:** A tiger eats other animals. Cow, goat and deer eat only plants.

### Q19
**Stem:** A bear eats fish, honey and berries. What kind of eater is a bear?
**Options:**
A) Herbivore
B) Omnivore
C) Carnivore
D) Insect
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Fish is animal food. Honey and berries come from plants and flowers. A bear eats both, so it is an omnivore.

### Q20
**Stem:** Look at this food chain: Grass → Rabbit → ? Which animal fits in the gap?
**Options:**
A) Cow
B) Fox
C) Goat
D) Deer
**Answer:** B
**Explanation:** Grass is eaten by the rabbit. A fox eats rabbits. Cow, goat and deer eat plants, not rabbits.

### Q21
**Stem:** The tailorbird makes a special nest. How does it make it?
**Options:**
A) By digging a hole in the sand
B) By building it with wax
C) By living in a big cave
D) By stitching leaves together
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** The tailorbird uses its sharp beak like a needle. It stitches big leaves together to make a cup-like nest.

### Q22
**Stem:** Which animal is NOT matched with the correct home?
**Options:**
A) Bee — hive
B) Horse — stable
C) Dog — kennel
D) Pig — nest
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A pig lives in a sty, not a nest. Birds live in nests.

### Q23
**Stem:** Which animal eats only plants AND lives in a burrow?
**Options:**
A) Snake
B) Fox
C) Lion
D) Rabbit
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** A rabbit eats grass and carrots, so it is a herbivore. It also lives in a burrow. Snake, fox and lion eat other animals.

### Q24
**Stem:** Humans are omnivores. Which meal shows that we eat both plant food and animal food?
**Options:**
A) A bowl of salad
B) A plate of fruits
C) A bowl of boiled peas
D) Rice with egg curry
**Answer:** D
**Explanation:** Rice comes from a plant. Eggs come from hens. This meal has both kinds of food.
