## Learning objectives
- I can spot nouns, verbs, and adjectives in a simple sentence.
- I can choose a, an, or the correctly.
- I can pick simple present or past forms that match time words.
- I can fix capitals and end marks in short sentences.

## Interactive lesson outline
- L1 (hook): Words have jobs — naming, doing, describing.
- L2 (reveal): noun → naming; verb → doing; adjective → describing; a/an/the → little helpers.
- L3 (demo): The fluffy cat jumps. → cat = noun, fluffy = adjective, jumps = verb.
- L4 (try): They ____ playing. → are.
- L5 (check): Which word is a noun? school (not run).
- L6 (wrap): Match the job, match the time, fix the mark. Sets ready — 24 MCQs each.

- answer: A
