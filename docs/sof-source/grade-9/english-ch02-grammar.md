## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Choose the correct sentence.
- **options**:
  - A) She don’t like tea.
  - B) She doesn’t like tea.
  - C) She doesn’t likes tea.
  - D) She not like tea.
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Third-person singular needs doesn’t + base verb.
- **skill**: agreement
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: Fill in: By next June, we ____ here for ten years.
- **options**:
  - A) will live
  - B) will have lived
  - C) lived
  - D) are living
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Future perfect marks duration completed by a future point.
- **skill**: tense
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q03
- **stem**: Change to passive: “The chef cooked the meal.”
- **options**:
  - A) The meal cooked the chef.
  - B) The meal was cooked by the chef.
  - C) The meal is cooking the chef.
  - D) The chef was cooked the meal.
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Object becomes subject; past simple → was/were + past participle.
- **skill**: voice
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: Reported speech: He said, “I am tired.” → He said that he ____ tired.
- **options**:
  - A) is
  - B) was
  - C) will be
  - D) were being
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Backshift present to past after a past reporting verb.
- **skill**: reported
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: Choose the correct article: She is ____ honest person.
- **options**:
  - A) a
  - B) an
  - C) the
  - D) no article
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Honest begins with a vowel sound → an.
- **skill**: articles
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06

### Q07

### Q08
- **stem**: Choose the correct verb: The news ____ surprising.
- **options**:
  - A) are
  - B) is
  - C) were
  - D) have
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: News is singular in standard English → is.
- **skill**: agreement
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q09
- **stem**: Fill in: If it rains, we ____ indoors.
- **options**:
  - A) stay
  - B) stayed
  - C) would stayed
  - D) had stayed
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: First conditional: If + present, will/can/other present forms; “stay” fits as a present/instructional result (or “will stay”). Among options, stay is correct.
- **skill**: conditional
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q10
- **stem**: Pick the correctly punctuated sentence.
- **options**:
  - A) Lets go, its late.
  - B) Let’s go; it’s late.
  - C) Lets go; its’ late.
  - D) Let’s go its late.
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Let’s = let us; it’s = it is; semicolon joins related clauses.
- **skill**: punctuation
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q20

- **skill**: verb-pattern
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q21
- **stem**: Choose: She ____ finished the novel before the exam started.
- **options**:
  - A) has
  - B) had
  - C) have
  - D) having
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Past perfect for earlier past before another past.
- **skill**: tense
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q22
- **stem**: Which sentence uses a non-defining relative clause correctly?
- **options**:
  - A) My brother who lives in Pune is a doctor. (only one brother, commas needed)
  - B) My brother, who lives in Pune, is a doctor.
  - C) My brother, that lives in Pune, is a doctor.
  - D) My brother which lives in Pune is a doctor.
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Non-defining clauses take commas; that is not used in non-defining clauses; who for people.
- **skill**: relative
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q23

- **skill**: tense
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q24

## Practice Set B

### Q01
- **stem**: Choose: The team ____ winning after half-time.
- **options**:
  - A) was
  - B) were
  - C) are
  - D) be
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Team as a single unit often takes singular was (especially in Indian exam English).
- **skill**: agreement
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q02
- **stem**: Active form of “The window was broken by the storm.”
- **options**:
  - A) The storm broke the window.
  - B) The storm was broken the window.
  - C) The window broke the storm.
  - D) The storm broken window.
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Restore the agent as subject; past simple active: broke.
- **skill**: voice
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: Reported: She said, “I will call tomorrow.” → She said she ____ call the next day.
- **options**:
  - A) will

  - C) shall
  - D) can
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Will → would; tomorrow → the next day.
- **skill**: reported
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04

- **difficulty**: easy
