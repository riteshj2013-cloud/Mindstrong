## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Matter is anything that has…
- **options**:
  - A) only colour
  - B) mass and occupies space
  - C) only smell
  - D) only taste
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Matter has mass and occupies space.
- **skill**: basics
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: In which state are particles most loosely packed and free to move far apart?
- **options**:
  - A) solid
  - B) liquid
  - C) gas
  - D) all equal
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Gas particles are farthest apart and move freely.
- **skill**: states
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: The change of a liquid into vapour at any temperature below boiling point is…
- **options**:
  - A) melting
  - B) evaporation
  - C) sublimation
  - D) condensation
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Evaporation occurs at the surface below boiling point.
- **skill**: change
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: Dry ice (solid CO₂) changing directly to gas is…
- **options**:
  - A) melting
  - B) freezing
  - C) sublimation
  - D) evaporation
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Solid → gas directly is sublimation.
- **skill**: change
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: Which has a definite shape and definite volume?
- **options**:
  - A) gas
  - B) liquid
  - C) solid
  - D) plasma only in stars
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Solids keep shape and volume.
- **skill**: states
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: Boiling occurs…
- **options**:
  - A) only at the surface
  - B) throughout the bulk of the liquid at a fixed temperature (for given pressure)
  - C) only in solids
  - D) without heat
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Boiling is bulk vaporisation at the boiling point for a given pressure.
- **skill**: change
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q07
- **stem**: Latent heat of vaporisation is the heat…
- **options**:
  - A) to raise temperature of vapour by 1°C
  - B) to convert unit mass of liquid to vapour at constant temperature
  - C) to melt any solid
  - D) stored only in solids
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: It is heat to change liquid → vapour without temperature change (per unit mass).
- **skill**: latent
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q08
- **stem**: Cooling caused by evaporation happens because…
- **options**:
  - A) higher-energy particles leave, lowering average kinetic energy of the rest
  - B) particles stop moving completely
  - C) mass of liquid increases
  - D) volume becomes exactly zero
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Faster particles escape; remaining liquid has lower average kinetic energy → cooler.
- **skill**: evaporation
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q09
- **stem**: Which factor increases the rate of evaporation?
- **options**:
  - A) decreasing surface area
  - B) increasing humidity
  - C) increasing wind speed
  - D) decreasing temperature
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Wind removes vapour, speeding further evaporation.
- **skill**: evaporation
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q10
- **stem**: Liquids have…
- **options**:
  - A) definite shape and volume
  - B) definite volume but no definite shape
  - C) neither shape nor volume
  - D) definite shape only

- **explanation**: Liquids take container shape but keep volume.
- **skill**: states
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q11

### Q13

### Q14

### Q15

### Q16

### Q17

### Q18

### Q19

### Q20

- **skill**: states

### Q21
- **stem**: Diffusion is fastest in…
- **options**:
  - A) solids
  - B) liquids

- **skill**: particle

### Q22

## Practice Set B

### Q01
- **stem**: Particles of matter are…
- **options**:
  - A) visible easily always as separate dots to the eye
  - B) extremely small
  - C) never in motion
  - D) without spaces between them
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Particles are too small to see individually; they move and have spaces.
- **skill**: particle
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: Naphthalene balls disappear over time mainly by…
- **options**:
  - A) melting to liquid first always
  - B) sublimation
  - C) condensation
  - D) freezing
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Naphthalene sublimes: solid → vapour.
- **skill**: change
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: Which state can be compressed the most easily?
- **options**:
  - A) solid
  - B) liquid
  - C) gas
  - D) all identical
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Large spaces between gas particles allow easy compression.
- **skill**: states
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: Steam at 100°C causes more severe burns than water at 100°C mainly because steam…
- **options**:
  - A) has lower temperature
  - B) carries latent heat of vaporisation released on condensation
  - C) is denser than water
  - D) has no energy
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Condensing steam releases latent heat in addition to cooling.
- **skill**: latent
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q05
- **stem**: The process of a gas changing into a solid without becoming liquid is…
- **options**:
  - A) deposition (sometimes taught with sublimation reverse)
  - B) melting
  - C) boiling
  - D) evaporation
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Gas → solid directly is deposition (reverse of sublimation).
- **skill**: change
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q06
- **stem**: Increasing surface area of a liquid…
- **options**:
  - A) decreases evaporation rate
  - B) increases evaporation rate
  - C) stops all motion
  - D) changes boiling point to 0 always
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: More surface → more molecules can escape per time.
- **skill**: evaporation
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: A liquid’s boiling point depends on…
- **options**:
  - A) colour only
  - B) external pressure
  - C) only the container’s brand
  - D) magnetism only
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Lower pressure → lower boiling point (e.g., on mountains).
- **skill**: change
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q08

  - C) Liquids flow and take container shape

- **skill**: states
- **difficulty**: easy
