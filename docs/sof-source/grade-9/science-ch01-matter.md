### Q07
- **stem**: Latent heat of vaporisation is the heat…
- **options**:
  - A) to raise temperature of vapour by 1°C
  - B) to melt any solid
  - C) to convert unit mass of liquid to vapour at constant temperature
  - D) stored only in solids
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: It is heat to change liquid → vapour without temperature change (per unit mass).
- **skill**: latent
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q08
- **stem**: Cooling caused by evaporation happens because…
- **options**:
  - A) particles stop moving completely
  - B) mass of liquid increases
  - C) volume becomes exactly zero
  - D) higher-energy particles leave, lowering average kinetic energy of the rest
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Faster particles escape; remaining liquid has lower average kinetic energy → cooler.
- **skill**: evaporation
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q12
- **stem**: A spoon of sugar is stirred into 50 mL of water until it dissolves, and the water level hardly rises. This shows that particles of matter —
- **options**:
  - A) are always at rest
  - B) have no mass
  - C) attract each other strongly
  - D) have spaces between them
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: The sugar particles fit into the spaces between the water particles, so the volume hardly increases. Particles do attract each other, but attraction alone does not explain how the sugar fits in without raising the level.
- **skill**: particle
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q14
