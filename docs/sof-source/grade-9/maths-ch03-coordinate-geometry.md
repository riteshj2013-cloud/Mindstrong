## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: The point where the x-axis and y-axis meet is called the…
- **options**:
  - A) quadrant
  - B) origin
  - C) abscissa
  - D) ordinate
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Their intersection is the origin (0, 0).
- **skill**: basics
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: In the ordered pair (3, −2), the abscissa is…
- **options**:
  - A) −2
  - B) 3
  - C) 0
  - D) 5
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Abscissa means the x-coordinate, which is 3.
- **skill**: basics
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: The point (−4, 5) lies in which quadrant?
- **options**:
  - A) I
  - B) II
  - C) III
  - D) IV
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: x negative, y positive → quadrant II.
- **skill**: quadrants
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: A point on the x-axis has…
- **options**:
  - A) x = 0
  - B) y = 0
  - C) x = y
  - D) x = −y
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: On the x-axis the y-coordinate is 0.
- **skill**: axes
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: The distance of point (0, 6) from the origin is…
- **options**:
  - A) 0
  - B) 6
  - C) 36
  - D) √6
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: It lies on the y-axis 6 units from (0,0).
- **skill**: distance
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: Which point lies in quadrant III?
- **options**:
  - A) (2, 3)
  - B) (−2, 3)

- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Both coordinates negative → quadrant III.
- **skill**: quadrants
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: The ordinate of (−7, 0) is…
- **options**:
  - A) −7
  - B) 0
  - C) 7
  - D) undefined
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Ordinate is the y-coordinate: 0.
- **skill**: basics
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q08
- **stem**: Points (2, 0), (−3, 0) and (5, 0) are…
- **options**:
  - A) on the y-axis
  - B) on the x-axis
  - C) in quadrant I
  - D) at the origin
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Each has y = 0, so all lie on the x-axis.
- **skill**: axes
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q09
- **stem**: The mirror image of (3, 4) in the x-axis is…
- **options**:

  - B) (3, −4)

- **difficulty**: medium

### Q10
- **stem**: If a point has equal abscissa and ordinate and lies in quadrant I, an example is…
- **options**:
  - A) (2, 2)
  - B) (−2, −2)
  - C) (2, −2)
  - D) (−2, 2)
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Equal positive coordinates sit in quadrant I.
- **skill**: quadrants
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q11
- **stem**: Distance between (0, 0) and (3, 4) is…
- **options**:
  - A) 5
  - B) 7
  - C) 12
  - D) 25

- **skill**: distance
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q12

- **skill**: axes

### Q13

### Q14

### Q15

### Q21
- **stem**: Which point on the y-axis is equidistant from (2, 0) and (−2, 0)?

### Q22

- **difficulty**: easy

### Q23

### Q02
- **stem**: Point (0, 8) lies…
- **options**:
  - A) in quadrant I
  - B) on the positive y-axis
  - C) on the positive x-axis
  - D) in quadrant II
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: x = 0, y > 0 → positive y-axis.
- **skill**: axes
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: In quadrant II, x is ____ and y is ____.
- **options**:
  - A) positive, positive
  - B) negative, positive
  - C) negative, negative
  - D) positive, negative
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Quadrant II: x < 0, y > 0.
- **skill**: quadrants
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: The distance of (−5, 0) from the origin is…
- **options**:
  - A) 5
  - B) −5
  - C) 0
  - D) 25
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Distance is absolute: 5 units along the x-axis.
- **skill**: distance
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05

- **options**:

### Q10

- **explanation**: x-axis: (a,−b); then y-axis: (−a,−b). Same as central inversion.

- **difficulty**: hard
