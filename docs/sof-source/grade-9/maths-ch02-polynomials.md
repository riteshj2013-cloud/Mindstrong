## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: The degree of the polynomial 5x³ − 2x + 7 is…
- **options**:
  - A) 5
  - B) 3
  - C) 2
  - D) 7
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The highest power of x with a non-zero coefficient is 3.
- **skill**: degree
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: A linear polynomial has degree…
- **options**:
  - A) 0
  - B) 1
  - C) 2
  - D) 3
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: By definition, linear polynomials are degree 1.
- **skill**: degree
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: The zero of the polynomial p(x) = 2x − 6 is…
- **options**:
  - A) 6
  - B) 3
  - C) −3
  - D) 2
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 2x − 6 = 0 → x = 3.
- **skill**: zeros
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q04
- **stem**: Which is a quadratic polynomial?
- **options**:
  - A) x³ + 1
  - B) 5x − 2
  - C) x² − 4x + 3
  - D) 7
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Degree 2 makes it quadratic.
- **skill**: degree
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: If p(x) = x² − 5x + 6, then p(2) equals…
- **options**:
  - A) 0
  - B) 2
  - C) 6
  - D) −4
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 4 − 10 + 6 = 0, so x = 2 is a zero.
- **skill**: evaluate
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: By the remainder theorem, the remainder when p(x) is divided by (x − a) is…
- **options**:
  - A) p(0)
  - B) p(a)
  - C) p(−a)
  - D) a
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Remainder theorem: remainder is p(a).
- **skill**: remainder
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: If (x − 1) is a factor of p(x), then…
- **options**:
  - A) p(0) = 1
  - B) p(1) = 0
  - C) p(−1) = 0
  - D) p(1) = 1
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Factor theorem: (x − a) is a factor iff p(a) = 0. Here a = 1.
- **skill**: factor
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q08
- **stem**: The constant polynomial 9 has degree…
- **options**:
  - A) 9
  - B) 1
  - C) 0
  - D) undefined
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Non-zero constants are degree 0. (The zero polynomial’s degree is left undefined.)
- **skill**: degree
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q09
- **stem**: Factorise: x² − 9
- **options**:
  - A) (x − 3)(x + 3)
  - B) (x − 9)(x + 1)
  - C) (x − 3)²
  - D) x(x − 9)
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Difference of squares: x² − 3² = (x − 3)(x + 3).
- **skill**: factorise
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q10
- **stem**: The number of zeros of a non-zero polynomial of degree n is at most…
- **options**:
  - A) n − 1
  - B) n
  - C) n + 1
  - D) 2n
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A degree-n polynomial has at most n zeros (unless identically zero).
- **skill**: zeros
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q11
- **stem**: Expand (x + 2)(x + 5).
- **options**:
  - A) x² + 7x + 10
  - B) x² + 10x + 7
  - C) x² + 3x + 10
  - D) x² + 7x + 7
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: x² + 5x + 2x + 10 = x² + 7x + 10.
- **skill**: expand
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q12
- **stem**: If p(x) = x³ − 1, then p(1) equals…
- **options**:
  - A) 0
  - B) 1
  - C) −1
  - D) 2
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: 1 − 1 = 0, so (x − 1) is a factor of x³ − 1.
- **skill**: evaluate
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q13
- **stem**: Which identity is correct?
- **options**:
  - A) (a + b)² = a² + b²
  - B) (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²
  - C) (a − b)² = a² − b²
  - D) (a + b)(a − b) = a² + b²
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: (a + b)² expands to a² + 2ab + b².
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q14

- **explanation**: The term 5x has coefficient 5.
- **skill**: coeff

### Q16

### Q18

### Q19

### Q20
- **stem**: Factorise: x² + 5x + 6
- **options**:
  - A) (x + 2)(x + 3)
  - B) (x + 1)(x + 6)
  - C) (x − 2)(x − 3)
  - D) (x + 5)(x + 1)
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Need two numbers that multiply to 6 and add to 5: 2 and 3.
- **skill**: factorise
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q21
- **stem**: The remainder when x³ + 3x + 1 is divided by (x − 1) is…
- **options**:
  - A) 5
  - B) 1
  - C) 3
  - D) 0
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: p(1) = 1 + 3 + 1 = 5.
- **skill**: remainder
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q22
- **stem**: (x + y)² − (x − y)² equals…
- **options**:
  - A) 4xy
  - B) 2xy
  - C) x² − y²
  - D) 0
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Expand: (x²+2xy+y²) − (x²−2xy+y²) = 4xy.
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q23
- **stem**: If (x − 2) is a factor of x² − kx + 8 and k is a constant, then k equals…
- **options**:
  - A) 2
  - B) 4
  - C) 6
  - D) 8
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: p(2)=0 → 4 − 2k + 8 = 0 → 12 = 2k → k = 6.
- **skill**: factor
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q24
- **stem**: The zeros of x² − 4 are…
- **options**:
  - A) 2 only
  - B) −2 only
  - C) 2 and −2
  - D) 4 and −4
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: x² − 4 = (x − 2)(x + 2) = 0 → x = ±2.
- **skill**: zeros
- **difficulty**: easy

## Practice Set B

### Q01
- **stem**: Which polynomial is of degree 4?
- **options**:
  - A) x³ + x⁴ − 1
  - B) 4x + 1
  - C) x² + 4
  - D) 4
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: The x⁴ term makes the degree 4.
- **skill**: degree
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: A quadratic polynomial can have at most how many zeros?
- **options**:
  - A) 1
  - B) 2
  - C) 3
  - D) 4
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Degree 2 ⇒ at most 2 zeros.
- **skill**: zeros
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q03
- **stem**: p(x) = x² − 3x − 4. A zero of p is…
- **options**:
  - A) 4
  - B) −1
  - C) both A and B
  - D) 3
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: (x − 4)(x + 1) = x² − 3x − 4, so zeros are 4 and −1.
- **skill**: zeros
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q04
- **stem**: The value of (a + b + c)² − (a² + b² + c²) equals…
- **options**:
  - A) 2(ab + bc + ca)
  - B) ab + bc + ca
  - C) 2abc
  - D) 0
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Expand (a+b+c)² = a²+b²+c² + 2(ab+bc+ca).
- **skill**: identity
- **difficulty**: medium

### Q05
- **stem**: If p(x) = x³ − 6x² + 11x − 6 and p(1)=0, which is a factor?
- **options**:
  - A) x + 1
  - B) x − 1
  - C) x − 6
  - D) x + 6
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: p(1)=0 ⇒ (x − 1) is a factor by the factor theorem.
- **skill**: factor
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: Expand (2x − 3)².
- **options**:
  - A) 4x² − 12x + 9
  - B) 4x² − 9
  - C) 4x² + 12x + 9
  - D) 4x² − 6x + 9
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: (2x)² − 2·2x·3 + 3² = 4x² − 12x + 9.
- **skill**: expand
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: The coefficient of x² in (x + 1)(x + 2)(x + 3) is…
- **options**:
  - A) 6
  - B) 11
  - C) 5
  - D) 9
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: First (x+1)(x+2)=x²+3x+2; times (x+3): x³+3x² + 3x²+9x + 2x+6 = x³+6x²+11x+6. Coefficient of x² is 6.
- **skill**: coeff
- **difficulty**: hard

### Q08
- **stem**: Remainder when 2x³ − 3x² + x − 1 is divided by (x − 2) is…
- **options**:
  - A) 5
  - B) 9
  - C) 1
  - D) 0
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: p(2) = 2·8 − 3·4 + 2 − 1 = 16 − 12 + 2 − 1 = 5.
- **skill**: remainder
- **difficulty**: medium
