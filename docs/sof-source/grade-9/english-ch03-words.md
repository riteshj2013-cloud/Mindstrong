## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Choose the synonym of “brief”.
- **options**:
  - A) lengthy
  - B) concise
  - C) noisy
  - D) ancient
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Brief means short/concise.
- **skill**: synonym
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q02
- **stem**: Antonym of “scarce” is…
- **options**:

- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Scarce ↔ abundant/plentiful.
- **skill**: antonym
