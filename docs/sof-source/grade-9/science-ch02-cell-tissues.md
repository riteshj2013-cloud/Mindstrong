- **stem**: In the cell shown, which labelled part is the control centre that contains the genetic material?
- **options**:
  - A) Part P
  - B) Part Q
  - C) Part R
  - D) Part S
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: R is the nucleus, the large round body with its own boundary that holds the chromosomes (DNA) and controls the cell's activities. S is a mitochondrion, which releases energy but does not hold the cell's main genetic material.

### Q04
- **stem**: Powerhouse of the cell refers to…
- **options**:
  - A) nucleus
  - B) golgi apparatus
  - C) cell wall
  - D) mitochondria
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Mitochondria release energy (cellular respiration).
- **skill**: organelle
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q05
- **stem**: Which is present in plant cells but not in typical animal cells?
- **options**:
  - A) cell wall
  - B) mitochondria
  - C) cell membrane
  - D) ribosomes
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Rigid cell wall of cellulose is plant-typical.
- **skill**: compare
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q06
- **stem**: Chloroplasts are associated with…
- **options**:
  - A) protein digestion
  - B) photosynthesis
  - C) nerve signals
  - D) bone growth
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Chloroplasts trap light to make food.
- **skill**: organelle
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q07
- **stem**: A group of similar cells performing a specific function forms a…
- **options**:
  - A) organelle
  - B) organ system only
  - C) tissue
  - D) population
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Tissue = group of similar cells with a common function.
- **skill**: organisation
- **difficulty**: easy

### Q08
- **stem**: The selectively permeable boundary of an animal cell is the…
- **options**:
  - A) cell wall
  - B) nuclear envelope only
  - C) cytoplasm
  - D) plasma membrane
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Plasma/cell membrane controls entry and exit.
- **skill**: organelle
- **difficulty**: easy

- **alt**: A plant tissue seen under a microscope: box-shaped living cells, each with a nucleus, whose walls are thin along the sides but thickened at every corner.
- **stem**: The drawing shows a tissue from the stalk of a young leaf. What does this tissue provide?
- **options**:
  - A) Impulse conduction
  - B) Flexibility and support to growing parts
  - C) Oxygen transport in blood
  - D) Rigid support as dead, thick-walled fibres
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: These living cells, with nuclei, have walls thickened only at the corners: this is collenchyma, which supports young stems and leaf stalks while letting them bend. Rigid support from dead fibres describes sclerenchyma, whose cells are thickened all the way round and have no nuclei.
- **skill**: tissue
- **difficulty**: medium
