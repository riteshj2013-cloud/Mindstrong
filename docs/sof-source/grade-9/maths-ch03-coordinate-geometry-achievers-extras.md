- **alt**: A coordinate grid with x from −4 to 5 and y from −3 to 5. Point A is at (−3, −1), point B at (2, −2) and point C at (4, 3).
- **options**:
  - A) (−1, 4)
  - B) (9, 2)
  - C) (−1, 2)
  - D) (1, 4)
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: In parallelogram ABCD, side AD is parallel and equal to side BC. Going from B to C is 2 right and 5 up, so going from A to D is also 2 right and 5 up: D = (−3 + 2, −1 + 5) = (−1, 4). (The diagonals AC and BD both have midpoint (0.5, 1), which checks.) (9, 2) is tempting because it adds the move from A to B onto C, making the parallelogram ABDC with the corners in a different order.
- **hint**: In Taniya's parallelogram, the move from B to C must equal the move from A to the missing corner.

### Q03
- **id**: g9-maths-ch03-ach-x-03
- **stem**: On a town map, Gitika's house is at A(2, 5) and her school is at B(−2, −3). A new bus stop is to be put on the x-axis at the same distance from both. Where should it go?
- **options**:
  - A) (0, 1)
  - B) (−2, 0)
  - C) (1, 0)
  - D) (2, 0)
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Let the stop be (x, 0). Its squared distance from A is (x − 2)² + 25 and from B is (x + 2)² + 9. Setting them equal: x² − 4x + 29 = x² + 4x + 13, so 8x = 16 and x = 2. The stop goes at (2, 0), which is 5 units from A and √(16 + 9) = 5 units from B. (0, 1) is tempting because it is the midpoint of AB, which is equally far from both, but it is not on the x-axis.
- **hint**: Let Gitika's bus stop be a point on the x-axis with one unknown, and set the squared distances to the house and the school equal.
- **skill**: point_on_an_axis_equidistant_from_two_points
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq

### Q04
- **id**: g9-maths-ch03-ach-x-04
- **stem**: On her treasure map, Advika marks the point T(2a − 6, a + 1), where a is an integer. The treasure lies inside the second quadrant, not on either axis. How many values can a take?
- **options**:
  - A) 5
  - B) 3
  - C) 4
  - D) 2
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: In the second quadrant the x-coordinate is negative and the y-coordinate is positive. So 2a − 6 < 0, giving a < 3, and a + 1 > 0, giving a > −1. The integers that fit are 0, 1 and 2, so a can take 3 values. 5 is tempting because it also counts a = −1 and a = 3, but those put T on the x-axis or the y-axis, which is not inside the quadrant.
- **hint**: Write the sign each coordinate of Advika's point T must have in that quadrant, and solve both conditions for a.

- **stem**: A straight canal runs along the x-axis of a map marked in kilometres. Mehak walks from her farmhouse at A(1, 3) to the canal to fill a pot, and then on to her aunt's house at B(5, 1). What is the shortest total distance she can walk?
- **options**:
  - A) 4√2 km
  - B) 2√5 km
  - C) 2√10 km
  - D) 8 km
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Reflect A in the x-axis to A′(1, −3). Any route A → canal → B is as long as the route A′ → same point → B, and the shortest of these is the straight line A′B: √((5 − 1)² + (1 + 3)²) = √32 = 4√2 km (about 5.66 km). 2√5 km is tempting because it is the straight distance AB, but that path never touches the canal.
- **hint**: Reflect Mehak's farmhouse in the x-axis; the shortest route through the canal then becomes one straight line.
