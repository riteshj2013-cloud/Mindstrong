### Q01
- **stem**: Read:

"Riya paused at the old library door. Dust motes swam in a shaft of late light. She had promised herself she would return the borrowed journal, yet her fingers tightened on its worn cover. Inside were her grandmother’s recipes — and a letter never sent. “Some stories,” the librarian had once said, “wait for the right reader.” Riya breathed in the smell of paper and decided the right reader might be her."

Why does Riya pause at the library door?
- **options**:
  - A) She is lost
  - B) She feels conflicted about returning the journal
  - C) She is angry at the librarian
  - D) She forgot her keys
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Her fingers tighten on the cover even though she promised to return it — hesitation and conflict.
- **skill**: inference
- **difficulty**: easy
