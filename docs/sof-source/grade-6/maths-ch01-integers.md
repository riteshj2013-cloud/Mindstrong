## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Which of these is an integer?
- **options**:
  - A) 3.5
  - B) -7
  - C) 2/3
  - D) √2
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Integers are whole numbers and their negatives. −7 is an integer; 3.5, 2/3 and √2 are not.
- **hint**: Integers are …, −2, −1, 0, 1, 2, … — no fractions or decimals.

### Q02
- **stem**: On a number line, which number lies to the left of −3?
- **options**:
  - A) −2
  - B) 0
  - C) −5
  - D) 3
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: Numbers decrease as you move left. −5 is left of −3.
- **hint**: On a number line, left means smaller.

### Q03
- **stem**: What is the additive inverse of 9?
- **options**:
  - A) 9
  - B) −9
  - C) 1/9
  - D) 0
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: The additive inverse of a is −a, because a + (−a) = 0. So the inverse of 9 is −9.
- **hint**: Additive inverse of a is the number that sums with a to 0.

### Q04
- **stem**: What is |−12|?
- **options**:
  - A) −12
  - B) 12
  - C) 0
  - D) 1/12
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Absolute value is the distance from 0, so |−12| = 12.
- **hint**: Absolute value drops the sign — distance from zero.

### Q05
- **stem**: Compute (−8) + (−5).
- **options**:
  - A) −13
  - B) 13
  - C) −3
  - D) 3
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Adding two negatives: add the magnitudes and keep the negative sign → −13.
- **hint**: Same signs: add the sizes and keep that sign.

### Q06
- **stem**: Compute 15 + (−9).
- **options**:
  - A) 24
  - B) −24
  - C) 6
  - D) −6
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: 15 + (−9) = 15 − 9 = 6.
- **hint**: Adding a negative is the same as subtracting.

### Q07
- **stem**: Compute (−20) − (−7).
- **options**:
  - A) −27
  - B) −13
  - C) 27
  - D) 13
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Subtracting a negative is adding: (−20) − (−7) = (−20) + 7 = −13.
- **hint**: Minus a negative becomes plus.

### Q08

  - D) −2
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: A negative times a positive is negative: −24.
- **hint**: Different signs when multiplying → negative product.

### Q09

### Q11
- **stem**: Which statement is true?
- **options**:
  - A) Every natural number is an integer
  - B) Every integer is a natural number
  - C) −1 is a natural number
  - D) 0 is not an integer
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Natural numbers 1, 2, 3, … are integers. Negatives and 0 are integers but not natural (in the usual school meaning).
- **hint**: Natural ⊂ whole ⊂ integers — which way do the sets nest?

### Q12
- **stem**: The temperature was −2°C in the morning and rose by 7°C. What is the new temperature?

  - B) 5°C
  - C) −9°C

- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: −2 + 7 = 5, so the new temperature is 5°C.
- **hint**: Start at −2, then add the rise.

### Q13

### Q16

### Q17

### Q18
- **stem**: A bank account has ₹200. Then ₹350 is withdrawn. Which integer shows the balance?

  - A) 550
  - B) −150
  - C) 150
  - D) −550
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 200 − 350 = −150, so the balance is ₹150 overdrawn, written −150.
- **hint**: Start with 200, subtract 350 — keep the sign for an overdrawn balance.

### Q19

- **options**:
  - A) 7
  - B) −7
  - C) 17
  - D) −17

### Q20
- **stem**: Compute (−2)³.
- **options**:
  - A) −8
  - B) 8
  - C) −6
  - D) 6
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: (−2)³ = (−2)×(−2)×(−2) = 4 × (−2) = −8.
- **hint**: Odd power of a negative stays negative.

### Q21

### Q23

  - A) 3
  - B) −3
  - C) 12
  - D) −12
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Negative ÷ negative = positive: 3.
- **hint**: Same signs when dividing → positive.

### Q24
- **stem**: Which expression equals −10?

  - A) (−4) + (−6)
  - B) (−4) − (−6)

### Q02

- **options**:
  - A) 1
  - B) 7

- **hint**: Distance is the absolute difference.
