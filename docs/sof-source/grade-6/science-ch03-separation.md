## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Handpicking is useful when —
- **options**:
  - A) unwanted pieces are large and easy to see
  - B) all particles are identical and invisible
  - C) you need to boil water
  - D) you need a magnet only
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Handpicking removes large, visible impurities by hand.
- **hint**: When handpicking works.

### Q02
- **stem**: Threshing separates —
- **options**:
  - A) grain from stalks
  - B) salt from seawater by freezing only
  - C) iron from sand with hands only
  - D) cream by filtering paper
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Threshing beats stalks so grain separates.
- **hint**: Grain harvest separation.

### Q03
- **stem**: Winnowing uses —
- **options**:
  - A) wind or blowing air to separate lighter husk
  - B) only magnets
  - C) only filters
  - D) only evaporation pans
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Lighter husk blows away; heavier grain falls.
- **hint**: Wind separation.

### Q04
- **stem**: Sieving separates particles based on —
- **options**:
  - A) size
  - B) colour only
  - C) magnetism only
  - D) taste only
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: A sieve lets smaller particles pass and holds larger ones.
- **hint**: Sieve property.

### Q05
- **stem**: Sedimentation means —
- **options**:
  - A) heavier insoluble solids settle at the bottom of a liquid
  - B) solids fly upward always
  - C) salt disappears forever
  - D) fibres become yarn
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Heavy particles settle when a mixture stands.
- **hint**: Settling down.

### Q06
- **stem**: Decantation is —
- **options**:
  - A) pouring off liquid carefully after settling
  - B) boiling metal
  - C) spinning cotton
  - D) measuring angles
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: After sedimentation, liquid is poured off the sediment.
- **hint**: Pour after settling.

### Q07
- **stem**: Filtration uses a filter to —
- **options**:
  - A) trap insoluble solids and let liquid pass
  - B) separate two gases by colour only
  - C) shear sheep
  - D) weave cloth

- **explanation**: Filter paper/cloth holds residue; filtrate passes.
- **hint**: Filter job.

### Q08
- **stem**: Evaporation separates —
