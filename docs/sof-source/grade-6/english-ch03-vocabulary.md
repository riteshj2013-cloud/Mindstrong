## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Synonym of ancient is —
- **options**:
  - A) old
  - B) modern
  - C) tiny
  - D) quick
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Ancient means very old.
- **hint**: Which word is closest in meaning to ancient?
