## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Cotton fibre comes from the —
- **options**:
  - A) cotton plant’s boll
  - B) stem of jute only
  - C) sheep’s fleece
  - D) silkworm cocoon
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Cotton fibres grow in the boll of the cotton plant.
- **hint**: Plant source of cotton.

### Q02
- **stem**: Wool is obtained from —
- **options**:
  - A) cotton plants
  - B) sheep (and some other animals)
  - C) jute stems

- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Wool is an animal fibre from sheep and similar animals.
- **hint**: Animal fibre for woollens.

### Q03
- **stem**: Silk fibre is produced by —
- **options**:
  - A) cotton plants

### Q04

### Q09
