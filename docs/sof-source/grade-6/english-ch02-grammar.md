## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: Choose the correct verb: She ____ to school by bus.
- **options**:
  - A) goes
  - B) go
  - C) going
  - D) gone
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: She is singular third person, so the present form is goes.
- **hint**: Match singular subject to singular verb.

### Q02
- **stem**: Choose the correct verb: The boys ____ playing cricket.
- **options**:
  - A) is
  - B) am
  - C) be
  - D) are
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: Boys is plural → are.
- **hint**: Plural subject needs a plural verb.

### Q03
- **stem**: Identify the noun: The librarian opened a new shelf.
- **options**:
  - A) librarian
  - B) opened
  - C) new
  - D) a
- **answer**: A
- **explanation**: Librarian names a person — a noun.
- **hint**: Which word names a person, place or thing?

### Q04
- **stem**: Identify the adjective: Meera read an exciting story.
- **options**:
  - A) Meera
  - B) exciting
  - C) read
  - D) story
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Exciting describes the story — an adjective.
- **hint**: Find the describing word.

### Q05
- **stem**: Choose the correct article: ____ apple a day keeps the doctor away.
- **options**:
  - A) A
  - B) An
  - C) The only ever
  - D) No article possible
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Apple begins with a vowel sound → an.
- **hint**: Use an before vowel sounds.

### Q06

  - B) An

### Q16

### Q01

### Q02
- **stem**: Choose the correct verb: Either Rina or her brothers ____ coming.
- **options**:
  - A) is
  - B) are
  - C) am
  - D) be
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: With either…or, the verb agrees with the nearer subject: brothers → are.
- **hint**: Agree with the noun closest to the verb.
