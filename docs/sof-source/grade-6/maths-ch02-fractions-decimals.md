## Practice Set A

### Q01
- **stem**: What is 3/4 + 1/4?
- **options**:
  - A) 4/8
  - B) 1
  - C) 3/8
  - D) 2/4
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Like denominators: 3/4 + 1/4 = 4/4 = 1.
- **hint**: Same bottoms — add the tops.

### Q02
- **stem**: What is 5/6 − 1/6?
- **options**:
  - A) 4/6
  - B) 2/3
  - C) 6/6
  - D) 4/12
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 5/6 − 1/6 = 4/6 = 2/3 in simplest form.
- **hint**: Subtract tops, then simplify.

### Q03
- **stem**: Which fraction is equivalent to 2/3?
- **options**:
  - A) 3/2
  - B) 4/6
  - C) 2/6
  - D) 6/3
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Multiply top and bottom by 2: 2/3 = 4/6.
- **hint**: Multiply or divide top and bottom by the same number.

### Q04
- **stem**: What is 2/5 of 40?
- **options**:
  - A) 8
  - B) 16
  - C) 20
  - D) 10
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: 2/5 × 40 = 16.
- **hint**: Divide by 5, then multiply by 2.

### Q05

  - B) 7/100
  - C) 70/10

- **hint**: One digit after the decimal → denominator 10.

### Q06
