### Q01
- **id**: g6-maths-ch02-ach-x-01
- **stem**: Shamita's grandfather harvests 480 kg of onions. He sells 3/8 of the harvest at the mandi, gives 1/4 of the onions that remain to his brother, and packs the rest equally into five sacks. How many kilograms go into each sack?
- **options**:
  - A) 36 kg
  - B) 60 kg
  - C) 45 kg
  - D) 75 kg
- **answer**: C
- **explanation**: At the mandi he sells 3/8 of 480 kg = 180 kg, leaving 300 kg. His brother gets 1/4 of 300 kg = 75 kg, leaving 225 kg. Each of the 5 sacks holds 225 ÷ 5 = 45 kg. 36 kg is tempting because it takes 1/4 of the whole 480 kg (120 kg), leaving 180 kg and 36 kg per sack, but the brother's share is 1/4 of the onions left after the mandi sale.
- **hint**: The brother's fraction is taken from the onions still left after the mandi sale, not from the whole of Shamita's grandfather's harvest.
- **skill**: fractions_of_successive_remainders
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers

### Q03
- **id**: g6-maths-ch02-ach-x-03
- **stem**: On a car trip from Pune to Goa, Ekansh's family covered 3/8 of the whole distance before lunch and 2/5 of it after lunch. They still had 63 km to go. How many kilometres more did they travel after lunch than before lunch?
- **options**:
  - A) 280 km
  - B) 7 km
  - C) 112 km
  - D) 21 km
- **answer**: B
- **explanation**: Before and after lunch they covered 3/8 + 2/5 = 15/40 + 16/40 = 31/40 of the trip, so the last 63 km is the other 9/40. Then 1/40 of the trip is 63 ÷ 9 = 7 km and the whole trip is 40 × 7 = 280 km. After lunch they went 16/40 of 280 km = 112 km and before lunch 15/40 of 280 km = 105 km, so they went 7 km more after lunch. 280 km is tempting because it is the length of the whole trip, but that is only a step on the way; the question asks for the difference between the two parts.
- **hint**: Find what fraction of the trip the last 63 km stands for, then work out the full distance of Ekansh's trip.
- **skill**: whole_trip_from_unlike_fractions
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers

### Q05
- **id**: g6-maths-ch02-ach-x-05
- **stem**: A tailor has a 13.3 m roll of cloth. For Vrinda's order she first cuts off 2/7 of the roll for curtains. She then cuts as many 1.5 m kurta lengths as she can from the rest. How much cloth is left over at the end?
- **options**:
  - A) 1.3 m
  - B) 0.8 m
  - C) 1 m
  - D) 0.5 m
- **answer**: D
- **explanation**: 2/7 of 13.3 m is 13.3 ÷ 7 × 2 = 3.8 m, so 13.3 − 3.8 = 9.5 m is left. 1.5 m fits into 9.5 m 6 times (6 × 1.5 = 9 m), leaving 9.5 − 9 = 0.5 m. 1.3 m is tempting because it cuts kurta lengths from the whole 13.3 m roll (8 × 1.5 = 12 m, leaving 1.3 m), but the curtain cloth was cut off first.
- **hint**: Work out the curtain cloth first, then see how many kurta lengths fit into what remains of Vrinda's roll.
- **skill**: fraction_cut_then_decimal_lengths
- **difficulty**: hard
- **type**: mcq
- **pictorial**: no
- **olympiad_section**: achievers
