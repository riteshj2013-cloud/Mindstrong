## Full description

**Mindstrong** helps kids ages **6–15** build reasoning, maths, and confidence with calm, focused practice.

**Daily sessions**  
Warm-up patterns, reasoning puzzles, maths challenges, and a short reflect — designed for about 15–20 minutes.

**Olympiad-style prep**  
Interactive lessons plus practice sets across **Grades 1–10** in Maths, English, and Science. Kid-facing copy uses ₹ where money appears and avoids exam-body branding.

**Built for families**  
Local-first on the device. Parent gate for settings and progress. Clear plans when you’re ready to go further.

**Think hard. Stay brave.**
